# Multi-Agent Review and Synthesis

Step 18 creates deterministic, evidence-backed review panels over validated specialist contracts and governed routing records.

Each contribution preserves routed scope, evidence, confidence, constraints, authority, and unresolved questions. Synthesis outcomes distinguish consensus, supported disagreement, unresolved conflict, minority findings, blocked review, and owner-decision requirements.

The synthesis layer must not fabricate consensus, suppress dissent, silently reconcile incompatible claims, or exceed the authority of its sources. Outputs remain read-only, advisory, or proposal-only and cannot execute work, mutate canonical content, grant approval, promote or certify content, assign work, or schedule actions.

Required diagnostics include missing evidence, scope violations, unresolved conflicts, blocked reviews, owner decisions required, and any attempted fabricated consensus.
