Storage & caches

All built-in maps and artwork stay embedded. Optional pack originals are retained separately from disposable previews. Clearing previews does not uninstall packs or delete maps.

Cleanup protects installed/current and previous rollback records, which retain pinned packs and draft/history/checkpoint references. Unreferenced originals are marked first and deleted only on a later cleanup after at least 60 seconds. Keep your original pack files and downloaded native drafts. Browser-origin storage can still be removed by browser settings or eviction. Decoded native renderer/GPU allocations are not measured or released by this prototype.
