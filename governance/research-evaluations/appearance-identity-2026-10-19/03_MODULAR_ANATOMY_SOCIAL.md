# 03 — FORM-01 Modular Modification and SOC-01 Social Presentation Studies

## Why these are separate
Dance of Theseus turns interchangeable body parts and material/color combos into a fashion/scoring loop. Multiversal already has a source-bounded Species/Form appearance schema; borrowing the expression and loop **does not authorize** editing biological truth through FDE.

## FORM-01: owner-routed modular modification proposal
**Proposed use cases:** tail prosthesis, mechanical extra arm, spectral decorative wing, ritual mask, shapeshifting appearance, hybrid creature ornament. Treat each as a different authority category:
- `presentation_attachment`: cosmetic-only, attaches to a sanctioned presentation anchor, no functionality.
- `authorized_form_projection`: read-only projection of form already recognized by Species/Form; no acquisition.
- `functional_augmentation_proposal`: possible equipment/body change requiring owner-domain mechanics, prerequisites, resource cost and explicit acceptance.
- `temporary_ability_visual`: visible projection of an already-owned active effect, owned by ability/action state.
- `injury_or_replacement_reference`: medical/injury/recovery/game-mechanics ownership external to the fashion editor.

**Proposal object:** stable owner entity/version, requested attachment category, anchor IDs, material/color visual families, owned-component reference, size/clearance constraints, upstream eligibility evidence, provenance/source rights, created-by, proposal state and missing requirements. Do not commit as current anatomical truth.

**Proposed UI:** anatomical canvas as a **read-only topology map** with authorized anchor selection, visual preview, coverage/occlusion conflict list, proposal inspection and explicit request-to-owner button. Renderer uses Species/Form-approved geometry, not a baked human template. Component thumbnails generated once per reusable component/rig family, never all outfit combinations.

**Species constraints:** Vespin 4 arms/wings, Moravi four legs, ManyToms composite occupancy, Mythragara derived hybrid forms, Arborae seasons and Suula persistent changes must obey existing CAPP/CASI source profiles. CAPP-07 and PPIA-06 already encode special-case policies; they are dependencies, not features to replicate. No new canon or statistics proposed in this study.

**Counterexample:** a decorative jet-wing cape uses presentation anchors and may pass FGA fit; it cannot unlock flight. A real mechanical wing request routes to actual augmentation/equipment and ability rules. If there is no defined owner for functional augmentation, leave unresolved and request definition; do not assume generic cybernetics system.

**Proof requirements:** canonical topology signature remains unchanged after visual edits; rejected anchor has visible reason; unknown nonhumanoid never coerced to human; saved import lacks functional side effects; no hidden-form leakage; reduced-motion accessible nonvisual description.

## SOC-01: GM-authored social presentation challenge
**Why:** Dance of Theseus' color/texture combo scoring and themed ballroom rounds demonstrate a cohesive gameplay loop, but Multiversal has existing CHA/social initiative, Social AC, SHP, moods, skills and GM adjudication. Do not import a universal score formula or create a new social economy.

**Optional encounter component:** a `PresentationChallenge` linked to an existing social scene/encounter authority:
- theme and culture/faction reference with provenance;
- eligible audience/judges (GM authorized);
- visible or hidden criteria, with role-based redaction;
- tags expressed as *descriptive* style criteria (formal, ceremonial, regional, provocative, practical, clandestine);
- difficulty and outcomes provided **only** by accepted Multiversal social mechanics or GM decision;
- outfit/style references as narrative evidence; skills, preparation, character identity and table play still matter;
- role-based GM review, proposal/revision, result explanation, consequences and action log/continuity in the owning social runtime.

**Scoring prototype, explicitly noncanonical:** for a paper playtest, GM announces 1–3 presentation criteria. A character earns narrative tags (matches, clashes, uncertain) based on approved cultural knowledge, wardrobe visual evidence and player intent. The GM then resolves a normal existing social check or adjudication. No automatic bonus, dice, SHP damage or XP awards unless existing social-rules authority explicitly supports them. Opposed attire styles can both be viable: conforming to expectations or intentionally subverting them with a coherent plan.

**GM scenario:** Vertigon corporate reception with 'licensed heraldic badge', 'formal dress', and 'no active weapons' declared by GM. A Vespin player presents an FGA-valid multi-arm ensemble. GM sees source-backed tags and an explanation; players see only criteria known in the fiction. Passing is a social/mechanical outcome, not determined solely by asset rarity or price.

**Combat/economy boundaries:** no skills overwritten; crafting resources only owned by crafting/inventory systems; actual disguise effectiveness requires appropriate rules/GM approval; no automatic faction rep change; no new business market.

## User research/validation
- Observe 3 GM and 3 player walkthroughs, ideally including accessibility and one nonhumanoid species; this is a proposed future test, NOT completed.
- Measure how many owner decisions are needed per challenge, how long setup takes, whether explanations feel fair, and whether attire dominates roleplay. Establish quantitative thresholds with actual test evidence rather than inventing budgets.
- Test adversarial GM hidden criteria vs player export and AI suggestion to avoid leaking secret motivations.
