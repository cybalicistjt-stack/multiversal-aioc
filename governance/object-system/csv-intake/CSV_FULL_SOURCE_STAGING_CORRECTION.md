# Full CSV Staging Correction

The previous 8E-009L10 batch contract covered only seven mapped item datasets (4,821 rows) and was incorrectly described as the complete CSV batch. The governed source archive contains 20 datasets and 19,199 rows.

This correction expands noncanonical raw-row staging to every registered dataset while preserving domain routing boundaries. Datasets without canonical object registries are staged to governed domain queues rather than forced into item templates. No new canonical IDs, source certifications, identity merges, or promotions are authorized.

The seven item datasets remain mapping-enabled. The remaining thirteen datasets are staged for their registered destination systems: item reconciliation, mixed object-boundary review, hazards/traps, vehicles/mecha/spacecraft, spells, and abilities. Their domain-specific mapping contracts remain subsequent work.
