# 8E-009A Item Family Discovery

**Status:** SOURCE-DISCOVERY DRAFT  
**Source basis:** `Item pdfs.zip` - 13 PDFs, 218 pages  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner

## Governed approach

The Item Type and Template Registry must be derived from all item-related sources together. Templates are understandable item-family definitions assembled from shared capability modules. Physical item type, magitech discipline, power behavior, storage, sentience, bonding, modularity, and progression remain separate composable classifications.

## Normalized item-family hierarchy

### Equipment and wearables

- Weapon: melee weapon, ranged weapon, firearm, energy weapon, ammunition.
- Protective equipment: armor, shield, powered armor, EVA suit, energy shield.
- Wearable: accessory, clothing, ring, charm, talisman.
- Implant: cybernetic implant, prosthetic, neural interface, biological implant.

### Containers and storage

- Ordinary container.
- Fluid container.
- Ammunition container.
- Charge holder.
- Spell-storage item.
- Extra-dimensional storage.
- Data-storage device.
- Soul or energy reservoir.

### Consumables

- Potion, poison, drug or serum, food or ration.
- Alchemical flask, oil or coating, dust or powder.
- Scroll, spell orb, grenade, single-use magical item.
- Medical consumable and consumable crafting component.

### Tools and devices

- Mundane and skill-enhancing tools.
- Scanner or sensor, communication device, navigation device.
- Medical, crafting, security, and environmental-control devices.
- Trap, deployable device, portal or transportation device.
- Computer, drone controller, autonomous device.

### Computer systems

- Computer.
- Computer component.
- Software or program.
- Expansion module.
- AI system.

### Magic implements

- Wand, staff, rod, scroll, spell orb.
- Tome, spellbook, living spellbook.
- Charm, talisman, ring, relic, magical focus.

### Modular hosts and upgrades

- EVA suit module, computer expansion module.
- Weapon and armor modifications.
- Magitech and vehicle modules.
- Material upgrade, rune or script package, integrated component.

### Powered and magitech devices

The physical item template is primary. The magitech discipline is orthogonal:

- Arcanotech, Ethertech, Runetech, Psitech, Necrotech.
- Biomagitech, Elementech, Chronotech, Chaosynth, Divinetech.

Capability overlays include charge-powered, overcharge, environmental dependency, discipline failure, maintenance, required knowledge, and special state tracks.

### Living, sentient, and bonded objects

- Sentient item, living item, symbiote.
- Familiar-like item, autonomous construct item.
- Bonded relic, evolving item, parasitic item.
- Sentient companion-item, including Living Spellbooks.

### Materials and crafting resources

- Raw material, refined material, component.
- Consumable crafting resource and magical catalyst.
- Biological and synthetic materials.
- Fuel and ammunition component.

## Object/non-object boundary decisions

1. Physical item type and magitech discipline are separate classifications.
2. A modification is an item only when independently acquired, installed, removed, transferred, or tracked; intrinsic properties remain on the host.
3. Inventory-tracked materials are item objects. Crafting procedures and material-effect rules are supporting rules.
4. Surgery, repair, cloning, installation, access, and similar transactions are services, not items.
5. Fixed workshops, laboratories, and portals are facilities; portable machines may be devices.
6. Actions, effects, conditions, checks, and failure tables remain linked mechanics, not duplicate item types.
7. Software is a digital item-like asset when it has storage, compatibility, acquisition, installation, and executable functions.
8. Living Spellbooks require both item and companion-body capabilities.
9. Symbiotes require a dual-domain decision but must never be reduced to an ordinary equipment wrapper.
10. Clones and inactive clone bodies are artificial beings, not items. Cloning vats, transfer devices, modifications, facilities, and services remain separately modeled.
11. Grenades combine consumable, deployable, weapon, and explosive capabilities.
12. Scroll and spell-orb type depends on persistence, consumption, capture, recharge, and release behavior.
13. Energy Shield Harnesses use protective-equipment and protection-pool capabilities rather than ordinary shield assumptions.
14. Exoskeletons and powered armor separate from ordinary armor when power, movement, strength, modules, or powered failure are present.

4. Ordinary shields versus energy shields and absorbable protection pools.
5. Exoskeleton classification: armor, powered frame, wearable vehicle, or multimode host.
6. Cybernetic implant versus biological implant, prosthetic, symbiote, and wearable augmentation.
7. Software, stored data, AI personalities, and executable spell formula hierarchy.
8. Source-unit normalization without rewriting credits, gp, weight, range, duration, or rating evidence.
9. Fire modes as intrinsic properties, modifications, ammunition behaviors, or granted actions.
10. Cross-host compatibility semantics for EVA, computer, weapon, armor, vehicle, and magitech modules.
11. Living or autonomous materials.
12. Portable portal device versus fixed transportation facility.
13. Trap object versus deployed scene hazard projection.
14. Composition of multiple magitech disciplines and state tracks.
15. Death, destruction, repair, resurrection, and replacement for living or sentient items.

## Source coverage

All 13 PDFs were reviewed. Coverage includes equipment, weapons, armor, EVA suits, cybernetics, containers, consumables, tools, computers, programs, magic implements, charge holders, modular upgrades, magitech, living objects, symbiotes, and materials. The bundle does not supply one normalized authoritative table for every named item, universal durability/condition rules, final economy normalization, or resolved cross-domain policy for symbiotes. Those gaps remain explicit rather than being filled by inference.
