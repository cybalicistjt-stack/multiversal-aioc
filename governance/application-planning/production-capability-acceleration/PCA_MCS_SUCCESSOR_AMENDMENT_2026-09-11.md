## Decision

The previously planned direct handoff `PCA-16 → SMB-08` is superseded by:

`PCA-16 → MCS-01..21 → SMB-08`

PCA's purpose, sixteen tranches, activation after CNI-13, owner boundaries and completion gates are unchanged. MCS is inserted as the map-specific authoring and cartography consumer of PCA's reusable generator, texture/material, recipe and style-production primitives.

PCA does not become the cartography owner. MCS does not reopen or duplicate PCA. `PCA_PROGRAM_BACKLOG.json` is updated so its strict successor is `MCS-01`.

This amendment does not select PCA or MCS, create either family preflight, change `CURRENT_WORK_POINTER.json`, or grant implementation authority. ARI-15 remains the selected current work item under its existing sealed family preflight.
