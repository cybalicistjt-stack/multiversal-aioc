## Purpose

MSWI converts the combined Planet Crafter / FOURSAVENT / GRAFT / DREADMOOR / NECROPOLIS / Nivalis Nights / Elderfeast / Sirens / Notmads / Punk Runner / Divinity / Billie Bust Up / JOIN US / Bloody Hell Hotel study into one Multiversal-native integration family.

The study does **not** justify duplicating capabilities that already exist. DPL already owns profession activities, enterprise operations, arts/performance, cybernetics/symbiotes/biotech and life-simulation foundations. MNCS already owns creature ecology, populations and ecology change. GPR owns reusable gameplay patterns. MERA owns engineering/refit. MBES owns built-environment, infrastructure, logistics and reactive-development hooks. MSWI exists to make those and the other governed domains interact visibly, persistently and coherently during play.

The central product requirement is:

> Major player effort can create attributable, inspectable and persistent consequences across the world, and the application can explain which governed systems responded and why.

## Roadmap role

MSWI is a **future interstitial family** between `MBES-24` and `SMB-08`.

It must not change the current OPS3 product selector. While this planning package is created, `operations/CURRENT.json` remains authoritative and MSWI has no branch or implementation authority.

The authoritative cross-program placement is recorded in `governance/application-planning/ROADMAP_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.json`; legacy successor fields are compatibility/provenance only.

## OPS3 execution design

Every future MSWI work item is deliberately smaller than the 24-minute execution target so closeout is part of the tranche rather than an extra cycle.

- total execution target: **24 minutes**;
- minimum closeout reserve: **8 minutes**;
- ordinary active implementation target: **14–16 minutes**;
- preload only the selected tranche's declared dependency closure;
- block unrelated history, roadmap archaeology and other-family source loading;
- split before governed start if the reserve is not credible;
- one owner `Continue` carries a governed-started tranche through focused implementation, required validation, durable evidence, reconciliation and successor marking unless an OPS3 owner-only boundary or genuine external blocker is reached;
- deterministic failures are never rerun unchanged.

This planning rule cannot grant implementation authority. A future work item becomes executable only when OPS3 `CURRENT.json` selects it and grants the appropriate authority.

## Domain boundaries

MSWI is an integration/orchestration family. It consumes existing owner-domain operations and emits attributable proposals/events/results through them.

It does not become a second ledger for World, Environment, Character, NPC, Creature, Species/Form, Vehicle/Mount, Base, Project/Time, Economy, Profession, Organization/Faction, Religion/Culture, Relationship/Reputation, Inventory/Asset, Crafting, Action/Event, Effect, Condition, Resource or Hazard truth.

Unknown source facts remain unresolved. A systemic interaction is not permission to invent anatomy, ecology, psychological effects, engineering behavior, social doctrine, physical laws or setting canon.

## Tranche design

### MSWI-01 — Systemic Worldplay Authority, Capability Crosswalk & Integration Contract

**Goal:** establish the exact owner-domain seams and prove the family adds integration instead of reopening completed/planned programs.

**Outputs:**
- owner-domain crosswalk for every MSWI capability;
- operation/event vocabulary and no-duplicate-ledger contract;
- systemic consequence envelope and proposal/commit boundary;
- clean-room benchmark provenance map;
- tranche-local dependency manifests used by all successors.

**Acceptance:** every capability has one authoritative owner path; no MSWI-owned field duplicates canonical owner state; no implementation path depends on the benchmark games.

### MSWI-02 — Persistent World-Transformation State, Dimensions, Thresholds & Attribution

**Goal:** support generic player-driven environmental/world transformation without hard-coding terraforming.

**Outputs:**
- transformation dimension/profile contract;
- examples including habitability, hydrology, fertility/biomass, pollution, radiation, corruption, magical saturation, industrialization, restoration and setting-defined dimensions;
- threshold/milestone model;
- source/actor/time/scope/provenance attribution;
- inspectable before/current/projected state.

**Acceptance:** the same mechanism can represent barren→habitable, polluted→restored, mundane→magically saturated, frozen→thawed and other owner-defined transitions without changing the core schema.

### MSWI-03 — Transformation Propagation, Reversibility, Recovery & Cross-System Consequence Routing

**Goal:** make transformation matter to governed systems rather than remain a dashboard meter.

**Outputs:**
- typed consequence routes into resources, hazards, travel, weather, encounters, settlement, economy, agriculture, crafting and creature habitat;
- reversible, irreversible and conditionally reversible transitions;
- failed/partial transformation recovery;
- conflict and cycle handling;
- provenance-preserving rollback/compensation rules.

**Acceptance:** transformation fan-out is deterministic/traceable and never allows a downstream system to mutate an upstream owner silently.

### MSWI-04 — Ecology Succession, Carrying Capacity, Habitat Suitability & Population-Band Runtime

**Goal:** convert accepted MNCS ecology/population definitions into lightweight campaign-time ecological state.

**Outputs:**
- habitat suitability evaluation;
- configurable population bands rather than mandatory per-individual simulation;
- carrying-capacity and recovery profiles where authored;
- succession stages and environment dependencies;
- migration pressure inputs/outputs.

**Acceptance:** ecology changes can be explained from accepted habitat/environment/population inputs and remain bounded enough for tabletop and application use.

### MSWI-05 — Food Web, Migration, Invasion, Extinction, Recovery & Harvest-Pressure Consequences

**Goal:** model meaningful ecological reactions to player/campaign activity.

**Outputs:**
- typed producer/prey/predator/decomposer or setting-equivalent dependency links;
- migration/territory shifts;
- introduced/invasive species hooks;
- overharvest/overhunting and resource-pressure hooks;
- extinction/local-extirpation/recovery/restocking/conservation states.

**Acceptance:** ecological consequences are event-driven, attributable and profile-defined; absent biological facts are never fabricated.

### MSWI-06 — Composite Entity Role Binding: Creature, Mount, Vehicle, Base, NPC & World Host

**Goal:** let one stable entity participate in multiple governed systems without duplicate identities.

**Outputs:**
- role-binding contract and compatibility rules;
- shared identity/state reference pattern;
- ownership/control/custody separation across roles;
- examples for living mobile bases, sentient ships, creature-mounted structures and living locations;
- role removal/transition behavior.

**Acceptance:** a composite entity remains one canonical identity while each owner domain retains authority over its own projection and operations.

### MSWI-07 — Creature Anatomy, Component Damage, Harvest Quality & Biological Provenance

**Goal:** connect combat/capture/harvest to source-authored creature anatomy and downstream uses.

**Outputs:**
- optional anatomical component graph;
- component condition/damage/availability projection;
- harvest method and quality consequences;
- biological-material provenance and conservation-safe outputs;
- crafting/cooking/alchemy/medicine/research/economy integration seams.

**Acceptance:** no generic monster automatically gains invented organs/yields; component outputs are traceable to accepted creature definitions and exact harvest events.

### MSWI-08 — Embodied Modification: Grafts, Implants, Symbiotes, Donor Lineage & Identity Consequences

**Goal:** deepen existing DPL-13 and form/transformation foundations with donor-aware body modification.

**Outputs:**
- body-location/component binding;
- donor/source lineage;
- compatibility/rejection/maintenance hooks where governed;
- grants/removals/retained-state/reversal behavior;
- optional memory, impulse, identity, social or psychological consequences only through explicit rules/content.

**Acceptance:** the system can model prosthetics, cybernetics, grafts, symbiotes, mutations and magical replacements without collapsing base identity or inventing consequences.

### MSWI-09 — Doctrine, Ideology, Belief Choice, Obligation & Organization Consequence Runtime

**Goal:** allow live organizations/movements to evolve doctrine without rewriting reusable Religion/Culture/Organization definitions.

**Outputs:**
- campaign-scoped doctrine state;
- values/taboos/practices/duties/acceptable-method choices;
- benefit + obligation/consequence pairings;
- membership/reputation/office/relationship integration;
- schism/reform/orthodoxy/divergence hooks.

**Acceptance:** reusable definitions remain unchanged; live doctrine changes are attributable campaign state with explicit consequences.

### MSWI-10 — Profession, Enterprise, Daily-Life & Downtime Loop Composition Adapters

**Goal:** make completed DPL capabilities feel like connected life rather than isolated screens.

**Outputs:**
- day/week/month planning envelope over Project/Time;
- adapters for work, training, relationships, business, research, crafting, rest, travel and obligations;
- opportunity-cost and scheduling conflict presentation;
- enterprise/customer/service hooks that reuse DPL-08 and Economy rather than duplicate them;
- base/venue/workforce integration.

**Acceptance:** all committed time/resource/business effects route through existing owners; MSWI owns orchestration only.

### MSWI-11 — Pursuit, Race, Chase, Convoy & Interception Scene Runtime

**Goal:** provide one reusable pursuit model across movement modes.

**Outputs:**
- shared pursuit topology/state;
- foot, mount, ground, water, air and space adapters;
- race, escape, catch, escort/convoy and interception objectives;
- route/environment/vehicle/ability/hazard integration;
- partial-success and changing-objective handling.

**Acceptance:** the runtime is mode-neutral and uses GPR/Vehicle/World/Scene owner operations rather than bespoke per-vehicle logic.

### MSWI-12 — Performance, Rhythm, Diegetic Cue, Telegraphing & Accessible Equivalent Contract

**Goal:** allow music, lyrics, chants, dance, ritual, speech and other performances to carry timing/mechanical information.

**Outputs:**
- cue/beat/phase/response model;
- event telegraphing contract;
- adaptive performance state hooks;
- DPL-10/GPR/MSAS integration;
- mandatory visual/text/haptic or otherwise appropriate non-audio equivalent for required information.

**Acceptance:** a performance can govern timing without making audio perception or copyrighted benchmark music a gameplay requirement.

### MSWI-13 — Grand and World Projects, Contribution Aggregation, Faction Opposition & Visible Milestones

**Goal:** support long-horizon collaborative efforts whose consequences span systems.

**Outputs:**
- World/Grand Project profile over APW/D26 Project authority;
- staged prerequisites and contribution aggregation;
- party/faction/settlement/organization support or resistance;
- material, labor, research, influence and event contribution types;
- milestone-triggered world consequences.

**Acceptance:** projects such as ecological restoration, rebuilding, colonization, large infrastructure, curse-breaking or world-scale transformation use one generic framework and never bypass owner-domain commits.

### MSWI-14 — Procedural Site and Scenario Remix, Seeded Assembly, Ephemeral State & GM Promotion

**Goal:** combine authored pieces into replayable exploration sites/scenarios without uncontrolled canon generation.

**Outputs:**
- deterministic seed/template/component assembly contract where determinism is declared;
- module slots for topology, environment, inhabitants, hazards, resources, clues, objectives, rewards and exits;
- ephemeral/proposed/persistent state distinction;
- GM review and promotion workflow;
- provenance and version compatibility.

**Acceptance:** generated sites can be discarded, replayed or promoted without silently creating campaign truth.

### MSWI-15 — Environment-State Map and Scene Variants & Persistent Visible Campaign Change

**Goal:** make campaign history visible when revisiting the same canonical place.

**Outputs:**
- stable-location multi-state projection contract;
- map/scene variant selection from accepted world/environment state;
- before/after/history comparison;
- fog/visibility-safe presentation;
- MCS/MAS/MCCS/ARI presentation bindings.

**Acceptance:** one location identity can display rebuilt, flooded, corrupted, restored, industrialized or other accepted states without cloning the location.

### MSWI-16 — Transformation Campaign Templates & Setting-Specific System Packs

**Goal:** package recurring systemic campaigns without hard-coding one genre.

**Outputs:**
- templates for restoration, colonization, corruption/purification, rebuilding, terraforming/habitability, industrialization, ecological recovery and setting-defined equivalents;
- required owner capability declarations;
- configurable dimensions/thresholds/milestones;
- scenario/Adventure/Project hooks;
- import/export/version rules.

**Acceptance:** templates configure existing MSWI primitives and owners; they do not fork the runtime or create genre-specific authority.

### MSWI-17 — Interaction-Density Analyzer, Effect Fan-Out, Loop Detection & Systemic Coverage Diagnostics

**Goal:** make the study's central design principle testable.

**Outputs:**
- declared interaction graph for an action/event/capability;
- downstream-response/fan-out inspection;
- dead-link, missing-route, circular-mutation and contradictory-response detection;
- coverage categories for mechanical, environmental, social, economic, narrative and presentation consequences;
- score/explanation that rewards justified connectivity but never fabricates mechanics to increase a number.

**Acceptance:** designers can answer “what else responds when this happens?” with exact owner-domain routes and gaps.

### MSWI-18 — Golden Systemic Worldplay Proof & SMB-08 Handoff

**Goal:** prove the family works as one integrated systemic layer and hand off a stable capability set to system maturation.

**Required golden scenarios:**
1. a party restores or transforms an environment, changing resources/hazards/habitat and later sees the same location visibly changed;
2. harvest pressure causes an attributable ecology response and recovery path;
3. one composite living asset functions across creature/mount/vehicle/base roles without duplicate identity;
4. creature-component harvesting feeds a governed downstream craft or research result;
5. an embodied modification preserves donor lineage and reversible/retained state;
6. a doctrine choice creates both benefit and obligation with campaign-scoped social consequences;
7. a profession/enterprise/downtime cycle competes for Project/Time and resources without a second ledger;
8. a cross-mode pursuit resolves through shared patterns;
9. a diegetic performance cue has functionally equivalent accessible presentation;
10. a grand/world project produces staged visible consequences;
11. a seeded procedural site remains ephemeral until accepted and promoted;
12. interaction-density diagnostics expose both valid fan-out and at least one intentionally missing/blocked route.

**Acceptance:** exact-head validation is green; deterministic receipts/provenance are present where required; no owner boundary is bypassed; no paid/cloud provider is required; the family closes `completed_verified`; `SMB-08` becomes the planned successor only after OPS3 reconciles current state.
