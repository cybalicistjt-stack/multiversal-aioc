# MXS-01 — Industry Capability Baseline Summary

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** STRATEGY SYNTHESIS  
**Prepared:** 2026-08-13

## Strategic conclusion

Current tabletop software is fragmented by specialty. Mature VTTs lead in spatial play, automation and extensibility; digital companions lead in guided Characters and integrated rules; world/campaign tools lead in knowledge organization, secrets, collaboration and offline/export; newer AI-assisted tools add campaign/rules retrieval and drafting.

Multiversal should therefore define parity by **user value**, not by cloning competitor screens.

## Product-floor categories

Multiversal must eventually provide or deliberately bridge:

1. searchable rules/content and provenance;
2. guided Character creation and interactive Character use;
3. reusable content libraries and Campaign organization;
4. world knowledge, timelines, relationships and secrets;
5. real-time collaboration and safe recovery;
6. data export/ownership and versioned portability;
7. rules automation with explainable results;
8. explicit GM adjudication and approval;
9. collaborative/private notes;
10. theater-of-the-mind Scene presentation;
11. abstract and tactical spatial play through staged maturity;
12. desktop/mobile/in-person companion workflows;
13. creator extensibility and publisher content;
14. optional AI rules/campaign/creation assistance;
15. future community/discovery/marketplace capabilities only under separate trust/governance controls.

## Staging rule

Each capability receives one disposition:
- `native_now_or_stage_a`;
- `bridge_now_native_later`;
- `post_alpha_expansion`;
- `intentionally_different`.

Full dynamic-VTT parity is not an A2 prerequisite. Multiversal can deliver substantial value first as the authoritative rules/content/Character/Campaign/live-state/creation layer while spatial maturity advances separately.

## Competitive acceptance rule

Every major Stage A product package asks:

### Parity Gate
Does it deliver the underlying user value a strong current tabletop product has taught users to expect?

### Integration Gate
Does it operate through shared Multiversal identity, permissions, provenance, recovery, accessibility and history rather than a disconnected subsystem?

### Multiversal Gate
What becomes possible because this capability is connected to the rest of the platform?

## Representative current product classes reviewed

The research tranche reviewed official/current materials for representative products including Foundry VTT, Fantasy Grounds, Roll20, Owlbear Rodeo, Alchemy, TaleSpire, Demiplane, World Anvil, LegendKeeper and Quest Portal.

Observed market patterns include:
- deep rules/VTT automation and extension ecosystems;
- browser/mobile lightweight spatial play;
- cinematic theater-of-the-mind presentation;
- 3D spatial tabletop experiences;
- guided digital Character/rules companions;
- interconnected world/campaign knowledge environments;
- private/offline/export-oriented creator tools;
- marketplace/publisher ecosystems;
- AI-assisted rules/campaign context and drafting.

Vendor documentation is evidence that a capability is offered, not independent proof of quality or user preference.

## Strategic non-goals

MXS-01 does not:
- declare one competitor the universal benchmark;
- require copying proprietary interaction designs;
- authorize a marketplace, full VTT, provider, pricing model or public community;
- turn every specialist feature into an Internal Alpha blocker;
- claim Multiversal currently provides capabilities that remain planned.

## MXS advantage

Competitor capabilities define the floor. Multiversal's advantage should come from the common model underneath them: a Character, Item, NPC, Faction, clue, Scene, world object or rule retains stable identity and governed history as it moves through search, creation, live play, social/investigation, economy, crafting, spatial projection, export and optional AI.
