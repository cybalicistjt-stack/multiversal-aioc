# CCP — Companion & Creature Partnership

**Program ID:** CCP  
**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED — PLANNED INTERSTITIAL  
**Activation:** after MSS-12  
**Successor:** DPL-01  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner  
**Planned:** 2026-08-20

## Purpose

CCP completes the living-creature partnership layer that is distinct from ICF husbandry/harvest and ordinary Character relationships. It governs pets, familiars, mounts, trained/work/service animals, intelligent creature companions, breeding/lineage, creature care, habitats and broader authored creature lifecycles without turning creatures into inventory objects or creating a second creature, relationship, Character, combat, economy or World ledger.

ICF remains ingredient/harvest/husbandry-production authority. MSS owns supernatural casting/pact effects. CCP owns the creature-partnership workflow and exposes governed seams to both.

## Tranches

1. **CCP-01 — Source Inventory, Creature Catalog Crosswalk & Authority Map**  
   Reconcile retained animal/familiar/mount/training/breeding/creature sources with the governed creature catalog, ICF, MIB-09, MIB-14, Combat, World, Economy, APW and MSS; identify explicit gaps and creature types requiring special treatment.

2. **CCP-02 — Companion Identity, Bond, Intelligence, Agency & Role Model**  
   Define companion profiles, sapience/intelligence, temperament, bond/trust, handler relationships, ownership/control versus voluntary association, permitted roles, needs and provenance without reducing an intelligent being to an Asset.

3. **CCP-03 — Taming, Recruitment, Rescue & Bond Formation**  
   Implement authored taming/recruitment/befriending/rescue/adoption/familiar-bond workflows with eligibility, risk, time, checks/choices, consent/agency where applicable, failure and relationship consequences.

4. **CCP-04 — Training, Commands, Tasks, Tricks & Behavior**  
   Govern learnable commands/tasks, trainer skill, repetition/project time, behavior profiles, reliability, distraction/fear, task prerequisites and bounded automation while preserving GM/creature agency.

5. **CCP-05 — Care, Needs, Health, Recovery, Aging & Welfare**  
   Model authored feeding, shelter, exercise, grooming, injury/care, illness exposure, recovery, aging and welfare requirements through existing condition/ingredient/project systems rather than a parallel survival ledger.

6. **CCP-06 — Mounts, Pack, Service, Work & Travel Integration**  
   Complete riding/mount capacity, pack animals, service/work creatures, hauling, travel pace/endurance, terrain suitability, gear, handlers and vehicle/base/travel seams.

7. **CCP-07 — Combat Companions, Familiars & Supernatural Bond Seam**  
   Connect trained combat companions and familiars to initiative/actions, commands, reactions, protection, defeat/recovery and MSS magical-conduit/pact effects without granting companion controllers hidden or automatic authority.

8. **CCP-08 — Breeding, Reproduction, Lineage & Inheritance**  
   Implement authored mating/reproduction, gestation/incubation, offspring, lineage, heritable traits, controlled breeding, pedigree and mutation/variant hooks without inventing unsupported genetics for fantasy/exotic species.

9. **CCP-09 — Habitats, Herds, Stables, Kennels & Facility Operations**  
   Integrate groups/herds, housing, enclosures, stables/kennels/aviaries/aquaria, feed/storage, staff, capacity, upkeep, breeding/training facilities, APW Projects, CEL and Economy.

10. **CCP-10 — Creature Ecology, Social Behavior, Lifecycle & World Integration**  
    Add governed ecology/lifecycle/social-group/migration/territory/lair/reproduction hooks for authored creatures so encounters, settlements and Worlds can reflect creature populations without simulating unsupported ecology by guesswork.

11. **CCP-11 — Content Packs, Search, Workbench & Golden Partnership Proof**  
    Publish companion/training/mount/breeding/habitat profiles, catalog coverage reports and inspectors; prove taming/training/travel/combat/care/breeding/facility/recovery paths across ordinary and supernatural companions.

## Invariants

- ICF retains biological harvest and production-husbandry truth.
- Creature definitions and Character/relationship systems remain canonical; CCP adds partnership workflows, not duplicate identity ledgers.
- Intelligent/sapient beings are never silently treated as property merely because the same system supports ordinary animals.
- Breeding/genetics rules are authored/profile-driven and do not fabricate species biology.
- Combat commands still pass through ordinary Action/permission/GM rules.
- MSS owns magical effects of familiar/pact bonds; CCP owns partnership state and care/training workflows.
