## 4. Future owner-gated decisions

### IA-OD-001 — Approve final internal-alpha feature scope

**Trigger:** Completion of the first full feature-packet cycle and dependency review.  
**Required evidence:** registry, packets, exclusions, dependencies, content corpus, cost, and schedule impact.

### IA-OD-002 — Approve experimental feature cohort

**Trigger:** AI, advanced maps, advanced vehicle operations, or broad offline synchronization becomes ready for testing.  
**Required evidence:** feature flag, fallback, cost, privacy, security, tester group, and disablement plan.

### IA-OD-003 — Approve production-provider or paid-service activation

**Trigger:** An implementation or alpha environment requires a provider account, paid plan, or recurring cost.  
**Required evidence:** recommendation, alternatives, measured trigger, monthly cost, ceiling, data classes, fallback, and provider exit.

### IA-OD-004 — Approve exact internal-alpha candidate and tester group

**Trigger:** Entry-critical features and release gates pass.  
**Required evidence:** exact commit and build, environment, content versions, tester list, security/privacy/accessibility/recovery evidence, known risks, rollback, and shutdown plan.

### IA-OD-005 — Accept residual blocking risk

**Trigger:** A material defect or limitation is proposed for owner-held acceptance.  
**Required evidence:** impact, affected roles and data, workaround, duration, mitigation, exit condition, and recommendation.

### IA-OD-006 — Promote or remove an alpha-required feature

**Trigger:** Dependency, cost, usability, or schedule evidence suggests changing a feature classification.  
**Required evidence:** effect on user journeys, acceptance criteria, downstream features, and internal-alpha product promise.

## 5. Ordinary decisions delegated to the design program

The design team may choose and record ordinary reversible options such as:

- document structure;
- feature packet order within approved dependency constraints;
- field names that do not alter canon;
- presentation variants;
- fixture identifiers;
- test organization;
- shared-component boundaries;
- error-state wording;
- accessibility patterns based on approved design standards.

These choices must still be reviewed and remain reversible.

## 6. Owner decision request format

Every future request should state:

- exact bounded question;
- recommendation;
- alternatives;
- user and project impact;
- cost;
- risk;
- reversibility;
- controlling sources;
- affected features and journeys;
- required response;
- next action after approval.

Silence, repository access, prior behavior, or a passing test does not imply approval.
