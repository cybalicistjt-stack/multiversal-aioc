# Application Implementation Roadmap — MNCS PDCP Reduction Amendment

**Date:** 2026-09-16  
**Authority:** owner-approved PDCP planning reconciliation  
**Implementation authority:** none

## Decision

Reduce the planned **MNCS — Multiversal NPC & Creature Studio** family from its historical 24-tranche baseline to 13 implementation/proof tranches after completed PDCP packet closure and explicit intra-family/cross-family overlap review.

Effective MNCS order:

`MNCS-01 → MNCS-04 → MNCS-05 → MNCS-06 → MNCS-08 → MNCS-10 → MNCS-12 → MNCS-14 → MNCS-15 → MNCS-18 → MNCS-20 → MNCS-22 → MNCS-24`

Historical tranche IDs and obligations remain recorded in `PDCP_MNCS_REDUCTION_RECEIPT.json`.

## Principal folds

- `01+02+03 → MNCS-01` — studio core plus NPC/creature recipe/context/resolution binding over shared PCA/PPIA/Packet-06 infrastructure;
- `06+07 → MNCS-06` — persona plus knowledge/belief/actor-context authoring;
- historical `09` — generic profession/household/economy/schedule/project/life truth absorbed to DPL/Profession, Economy/Organization and Project/Time, with NPC bindings retained in `06/15/20/24`;
- `10+11 → MNCS-10` — mechanics-on-demand and loadout/capability composition;
- `12+13 → MNCS-12` — creature ecology plus behavior/social structure/tactics authoring;
- `15+16+17 → MNCS-15` — social groups, populations and creature cohorts over one Packet-06 multi-resolution kernel;
- `18+19 → MNCS-18` — secrets/reveal and GM-facing conversation/roleplay preparation;
- `20+21 → MNCS-20` — continuity/change over time plus promotion/demotion/retirement/resolution management;
- historical `23` — generic provenance/version/review/import-export and creator/AI infrastructure absorbed to ARI/PCA/Packet 07, with MNCS-specific presets/batch/handoff/proof residuals in `01/05/15/22/24`.

## Preserved standalone seams

`MNCS-04`, `05`, `08`, `14`, `22` and `24` remain distinct because identity/origin, instant-improv UX, reputation/party-association integration, form/template compatibility, runtime handoff, and golden proof remain separate bounded implementation/proof responsibilities.

MNCS does not duplicate:

- PCA recipe/generation infrastructure;
- PPIA-02 or Character/NPC/Creature identity/placement/live-instance authority;
- MCCS presentation authoring;
- MIB-09 reputation mutation;
- DPL/Profession/Economy/Project life-simulation truth;
- Packet-01 social-decision runtime semantics;
- Packet-03 autonomous-process orchestration;
- Packet-06 multi-resolution accounting semantics;
- Packet-07 generic creator/review/debug semantics;
- reduced MRCS reusable definition authoring;
- reduced GPR/canonical gameplay execution;
- ARI/PCA generic provenance/review/import-export infrastructure.

## DAG preservation

No dependency-graph mutation is required. `MNCS-01` remains the family start/rotation milestone and `MNCS-24` remains the final/golden milestone consumed by downstream planning. Sparse IDs are deliberate.

## Count impact
