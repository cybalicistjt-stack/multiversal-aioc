## Purpose

KFR turns the recovered mine-note concept of broad-to-specific familiarity into a reusable governed relation. Familiarity with a family, class, technology, profession, manufacturer, model or related system may support partial transfer to narrower or adjacent subjects without pretending all knowledge is identical or binary.

KFR does not replace Character knowledge, Skills, Professions, Research, Vehicles, Equipment, Organizations or permissions. It supplies explicit familiarity relations, confidence/transfer rules and projections that those owners may consume.

Recovered basis: RDC-02 / RD-03. The owner has explicitly directed that suitable recovered mine-note families be added to the roadmap.

## Selection invariants

- Familiarity is not permission, ownership, proficiency, certification or action authority by itself.
- Unknown remains unknown; the system does not invent missing knowledge.
- Transfer must be source/profile-driven and explainable rather than one universal percentage.
- Domain owners retain canonical Character, Skill, Profession, Vehicle, Equipment, Research and Organization state.
- Hidden/private knowledge is filtered before projection, search, AI context or explanation.
- KFR-01 is selection-only until its own governed start; no persistence or migration `0022` is reserved at selection.
