## MCB-01 category and positioning thesis — explicitly hypothetical

**Candidate category:** an original Multiversal multi-genre RPG plus **GM–Player–Creator companion** for character, governed Campaign, rules/lore and session flow. The narrowest proposed beachhead is a GM trying to get one group from character setup through governed decisions with fewer disconnected steps. This is a **hypothesis** requiring actual user research and installed-app task tests, not an established demand claim.

**Distinguish the category from a full virtual tabletop.** Online VTT products emphasize maps, token interaction and live session presentation; some also provide character sheets, campaign preparation, mobile support, multi-system sheets, AI and collaboration. Multiversal cannot honestly claim these capabilities are absent from substitutes. Candidate value should be tested on first-session **workflow continuity and role/permission clarity**, with the risk that an RPG-specific companion may require additional tools for non-Multiversal games or full-map play.

**Potential alternatives include using paper, a notes app, a VTT, an RPG-specific character tool, a world wiki, or a combination.** The choice among these is not yet measured. No win-rate or total addressable market is established.

## MCB-02 personas / jobs to be done (all unvalidated)

- **GM:** wants to set up the first Session and make permissioned adjudications; might struggle with switching tools and invitation/access status. Benchmark on a first complete authorized Campaign action rather than "features checked".
- **Player:** wants a clear Character, valid invitation, current progression, and continuity when moving between phone and computer. Benchmark time to first safe action, account recovery and accidental Campaign access, using actual independent identities only under later PCV authority.
- **Creator:** wants to connect custom settings, species, items and ability trees without confusing owned raw content with Campaign-restricted derivatives. Benchmark whether content provenance and sharing consequences are understandable.
- **Hybrid organizer:** wants in-person focus plus bounded digital aids for mixed attendance and intermittent connectivity. Benchmark the gap between planned offline rights and actual local-host Session authority; do not promise full offline functionality.

These are four **design hypotheses**, not four validated customer archetypes. The registry carries the explicit alternatives, proposed pain hypotheses and validation protocol for each. No persona has customers sampled, conversion metrics or measured willingness to pay.

## MCB-03 customer research instrument, not active recruitment

Prepare a future consented, independently approved interview protocol: begin with last-actually-used workflow, collect behavioral examples and timestamps, avoid leading the participant toward Multiversal, then use a short scenario task with **the correct actual app build**. Separate role, prior RPG experience, group setup, homebrew use, device/connection, alternatives, session-critical failures and the cost of switching. Ask permission before any recording or personal data collection; do not ingest private Campaign notes or student/minor data into the research file.

The registry records **eight neutral interview prompts, all response counts zero**. Future analysis should preserve respondent-level anonymization, sample selection, contradictions and negative reactions, and distinguish spontaneous from prompted answers. No interviews are authorized by this source-only deliverable.

## MCB-05/06/07 brand framework and messaging

**Voice:** warm, knowledgeable, imaginative, precise about permissions and capability limits, not salesy or obsequious. Make the subject the table's story and user tasks rather than claiming that novelty or AI alone makes a better RPG experience. Avoid "the only", "all content included", "fully secure", "works everywhere", "zero setup" and guaranteed accessibility.

**Identity-system handoff:** logo versions, typography, contrast, mobile-safe hierarchy, game-genre adaptability, reduced motion, license-cleared art/creatures, icon consistency and creator co-branding must be specified and reviewed by the actual AXI/brand owners. This tranche **does not** replace their design system or certify media rights. No final visual identity is selected here.

**Draft audience message pattern:** `role → painful task hypothesis → approved capability → bounded benefit → independent proof → consent-safe CTA`. If the proof is only source/design, mark the line `INTERNAL_DRAFT_NOT_PUBLIC`. The registry has eight such lines for GM, Player, Creator and hybrid sessions and their current hold state.
