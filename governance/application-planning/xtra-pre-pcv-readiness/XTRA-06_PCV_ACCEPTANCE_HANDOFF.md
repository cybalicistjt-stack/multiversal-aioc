## Diagnostic and evidence receipt contract

For each eventual test result record `pcv_item`, `case_id`, exact immutable app head, role, supported platform, device binding, timestamp, actual result, server/owner evidence and artifact digest. A real two-human test needs independent humans/identities/devices and demonstrable rights/hidden-information paths, not two mock users in the same process.

Classify failures by causal layer: incorrect source contract, authorization/entitlement, transport/reconnect, persistence/event duplication, UI/accessibility/presentation, asset-rights provenance, provider/runner or human test fixture. A canceled/queued runner is infrastructure evidence, not a passing or failing feature. Preserve the first failure and competing causes; avoid repeatedly polling same-head deterministic errors.

The matrix marks every source acceptance item `BLOCKED_BY_PREDECESSOR_NOT_RUN` and every adversarial case `NOT_RUN`. Do not fill `actual_result=PASS` from a schema test or from this report.
