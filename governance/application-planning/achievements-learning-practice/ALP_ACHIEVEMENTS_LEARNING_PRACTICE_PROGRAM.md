## Purpose

ALP consolidates the recovered mine-note achievement, onboarding/mastery and diegetic-practice concepts into one governed learning/recognition family. It preserves the later owner-confirmed optional Synced Achievements direction while separately developing safe practice/training spaces and mastery guidance.

ALP consumes Character progression, Reputation/Relationship, World/Scene, Projects, GCL, ISE, MAL and campaign authority. Achievements recognize evidence and may link to explicitly governed rewards, but they do not become universal permission or capability gates.

Recovered basis: RDC-08A/RDC-08B, RD-13 and RD-16. The owner has explicitly directed that suitable recovered mine-note families be added to the roadmap.

## Tranches

1. **ALP-01 — Authority Crosswalk & Achievement/Learning/Practice Taxonomy**  
   Reconcile owner-confirmed campaign Achievements, platform onboarding/mastery milestones, training/practice Scenes, Projects and reward authorities. Separate recognition, teaching evidence, campaign accomplishments and mechanical rewards.

2. **ALP-02 — Achievement Definitions, Criteria, Evidence, Scope & Provenance**  
   Define stable achievement criteria, evidence receipts, author/scope, visibility, versioning, repeatability, revocation/correction and provenance. Criteria are auditable and cannot be silently satisfied by AI inference.

3. **ALP-03 — Platform Onboarding & Mastery Milestones**  
   Build optional learning milestones that guide users through Multiversal systems without requiring badge collection or hiding ordinary access behind completion. Support progressive disclosure and user-controlled learning pace.

4. **ALP-04 — GM-Authored Campaign Achievements, Titles, Reputation & Reward Links**  
   Support GM/campaign-local achievements with explicit authorship and scope. Link titles, reputation, faction access, cosmetic/story recognition or explicit Reward Entries through their owning systems; mechanically significant rewards remain power-accounted.

5. **ALP-05 — Diegetic Practice Spaces, Training Scenes & Simulations**  
   Let authored places such as academies, ranges, simulators, Prepariversity-like sites and other real setting locations provide safe practice/tutorial experiences while keeping non-diegetic tutorials first-class.

6. **ALP-06 — Rehearsal, Retry, Safe Failure & Training/Project Integration**  
   Define practice/rehearsal state, retries, coaching/feedback and bounded safe-failure semantics, consuming KFR/DPL/Projects/Character systems where learning has canonical effects rather than inventing a second advancement ledger.

7. **ALP-07 — Player/GM UX, Accessibility, Notifications & Recognition History**  
   Provide achievement/mastery history, opt-in notification behavior, accessible representations, privacy/visibility controls and GM authoring/review surfaces without manipulative engagement requirements.

8. **ALP-08 — MAL/ISE/WCI/GCL Integration & Golden Learning/Recognition Proof**  
   Prove one platform learning path, one GM-authored campaign achievement and one diegetic practice experience using ISE/MAL where useful, while demonstrating that users can learn and play without either microgames or achievement gating.

## Selection invariant

ALP-01 selection grants no implementation authority. Achievements remain optional recognition/learning structures, not universal permission gates. Achievement completion cannot grant unrelated capabilities by implication. Mechanically meaningful rewards commit only through their owning Reward/Progression/Reputation/Faction systems. Platform learning badges and campaign achievements remain distinguishable in scope and authorship. Diegetic practice remains optional. AI may explain progress or propose criteria but may not silently award achievements or infer hidden completion evidence. ALP-02+, VTI-01+, provider activation, tester distribution, release and deployment remain unauthorized.
