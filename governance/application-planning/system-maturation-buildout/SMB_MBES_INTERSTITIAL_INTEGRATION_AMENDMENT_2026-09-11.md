# SMB — MBES Interstitial Integration Amendment — 2026-09-11

**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED FUTURE INTEGRATION AMENDMENT  
**Implementation authority:** none

## Effective sequence

`MSAS-01..21 → MRCS-01..21 → MBES-01..24 → SMB-08 → SMB-09`

## SMB-08 intake change

SMB-08 must consume completed MBES capabilities for first-party built-environment and settlement content rather than introducing bespoke construction, housing, facility, city or environmental-engineering mechanics.

Expected MBES inputs include:

- personal homes, rooms, décor and reusable blueprints;
- bases, facilities, utilities, storage/logistics and production chains;
- terrain/hydrology engineering and environmental externality hooks;
- branch/world/environment condition-aware construction;
- Oara-compatible reactive-world signals and owner-response interfaces;
- residents/staff/facility-use integration;
- damage/maintenance/repair/defense infrastructure;
- settlement/district/city services, transport, property/economy and community projects;
- multi-settlement/regional specialization networks.

## Boundary

SMB-08 remains a first-party content-production tranche. It does not become the owner of the generic MBES runtime, and it may not redefine MIB-14, World/Environment, MCS, MRCS, CEL, ODL, SCL, Economy, Character or Action/Event authority.

This amendment creates no current implementation authority and does not alter ARI-16.
