## Why MBES is interstitial

Multiversal already owns the pieces of construction gameplay across multiple mature systems:

- MIB-14 supplies base/facility/platform foundations;
- APW/D26 supplies Projects and campaign time;
- MIB-12/MIB-13 supply crafting and economy;
- ICF supplies cultivation/production definitions;
- CEL/APM supplies life/cozy orchestration and bounded automation;
- MCS supplies terrain, settlement, city and interior spatial authoring;
- MRCS supplies rules/content/blueprint authoring;
- ODL supplies organization/business/base/settlement relationship seams;
- SCL supplies strategic settlement/world/campaign consequences;
- World/Environment/Reality/Branch owners supply actual environmental and world-condition truth.

Without MBES, those capabilities remain fragmented. MBES closes the player/GM experience before SMB-08 begins substantial first-party content production.

## Owner-directed world-reactivity requirement

Different branches, realities, worlds and environments may change construction viability, material behavior, utilities, hazards, ecological capacity and development consequences.

Oara is a required golden reference case because its planetary sentience responds to pollution and over-development. MBES must support Oara through a generic owner-domain reactive-world interface:

`construction/operation → typed externality observations → World/Environment interpretation → governed response/consequence`

MBES may record and project externalities but cannot invent the canonical response. Exact Oaran thresholds and reactions remain owned by the canonical World/Environment rules.

## Successor impact

- MRCS-21 successor becomes `MBES-01`.
- MBES-24 successor is `SMB-08`.
- SMB-08 consumes the completed built-environment/settlement gameplay layer rather than inventing a parallel first-party base/city engine.
- SAA remains after SMB-09 and is unaffected except that later authored stories may consume MBES-built places and development consequences.

It does not move the current ARI pointer, authorize provider activation, reserve a migration, publish content or perform application implementation.
