## Source-supported design patterns

The supplied material demonstrates useful structures for LNG:
- explicit grammar/vocabulary decisions and corrections;
- language-family and historical-stage evolution;
- dialect/register distinctions;
- lexicon, phrasebook, idiom and cultural-use material;
- compositional magical-language morphology in the Arcan source;
- distinct phonological/grammatical character between Dominix language families.

## Owner temporal ruling

The supplied Dominix languages are **forward in time from the current Dominix setting**. Their existence in the source library does not make them current-period languages.

LNG must therefore represent them as future-stage candidate/reference content until the appropriate Dominix chronology is explicitly mapped. They may inform ancestor/descendant design, but present-era campaign projections, NPC language knowledge, naming tools, translation aids, AI context and search must exclude them unless an authorized temporal/multiversal knowledge path makes them available.

No source-generated language statement is silently promoted to canonical setting truth without reconciliation.
