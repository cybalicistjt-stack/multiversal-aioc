# LNG — Language & Linguistics Foundation

**Program ID:** LNG  
**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED — PLANNED INTERSTITIAL  
**Activation:** after LSS-10  
**Successor:** MSS-01  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner

## Purpose

LNG lets creators track, build and use fictional languages without turning Multiversal into an academic linguistics simulator. The target is strong Levels 1–5 support (registry through gameplay) plus a bounded Level-6 generation/consistency assistant.

## Tranches

1. **LNG-01 — Source Intake, Language Registry & Timeline Authority**  
   Catalog existing languages/dialects/scripts and bind availability to World/Reality/Timeline/Era. Future/historical stages remain inaccessible outside legitimate temporal knowledge paths.

2. **LNG-02 — Lexicon, Phrase, Script & Provenance Model**  
   Words, compounds, phrases, idioms, ritual expressions, slang, pronunciation, meanings, etymology, scripts, examples, terminology and accepted/deprecated/rejected design decisions.

3. **LNG-03 — Guided Language Construction Toolkit**  
   Approachable basic/advanced creator workflows for phonology, phonotactics, morphology, syntax, grammar, numerals, pronouns, tense/aspect, comparison and word formation.

4. **LNG-04 — Families, Dialects, Registers & Historical Evolution**  
   Parent/descendant language stages, dialects, registers, borrowing, semantic drift, sound/grammar changes and timeline-aware language evolution.

5. **LNG-05 — Character Knowledge, Translation & Gameplay Integration**  
   Fluency/literacy/intelligibility, inscriptions, partial comprehension, linguistic clues, naming, culture/species/faction/World links and permission-safe Character knowledge.

6. **LNG-06 — Generation, Consistency Tools & Golden Language Proof**  
   Deterministic/rule-guided word and name candidate generation, derivation/evolution assistance, contradiction detection, translation proposals clearly separated from canon, and multi-era golden language fixtures.

## Temporal rule

The owner-supplied Dominix language material is currently **future-stage reference relative to the active Dominix setting period**. It may be stored as source/provenance and used to design historical evolution, but it must not silently populate present-era speech, NPC knowledge, naming, translation, AI context or player search.

## Invariants

- Language, script, dialect/register and historical stage are distinct.
- A language may use multiple scripts; a script may serve multiple languages.
- AI/generated translations and words are proposals until accepted.
- Missing vocabulary/grammar remains unknown rather than fabricated as canon.
- Character language knowledge and GM-hidden language facts remain permission-scoped.
- Language evolution never retroactively changes earlier eras.
