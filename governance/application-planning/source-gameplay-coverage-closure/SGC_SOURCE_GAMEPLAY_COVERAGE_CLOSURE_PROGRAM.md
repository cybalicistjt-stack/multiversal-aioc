# SGC — Source & Gameplay Coverage Closure

**Program ID:** SGC  
**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED — PLANNED INTERSTITIAL  
**Activation:** after VTI-12  
**Successor:** MIB-16  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner  
**Planned:** 2026-08-20

## Purpose

SGC is the explicit closure gate that prevents retained source mechanics, content families, prior approved design work or discussed product functions from silently disappearing between source recovery and implementation. Every retained concept must receive a traceable disposition: implemented, planned/owned elsewhere, content-only, explicitly superseded, intentionally unsupported, unresolved-owner-decision, or provenance-only.

SGC is not permission to invent missing rules. It turns unknowns and incomplete records into visible closure records instead of filling them with guesses.

The 2026-08-27 recovered mine-note roadmap amendment and the outputs of SSA, KFR, ODL, MAL and ALP are mandatory SGC inputs. Routed mine-note candidates that deliberately remain in existing owners—such as scale transition in SCL, story/telling identity in CSW/WCI, annotations in WCI/current owners, motivation in Character/CSW and UZ conceptuality in setting-specific/SGC-05 review—must receive explicit closure rather than being considered covered by broad program names alone.

## Tranches

1. **SGC-01 — Retained Source Corpus Inventory & Disposition Taxonomy**  
   Establish the final source/document/CSV/design-conversation inventory in scope, stable source references and the allowed disposition states with evidence requirements. Include the recovered mine-note disposition/register and roadmap amendment as retained planning/recovery sources.

2. **SGC-02 — Mechanics, Rules, Loops & Runtime Coverage Matrix**  
   Map every retained mechanical rule/loop/function to the owning runtime/program/tranche, implementation evidence or explicit unresolved/superseded disposition; detect orphan mechanics and duplicated authorities. Include SSA/KFR/ODL/MAL/ALP and mine-note concepts intentionally routed into existing owners.

3. **SGC-03 — Abilities, Powers, Progression, Traditions & Character-System Coverage**  
   Close retained ability-tree, species, profession, prestige, magic, innate, elementalist, faction, proficiency, progression and related records including missing-definition/no-effect/unspecified-value records without fabricating absent source data. Audit recovered Character-motivation and familiarity relationships against their exact owners.

4. **SGC-04 — Items, Creatures, Vehicles, Facilities, Materials & Content-Entity Coverage**  
   Reconcile retained content catalogs against governed definitions/packs, including creature templates/ecology, gear, materials, facilities, vehicles/modules, recipes and other content families; record gaps and exact owning expansion path.

5. **SGC-05 — Specialized & Edge-Case Gameplay Closure**  
   Resolve smaller systems that should reuse shared engines rather than become standalone programs: freerunning/mobility, traps, nonlethal combat, weapon proficiency, martial styles, hazards, rest/exhaustion, specialist medicine, construct creation, expeditions and other retained edge mechanics. Include the targeted Toon/UZ conceptuality hold only if that setting is active enough to resolve it; otherwise record its explicit source-hold disposition.

6. **SGC-06 — UX, Creator, Avatar, Accessibility & Workflow Coverage**  
   Map retained screen/workflow/design requirements—including Character appearance/avatar construction, authoring tools, GM/Player/Creator flows, accessibility-equivalent state and device behavior—to implemented or explicitly planned product surfaces. Include mine-note spatial authoring, microgame/practice and user-annotation UX routes.

7. **SGC-07 — Gap Resolution Queue, Owner Decisions, Supersession & Explicit Unsupported Records**  
   Produce and resolve the bounded owner-decision queue for genuine conflicts/unknowns; record why superseded or intentionally unsupported material is not implemented; prohibit silent deletion or vague future buckets.

8. **SGC-08 — Final Source-to-Product Traceability, Regression & Closure Proof**  
   Publish the final source→system→tranche→implementation/content/disposition graph, unresolved exception register, coverage metrics and regression validators. Completion requires zero unclassified retained concepts, including every recovered mine-note candidate and routed non-program concept.

## Invariants

- No retained concept is considered covered merely because a broad program name could theoretically contain it.
- Missing definitions/effect text/amounts remain explicit source gaps unless authoritative evidence resolves them.
- SGC does not create duplicate mechanics or content ledgers; it routes to owning systems.
- Historical conversation/source evidence cannot override newer canonical repository authority.
- Completion requires disposition closure, not necessarily implementation of intentionally unsupported or provenance-only material.
