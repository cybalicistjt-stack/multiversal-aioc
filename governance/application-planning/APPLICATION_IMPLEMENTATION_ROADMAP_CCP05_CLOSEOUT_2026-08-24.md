## Delivered semantics

CCP-05 adds four governed care/welfare seams: need support, health/recovery support, aging/lifecycle reference and welfare observation. The layer references source/profile-owned biology, health and lifecycle facts rather than creating a replacement physiology/health ledger.

Source-confirmed sapient care/treatment participation requires explicit voluntary consent. No forced treatment, automatic compliance or ownership is created. MIB-09 remains relationship identity/numeric-state authority, and care does not automatically alter relationship values.

No universal need schedule or quantity, healing/recovery rate, aging curve, mortality rule, welfare score, neglect threshold, care-quality score or outcome formula was introduced. Migration `0022` remains unreserved.
