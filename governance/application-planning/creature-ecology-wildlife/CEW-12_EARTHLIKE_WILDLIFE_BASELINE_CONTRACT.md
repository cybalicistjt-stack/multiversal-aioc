# CEW-12 Earthlike Animal & Wildlife Baseline Contract

**Contract ID:** `CEW-EARTHLIKE-BASE-1.0`  
**Work item:** CEW-12 — Earthlike Animal & Wildlife Baseline  
**Authority:** CEW-ID-1.0, CEW-CLASS-1.0, CEW-HAB-1.0, CEW-DIST-1.0, CEW-ECO-1.0, CEW-COG-PART-1.0, CEW-HAV-LIN-1.0, CEW-REL-PATH-1.0, and ENV-HS-1.0  
**Application implementation authority:** none

## Governing rule

Earthlike baseline is an ecological content library, not a canonical Earth distribution claim.

CEW-12 establishes a broad, ordinary animal/wildlife profile library for ecological authoring and later gap analysis. It is deliberately not an exhaustive encyclopedia of terrestrial life, not a canonical creature-definition registry, not a statblock library, and not a completeness score.

Source recovery and governed first-party baseline authoring remain distinguishable.

## Retained source recovery

The retained `animals 11-16-24.PDF` table contains 33 explicit animal labels. Thirty-two ordinary labels seed CEW-12 baseline profiles. `Spider (Giant)` remains source evidence but is excluded from the ordinary baseline because the source itself marks the extraordinary size variant.

The retained animals table is evidence, not a canonical statblock registry.

CEW-07 found no safely recovered statblock records for this source family. CEW-12 does not reinterpret that as evidence absence, and it does not promote the table's AC, HP, speed, attacks, damage, traits, or related legacy mechanics into canonical creature definitions.

`Beast Creatures 1.PDF` and `Beasts 2.PDF` provide broad ecological/thematic context. Their Beast collection membership does not mean ordinary Earthlike identity and their source labels do not become canonical Creature Definitions by name.

## First-party baseline authoring

CEW-12 adds governed first-party baseline profiles to broaden ordinary coverage across mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, bony and cartilaginous fish, insects, arachnids, crustaceans, mollusks, and other invertebrates.

These additions are labeled `governed_first_party_baseline`. They are authoring, not recovered source claims. Source-seeded profiles explicitly retain `animals 11-16-24.PDF` as the identity-label seed while their habitat-family and ecological-role fields are separately governed first-party ecological authoring.

The 100-profile library is a useful baseline, not an exhaustive species target and not a quota for later expansion.

## Ecology and environment

Habitat families and ecological roles are broad CEW content descriptors intended to make the baseline useful across ENV-HS-1.0 environment matching and future CEW gap analysis.

Habitat suitability does not create canonical presence, nativeness, rarity, commonness, visibility, or range. Ecological role does not create encounter placement. Canonical distribution remains governed by CEW-DIST-1.0.

CEW-13 owns environment-driven wildlife gap expansion.

## Personhood and relationships

Ordinary does not mean nonsapient.

Cognition/personhood remains unknown unless explicit source or governed authority establishes it. The baseline therefore cannot be used to erase sapience, personhood, autonomy, or NPC eligibility in a particular setting.

Domestic does not mean pet, companion, mount, familiar, owned, bonded, or trained.

Relationship pathways remain governed by CEW-REL-PATH-1.0 and completed CCP systems. Large size does not create mount eligibility; small size does not create familiar compatibility. Sapient/person-level relationships require voluntary consent.

Likewise, ordinary Earthlike similarity does not establish Havalaea Time-of-Troubles lineage, later-imported status, or NPC projection.

## Identity boundary

A CEW-12 baseline profile ID is a noncanonical library identity. It is not an `mv.object.creature-definition` stable ID.

Name similarity between a baseline profile, source label, ingredient record, recovered statblock, or canonical Creature Definition does not create identity equivalence. CEW-ID-1.0 remains controlling.

## Mutation boundary

CEW-12 has no application implementation authority.

It performs no Multiversal-app runtime, schema, UI, migration, canonical Creature Definition, canonical distribution, live relationship, encounter-placement, or campaign-state mutation.

## Completion boundary

CEW-12 is `completed_verified` only after exact-head validation, merge, canonical post-merge verification, and strict-successor selection.

CEW-13 is the strict successor.
