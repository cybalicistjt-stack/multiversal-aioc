# CEW-09 — Intelligence, Personhood, Domestication & Partnership Classification — Completion Report

**Contract:** `CEW-COG-PART-1.0`  
**State:** completion candidate  
**Owner:** John Brandon Turner  
**Application implementation authority:** none

## Completed scope

CEW-09 establishes the source-backed classification boundary for intelligence/cognition, personhood/sapience, definition-level domestication/trainability, consent-sensitive partnership semantics, and NPC-capable presentation.

The tranche recovers the five-tier intelligence model from `Animal training.PDF`, preserves playable-creature and Havalaea Sapient Animal conversion semantics from `Player Creatures.PDF`, and records bounded behavior/cognition evidence from retained animal, Beast, and Havalaea creature sources. Twelve direct evidence records are preserved without canonical stable-ID binding.

## Key decisions

- Intelligence and personhood remain independent axes.
- Tier 5 / INT 9+, dialogue, language, tactics, telepathy, or strong intelligence descriptors do not auto-create sapience/personhood.
- Sapience does not erase Animal biological/ecological identity or require humanoid anatomy.
- The Havalaea Sapient Animal Template is conversion/template authority, not a blanket fact for all Havalaea Creature Definitions; CEW-10 owns native-lineage application.
- Definition-level wild/domesticated/trainable/untrainable claims require explicit authority. Temperament and `Loyal`, `Docile`, or `Bonded` source tags do not create live ownership, taming, training, obedience, or relationship state.
- Source-confirmed sapient beings cannot enter through taming; voluntary relationship formation requires explicit consent.
- Rescue remains nonbonding by itself.
- Mount, pet/companion, familiar and related pathway roles remain CEW-11-owned.
- NPC projection is presentation only and does not change creature identity, animal ecology, personhood, ownership, or tamability.
- The current 27 canonical Creature Definitions remain unknown for CEW-09 stable-ID cognition/personhood/domestication bindings because no explicit bindings are presently governed.

## Scope preserved

CEW-09 performs no canonical Creature Definition population, no Havalaea native-lineage assignment, no relationship-pathway assignment, no new creature creation, and no Multiversal-app runtime/schema/UI/migration mutation.

## Strict successor

**CEW-10 — Havalaea Native Fauna & Time-of-Troubles Ecology** is the strict successor and is selected only after CEW-09 verification/merge closeout.
