# CEW-09 — Cognition, Personhood, Domestication & Partnership Contract

**Contract:** `CEW-COG-PART-1.0`  
**Work item:** CEW-09  
**Owner:** John Brandon Turner  
**Application implementation authority:** none

## Purpose

CEW-09 classifies source-backed cognition, personhood/sapience, definition-level domestication/trainability, consent-sensitive partnership semantics, and NPC-presentation eligibility without converting those facts into creature identity, ownership, live relationship state, or future CEW pathway assignments.

**Intelligence is not personhood.** Intelligence scores, training tiers, reasoning statements, language, telepathy, tactical behavior, or source phrases such as “highly intelligent” are evidence for cognition only at the scope the source supports. Personhood and sapience require their own source or owner authority.

**Sapience does not erase animal ecological identity.** A sapient animal remains capable of retaining its Animal biological/ecological identity. Sapience does not imply humanoid anatomy, and animal/Beast terminology does not imply nonsapience.

## Intelligence and cognition

`Animal training.PDF` supplies a five-tier training-oriented intelligence model:

| Tier | Intelligence | Source training implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1–2 | instinctual training; simple commands |
| 2 | 3–4 | basic behavioral commands with repetition |
| 3 | 5–6 | tactical and multi-step tasks |
| 4 | 7–8 | complex instructions and teamwork |
| 5 | 9+ | reasoning, improvisation and dialogue |

These bands are retained as source training/cognition evidence. Tier 5 or INT 9+ is not a universal personhood threshold. Speech or dialogue capability is likewise not automatic personhood authority.

Representative creature-source cognition descriptions remain source-scoped. Tactical pack behavior, strong comparative-intelligence language, an intelligence score, or communication ability may all be recorded without silently promoting the subject to sapient/person status.

## Personhood and sapience

`Player Creatures.PDF` contains conversion authority that can give a normally nonsapient species full sentience and language in the playable-creature conversion process. That conversion is not a base-species rewrite.

The same source contains the Havalaea Sapient Animal Template: fully sentient, speaking, culturally aware, minimum INT 8, PC/NPC-capable, and not dependent on humanoid anatomy. CEW-09 records the template semantics but does not apply it to every Havalaea Creature Definition. Havalaea native-lineage and Time-of-Troubles application remains CEW-10-owned.

NPC-capable presentation is independent of personhood and identity. A source/owner-backed animal may be eligible for NPC or hybrid presentation without becoming humanoid, owned, tame, or non-animal.

## Domestication and training

Definition-level CEW-09 facts may state only what authority supports: source-asserted wild, source-asserted domesticated, source-asserted trainable, source-asserted generally untrainable, or unknown.

**Trainability is not current training state, ownership, or obedience.** Temperament can affect training context, but temperament does not establish domestication. A `Loyal` trait does not establish ownership. A `Docile` trait does not establish that an individual is tame. A source `Bonded` tag is not a substitute for current relationship evidence.

Individual taming, recruitment, training, command, bond, ownership, and live relationship state remain with the existing CCP/runtime authorities rather than the reusable Creature Definition classification.

## Partnership and consent

CEW-09 consumes CCP-02 and CCP-03 semantics without assigning future relationship roles.

**Source-confirmed sapient beings cannot enter through taming.**

**Sapient relationship formation requires explicit voluntary consent.**

Rescue remains nonbonding by itself. No classification creates automatic ownership or automatic obedience, and there is no universal taming, recruitment, eligibility, or bond-success formula.

**Mount, pet/companion, and familiar pathway roles remain CEW-11-owned.** CEW-09 may preserve consent and autonomy requirements that those future pathways must obey, but it does not populate them.

## Canonical stable-ID boundary

The current 27 governed Creature Definitions have no explicit CEW-09 stable-ID bindings for intelligence/cognition, personhood/sapience, or domestication/training. Their states therefore remain unknown for these axes unless and until explicit source-backed or governed stable-ID evidence is established.

Display-name similarity, source collection, game type, ecology, behavior, namespace, or apparent fit cannot substitute for an explicit binding.

## NPC projection boundary

NPC projection requires source or owner backing and is a presentation capability only. It does not:

- change Creature Definition identity;
- erase animal ecological identity;
- create personhood;
- create ownership, tamability, training, bonding, or obedience.

## Handoff

**CEW-10 is the strict successor.** CEW-10 owns Havalaea native fauna and Time-of-Troubles lineage/ecology application. CEW-11 remains the owner of mount, pet/companion, familiar, work/service, and supernatural partnership-pathway crosswalks.

CEW-09 carries **no application implementation authority** and authorizes no Multiversal-app runtime, schema, UI, migration, relationship-ledger, or campaign-state mutation.
