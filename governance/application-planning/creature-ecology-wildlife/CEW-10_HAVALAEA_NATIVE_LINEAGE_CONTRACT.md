# CEW-10 — Havalaea Native Fauna & Time-of-Troubles Ecology Contract

**Contract:** `CEW-HAV-LIN-1.0`  
**Work item:** CEW-10  
**Owner:** John Brandon Turner  
**Application implementation authority:** none

## Purpose

CEW-10 defines the Havalaea ecological-lineage boundary needed to distinguish owner-approved Time-of-Troubles native fauna from later imported animals while preserving the setting's source-described convergence, relocation, reintroduction, engineering, ferality, and Riftfall-displacement history.

**Presence on Havalaea is not native-lineage proof.** CEW-05 already establishes Havalaea setting presence for five canonical Creature Definitions while explicitly leaving their native status unknown.

**Birth on Havalaea alone is not Time-of-Troubles lineage proof.** The owner-approved native-fauna invariant requires descent from a Time-of-Troubles lineage; later-imported lineages remain distinguishable even when later descendants are born on Havalaea.

**Cultural or character nativeness is not ecological lineage.** `Player Creatures.PDF` may create a culturally aware native of Havalaea through the Havalaean Sapient Animal Template without proving that the converted character or base species descends from a Time-of-Troubles ecological lineage.

## Lineage/origin classes

CEW-10 recognizes the following source/authority-scoped lineage states:

- `time_of_troubles_native_lineage` — the owner-defined Havalaea-native ecological lineage class. Individual assignment requires explicit source, owner, or governed stable-ID authority.
- `later_imported_lineage` — lineages introduced after the Time-of-Troubles native-lineage boundary.
- `relocated_returned_or_reintroduced_lineage` — lineages with explicit relocation and later return/reintroduction history; this does not determine their older ancestry.
- `warden_bred_or_engineered_lineage` — Green Warden or related breeding/engineering/seeding history; this does not automatically establish native descent.
- `rift_displaced_hybridized_or_conceptual_lineage` — explicitly non-native/displaced/hybridized/conceptual Riftfall or convergence-origin occurrences.
- `unknown` — the required state whenever lineage authority is insufficient.

**Warden-bred, engineered, seeded, wild, feral, relocated, returned, or reintroduced status does not silently become Time-of-Troubles native lineage.** These facts remain separately useful and may coexist with a lineage class when explicit authority establishes both.

## Source world ecology

`Havalaea.PDF` describes Havalaea as a controlled multiversal convergence world and biodiverse ark involving species and ecosystems from across realities. The Green Wardens terraformed the world to balance thousands of species, and Goblin Imperial Engineering seeded the zones.

That history makes source provenance essential. Havalaea's fauna cannot be modeled as a single undifferentiated native pool.

The source also explicitly preserves several distinct ecological histories:

- Nursery creatures were relocated to safer worlds after the Goblin Empire returned;
- reintroduced species exist, and a relocated species may wander back;
- Chirrals were bred by the Green Wardens and now reproduce wild;
- Dustroot Hounds were once domesticated and later went feral;
- Riftfall Verge creatures are often not native, but displaced, hybridized, or partially conceptual.

None of those histories can be converted into Time-of-Troubles ancestry without further authority.

## Current canonical Havalaea Creature Definitions

The five current setting-scoped Creature Definitions are:

- `mv.setting.havalaea.creature.rootstalker`
- `mv.setting.havalaea.creature.hisscap-frog`
- `mv.setting.havalaea.creature.mossling-glider`
- `mv.setting.havalaea.creature.sapcrawl-varnet`
- `mv.setting.havalaea.creature.jungle-slip-beetle`

CEW-05 proves setting presence for these five but not native lineage. `Havalaea Creatures.PDF` contains exact or near display labels for several current definitions, but CEW-ID-1.0 and CEW-COV-1.0 prohibit name-only canonical binding. CEW-10 therefore records **zero** explicit Time-of-Troubles stable-ID lineage assignments and keeps all five lineage states unknown.

## Sapient native animals and NPC projection

**Sapience does not erase animal ecological identity.** If source or owner authority confirms that a Time-of-Troubles native Havalaean animal is human-level/sapient, it still retains its animal ecological identity.

Such an animal may project through the existing NPC system when source or owner authority supports that presentation. **NPC projection does not imply humanoid identity, ownership, tamability, or loss of autonomy.**

CEW-09 remains authoritative for cognition/personhood and voluntary-consent rules. Sapient/person-level animals cannot be routed through taming, and relationship formation requires explicit voluntary consent.

## Distribution and ecology separation

Lineage/origin is independent of current distribution and habitat suitability. Native lineage does not widen range, and current range does not prove lineage. Likewise, wild, feral, domesticated, adapted, returned, reintroduced, or Warden-managed status is not a lineage shortcut.

## Relationship-pathway boundary

CEW-10 does not assign mount, pet/companion, familiar, work/service, or supernatural partnership roles. Those remain relationship/pathway capabilities rather than base creature types or lineage classes.

**CEW-11 is the strict successor.** CEW-11 owns the mount, pet, familiar, companion, work/service, and related relationship-system crosswalk while consuming CEW-09 consent/personhood constraints and CEW-10 lineage/ecological identity.

CEW-10 carries **no application implementation authority** and authorizes no Multiversal-app runtime, schema, UI, migration, canonical Creature Definition population, ownership, taming, training, bonding, or campaign-state mutation.
