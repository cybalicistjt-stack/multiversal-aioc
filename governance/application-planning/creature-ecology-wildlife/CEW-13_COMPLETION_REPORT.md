# CEW-13 Completion Report

## Result

CEW-13 — **Environment-Driven Wildlife Gap Expansion** establishes `CEW-ENV-GAP-1.0`, audits all **76** governed ENV presets against the completed CEW-12 ordinary baseline, and authors **29** noncanonical first-party wildlife profiles only where narrower ordinary ecological representation is useful.

The work treats environment specificity rather than raw species count as the gap signal. CEW-12 remains the reusable 100-profile baseline; CEW-13 is a bounded coverage repair, not a species quota.

## Preset audit

The 76-preset audit resolves to:

- **32** presets with ordinary environment-specific gap expansion;
- **14** presets where CEW-12 reuse is sufficient;
- **10** nonordinary/space/alien presets deferred to CEW-14;
- **3** extraordinary/extreme-adaptation presets deferred to CEW-15;
- **17** built/contextual presets where wildlife presence requires independent distribution or local-placement authority.

The audit records **29** ordinary environment-gap anchors. These include flowing freshwater, floodplain, estuary, peat wetland, flooded forest, tidal flats, coral reef, kelp forest, deep ocean, ocean trench, savanna, steppe, scrub, hills, canyon, rocky desert, salt flats, taiga, tundra, glacier, polar ice, sea ice, farmland, suburb, road/trail, mine/quarry, transit-hub, harbor/dock and submerged-cave distinctions.

## Expansion library

The **29** authored profiles are `noncanonical_environment_gap_profile` records with `governed_first_party_environment_gap_expansion` provenance. The one-profile-per-gap representation is the bounded CEW-13 ecological anchor layer; it is not a claim that a habitat contains only one species.

They carry ENV-HS-1.0-aligned creature-side habitat predicates. They receive:

- zero canonical stable-ID bindings;
- zero canonical distribution bindings;
- zero statblocks;
- zero encounter placements;
- unknown cognition/personhood unless separately governed;
- unknown relationship pathways unless separately governed.

Profile count is the result of the bounded gap repair, not a quota or completeness score.

## Deferred work

Spaceborne, alien and other nonordinary wildlife gaps remain explicitly deferred to **CEW-14 — Multiversal & Alien Wildlife Expansion**.

Monster, supernatural, mutated and extraordinary/extreme-adaptation creature gaps remain explicitly deferred to **CEW-15 — Monster, Extraordinary Creature & Creature-Type Gap Expansion**.

These deferrals are deliberate scope boundaries, not claims of absence.

## Locked boundaries

Habitat suitability remains separate from canonical distribution. Environment similarity does not create creature identity. Environment or overlay selection does not create adaptation, presence, visibility, encounter placement, relationship state, abilities or personhood.

Built/settled environments do not receive automatic wildlife populations merely because ordinary animals could plausibly use them.

## Mutation boundary

CEW-13 performs no Multiversal-app runtime, schema, UI, migration, canonical Creature Definition, canonical distribution, relationship-state, encounter-placement or campaign-state mutation. It grants no application implementation authority.

## Closeout

CEW-13 is `completed_verified` only after exact candidate repository-health/control-plane validation and exact canonical post-merge verification.

Strict successor: **CEW-14 — Multiversal & Alien Wildlife Expansion** (`selected_not_started`).
