# CEW-10 — Havalaea Native Fauna & Time-of-Troubles Ecology — Completion Report

**Contract:** `CEW-HAV-LIN-1.0`  
**State:** completion candidate  
**Owner:** John Brandon Turner  
**Application implementation authority:** none

## Completed scope

CEW-10 establishes the governed Havalaea lineage/origin classification needed to preserve the owner's distinction between Time-of-Troubles native fauna and later imported animals without flattening the setting's source-described ecological history.

The tranche records the world-level convergence-ark context from `Havalaea.PDF`, the 46-profile setting-associated creature corpus from `Havalaea Creatures.PDF`, and the culturally native sapient-animal conversion semantics from `Player Creatures.PDF`. It preserves relocation, return/reintroduction, Warden-bred/engineered, feral, and Riftfall non-native/displaced histories as distinct facts rather than treating any of them as shortcuts to native lineage.

## Key decisions

- Time-of-Troubles native lineage is an owner-approved ecological-lineage class requiring descent authority; birth or current presence on Havalaea alone is insufficient.
- Later imported animal lineages remain distinguishable even when descendants are born on Havalaea.
- Cultural/character nativeness from the Havalaean Sapient Animal Template is not ecological-lineage proof.
- Warden breeding, engineering, seeding, wild reproduction, ferality, relocation, return, and reintroduction do not automatically establish Time-of-Troubles descent.
- Riftfall explicitly preserves non-native, displaced, hybridized, and partially conceptual creature origins.
- The five current canonical Havalaea Creature Definitions retain CEW-05 setting presence but remain lineage-unknown because no explicit stable-ID Time-of-Troubles or later-imported lineage authority is governed.
- The dedicated Havalaea creature PDF remains source evidence rather than name-only canonical identity or lineage authority.
- Human-level/sapient native Havalaean animals retain animal ecological identity and may project through the NPC system when source or owner authority supports that classification.
- NPC projection does not imply humanoid identity, ownership, tamability, obedience, or loss of autonomy.
- Sapient/person-level relationship formation continues to require CEW-09/CCP voluntary-consent handling.
- Mount, pet/companion, familiar, work/service and related relationship pathways remain CEW-11-owned.

## Scope preserved

CEW-10 performs no canonical Creature Definition lineage population, no name-only source binding, no new creature creation, no relationship-pathway assignment, and no Multiversal-app runtime/schema/UI/migration mutation.

## Strict successor

**CEW-11 — Mount, Pet, Familiar & Companion-System Crosswalk** is the strict successor and is selected only after CEW-10 verification/merge closeout.
