# CEW-13 — Environment-Driven Wildlife Gap Expansion Contract

**Contract:** `CEW-ENV-GAP-1.0`  
**Work item:** CEW-13 — Environment-Driven Wildlife Gap Expansion  
**Authority:** CEW-EARTHLIKE-BASE-1.0, CEW-HAB-1.0, CEW-DIST-1.0, CEW-ECO-1.0, ENV-HS-1.0 and ENV-CD-1.0  
**Application implementation authority:** none

## Governing rule

CEW-13 repairs **ordinary ecological representation gaps** exposed by the completed ENV library. A gap is not a missing canonical creature and is not a mandate to invent a population merely because an environment exists.

**Habitat suitability is not canonical distribution.**

The completed 76-preset / 19-archetype / 47-overlay ENV corpus is compared against CEW-12's 100-profile ordinary Earthlike baseline. CEW-12 profiles are reused whenever their existing abstraction is sufficient. New CEW-13 profiles are authorized only when a governed ENV preset contains a materially distinguishing ordinary ecological condition that the CEW-12 broad habitat families do not represent well enough for later ecological matching and gap analysis.

## Gap qualification

A qualifying CEW-13 gap requires all of the following:

1. a governed ENV preset or Habitat Signature condition;
2. an ordinary ecological distinction meaningful to wildlife use;
3. inadequate representation by the CEW-12 broad baseline at that same distinction;
4. a bounded first-party ecological profile that can describe the distinction without claiming canonical identity, range, encounter placement or relationship state.

**No numeric gap score** is authorized. Profile count is not a target, quota, quality score or proof of completeness.

The preset audit therefore allows five outcomes:

- `cew13_gap_expansion_authored`;
- `cew12_baseline_reuse_sufficient`;
- `deferred_nonordinary_cew14`;
- `deferred_extraordinary_cew15`;
- `distribution_dependent_no_auto_expansion`.

## Environment-driven ordinary expansion

CEW-13 adds governed first-party ecological profiles for narrower ordinary niches including flowing freshwater, floodplains, estuarine/brackish transitions, peat wetlands, flooded forests, tidal flats, coral reefs, kelp forests, deep-ocean and trench environments, savanna/steppe/scrub distinctions, rocky/canyon/salt-flat landforms, taiga/glacier/polar/sea-ice conditions, and selected rural/settled/infrastructure edge habitats.

Every added profile is a `noncanonical_environment_gap_profile`. It is content-authoring material, not a promoted Creature Definition and not a recovered-source record.

Its Habitat Signature predicates are explicitly labeled `CEW-13 governed first-party`. They may use `requires`, `prefers`, `tolerates`, `excludes`, `depends_on`, or `unknown` against governed ENV-HS-1.0 dimensions. They do not alter ENV or write back to any preset, archetype, overlay or local instance.

## Distribution and discovery boundary

Environment selection cannot establish a creature population.

No CEW-13 profile receives canonical World/Reality/Setting/Place distribution. Built, settled, ruined, industrial and infrastructure contexts that do not themselves justify a distinct ordinary ecological profile remain `distribution_dependent_no_auto_expansion` where appropriate. A rat, pigeon, gull, fox, seal or any other profile may only become canonically present when CEW-DIST-1.0 or separately authoritative local/campaign placement establishes that fact.

ENV-CD-1.0 may later consume the new habitat predicates as one input to GM discovery, but distribution, visibility, frequency, season/activity and other gates remain separate.

## Deferred scopes

**CEW-14 — Multiversal & Alien Wildlife Expansion** owns spaceborne, alien, vacuum/zero-gravity, gas-giant, nebular, wormhole, black-hole and other nonordinary wildlife expansion. CEW-13 does not manufacture ordinary Earthlike analogues for those environments.

**CEW-15 — Monster, Extraordinary Creature & Creature-Type Gap Expansion** owns monster/extraordinary wildlife and gaps whose distinguishing concept requires mutation, supernatural adaptation or extraordinary/extreme-environment creature construction. Ordinary ecological profiles can still be independently compatible with an extreme environment if separately supported, but the environment does not generate such adaptation.

Active overlays may satisfy an explicitly authored creature predicate. Overlay presence alone never invents an affinity, adaptation, type, ability, distribution or identity.

## Identity, personhood and relationship boundaries

Display-name similarity never creates a canonical stable-ID binding.

Ordinary Earthlike framing does not mean nonsapient. CEW-09 personhood/cognition authority remains controlling. CEW-10 Havalaea lineage is not inferred from Earthlike similarity. CEW-11 relationship pathways remain unknown unless separately governed. Domesticity, size or ecological fit cannot create pet, companion, mount, familiar, ownership, taming, training or bond state. Sapient/person-level relationships still require voluntary consent.

## Encounter and runtime boundary

Ecological suitability and descriptive ecological function do not create encounter quantity, participant role, placement, difficulty, visibility or live scene state.

CEW-13 performs no Multiversal-app runtime, schema, UI, migration, canonical Creature Definition, canonical distribution, relationship-state, encounter-placement or campaign-state mutation.

## Handoff

The strict successor is **CEW-14 — Multiversal & Alien Wildlife Expansion**.

CEW-14 may consume the completed CEW-12 ordinary baseline and CEW-13 environment-gap audit while preserving the explicit CEW-13 deferrals. It must not reinterpret CEW-13's lack of ordinary coverage in space/nonordinary environments as proof that no canonical creature exists there.
