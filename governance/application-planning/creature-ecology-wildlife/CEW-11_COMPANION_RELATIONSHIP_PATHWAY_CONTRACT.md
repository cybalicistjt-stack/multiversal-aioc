# CEW-11 Companion Relationship Pathway Contract

**Contract ID:** `CEW-REL-PATH-1.0`  
**Work item:** CEW-11 — Mount, Pet, Familiar & Companion-System Crosswalk  
**Authority:** CEW-ID-1.0, CEW-CLASS-1.0, CEW-COG-PART-1.0, CEW-HAV-LIN-1.0, and completed CCP-03 through CCP-10 authorities  
**Application implementation authority:** none

## Governing rule

Mount, pet/companion, familiar, work, service, and combat-partner status are relationship pathways, not creature types.

A reusable creature Definition or source profile may be eligible for zero, one, or several pathways. Eligibility is a content/provenance classification that points to the already completed CCP systems. It is not a replacement relationship system and it is not live campaign state.

Eligibility does not create ownership, bonding, taming, training, obedience, or placement state.

## CCP ownership crosswalk

CEW-11 consumes rather than replaces the completed CCP seams:

- CCP-03 owns taming/recruitment/rescue/bond formation and the transition into an accepted relationship;
- CCP-04 owns training, commands, tasks, tricks, and behavior;
- CCP-05 owns care, needs, health, recovery, aging, and welfare;
- CCP-06 owns mount, pack, service, work, and travel integration;
- CCP-07 owns combat companions, familiars, and supernatural-bond seams;
- CCP-08 owns breeding, reproduction, lineage, and inheritance;
- CCP-09 owns habitats, herds, stables, kennels, and facility operations;
- CCP-10 owns creature ecology, social behavior, lifecycle, and World integration.

CEW-11 creates no duplicate relationship, ownership, creature, Item, travel, combat, facility, breeding, or ecology ledger.

## Eligibility states

The governed eligibility states are:

- `asserted_eligible` — explicit source, owner, or governed stable-ID authority establishes the pathway;
- `conditional_profile_scoped` — the source establishes a pathway only under stated morphology, size, locomotion, capacity, training, equipment, environment, cognition/personhood, or other profile conditions;
- `sapient_voluntary_partnership_only` — source/owner authority supports the pathway only as a voluntary person-level relationship;
- `asserted_not_normally_bondable` — the source explicitly describes normal non-bondability without declaring metaphysical impossibility;
- `explicitly_ineligible` — explicit authority rules the pathway out at the stated scope;
- `unknown` — no sufficient authority exists;
- `not_applicable` — the pathway is outside the subject's declared scope.

Unknown is first-class. Source silence is not ineligibility.

## Consent and personhood

Physical capability is not consent.

Source-confirmed sapient/person-level creatures require explicit voluntary consent for mount, pet/companion, familiar, work, service, combat-partner, and other relationship roles. Neither intelligence, morphology, carrying capacity, NPC presentation, Havalaea lineage, domestication language, nor training potential overrides that requirement.

NPC projection does not create a relationship pathway, and a source/owner-supported human-level Havalaean animal keeps its animal ecological identity when using NPC presentation.

## Non-inference rules

A magical creature is not automatically familiar-compatible.

A trainable or domesticated creature is not automatically a pet or companion.

Likewise:

- size or strength does not establish mount eligibility;
- locomotion does not establish mount eligibility;
- animal or Beast classification does not establish pet status;
- high intelligence does not establish companion or familiar eligibility;
- ecological role, habitat suitability, canonical distribution, or setting presence does not establish a relationship pathway;
- Time-of-Troubles native lineage, later-imported lineage, or another Havalaea lineage state does not establish a relationship pathway;
- source collection membership does not establish a pathway;
- display-name similarity does not create a canonical stable-ID binding;
- a source mount/familiar example does not create a current mounted/bonded relationship.

## Retained source precedence

`Mounts.PDF`, `Familiars and pets.PDF`, and `Animal training.PDF` remain retained source/provenance evidence. `Mounts.PDF` explicitly supplies mount examples; `Familiars and pets.PDF` explicitly distinguishes pet and familiar roles and describes willing sentient familiar bonds and mutual agreement. Those source statements may support profile-level eligibility evidence.

Completed CCP-06 and CCP-07 authority takes precedence over legacy universal formulas in retained Mounts and Familiars source material.

Therefore CEW-11 does not promote legacy universal carrying-capacity, speed, endurance, training-DC, familiar-power, bond-tier, telepathy, shared-sense, resurrection/dismissal, supernatural-range, or related formulas. CCP-04/06/07 remain the current system owners.

## Canonical binding boundary

The current 27 governed Creature Definitions contain no explicit CEW-11 stable-ID relationship-pathway bindings. The five canonical Havalaea Creature Definitions likewise contain no explicit relationship-pathway bindings. Retained source labels and apparent-fit mechanics cannot be used to fill those facts by name similarity.

All 27 remain `unknown` for CEW-11 stable-ID relationship-pathway binding until explicit source/owner/governed identity evidence exists; the Havalaea subset remains five of five unknown.
