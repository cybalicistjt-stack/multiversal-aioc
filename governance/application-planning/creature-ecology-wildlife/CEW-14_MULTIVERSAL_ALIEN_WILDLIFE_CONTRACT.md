# CEW-14 — Multiversal & Alien Wildlife Expansion Contract

**Contract:** `CEW-ALIEN-WILD-1.0`  
**Work item:** CEW-14 — Multiversal & Alien Wildlife Expansion  
**Authority:** content/recovery/design/provenance only; no application implementation authority.

## Purpose

CEW-14 extends the creature ecology corpus into nonordinary and alien wildlife after the ordinary Earthlike baseline and environment-driven ordinary gaps are complete. Its governing rule is simple: **source recovery precedes new invention**.

The tranche therefore does two things in order:

1. carries forward already-governed Havalaea setting-fauna recovery and recovers the retained Skoaltarran setting collection as source-scoped records; then
2. authors noncanonical, explicitly non-sapient first-party wildlife profiles only for CEW-13 nonordinary environment gaps that remain ecologically thin.

CEW-14 does not turn every strange environment into a monster list. CEW-15 owns monster, extraordinary-creature and creature-type gap expansion.

## Source-first recovery

`Havalaea Creatures.PDF` remains governed by CEW-10 at 46 source profiles. CEW-14 does not reopen or rewrite Time-of-Troubles, later-import, reintroduced, Warden-bred, Riftfall, personhood, NPC-projection or relationship-pathway decisions.

`Skoaltarran Creatures.PDF` contributes 39 safe source-statblock records. CEW-14 recovers all 39 as `recoverable_source_record` entries, retaining the source ordinal, label and environment section while leaving the complete source statblocks in the retained PDF.

**Setting-collection membership does not prove native status.** A Skoaltarran collection record is setting-scoped source evidence, not proof of canonical stable identity, native/introduced status, canonical geographic range, commonness or encounter presence.

Source type, Intelligence, language, CR, mechanics, appearance, name similarity and ecological resemblance do not create identity or personhood. Source records remain `personhood_state: unknown` unless separate source/owner/governed authority establishes otherwise.

## CEW-13 nonordinary gap handoff

CEW-13 explicitly deferred ten nonordinary presets to CEW-14:

- Bustling Space Station
- Abandoned Space Station
- The Void
- Asteroid Field
- Gas Giant Upper Atmosphere
- Space Graveyard and Starship Junkyard
- Floating Megacity
- Black Hole Proximity
- Wormhole Convergence
- Nebula

These presets justify ecology design contexts, not canonical populations. No retained source creature is bound to one of these presets merely because its name, type, abilities or apparent habitat seems similar.

CEW-14 therefore records one governed ecological gap anchor for each deferred environment and fills those gaps with first-party noncanonical wildlife profiles. Profile count is a consequence of ecological coverage, never a quota or completion score.

## First-party alien wildlife semantics

New CEW-14 profiles are `noncanonical_nonsapient_alien_wildlife_profile` records. Their non-sapient and non-person status is an explicit first-party design assertion at profile scope; it is not inferred from animal/beast form, Intelligence score, size, ecological role or creature type.

Each profile:

- has no canonical stable-ID binding;
- has no canonical World/Reality/Setting/Place distribution binding;
- creates no statblock;
- creates no campaign or encounter placement;
- creates no mount, pet, companion, familiar, ownership, taming, training or bond state;
- uses CEW-ECO controlled ecological-role values where applicable;
- uses `CEW-HAB-1.0` predicate classes against `ENV-HS-1.0` dimensions;
- may reference a CEW-14 `environment_context_id` through `special_environment_contexts`;
- remains reusable ecological design material until separately governed identity/distribution authority exists.

## Habitat and distribution boundary

Habitat suitability is not canonical distribution. A vacuum-tolerant profile does not occur in every vacuum. A gas-giant profile does not inhabit every gas giant. A wormhole-associated profile does not follow every wormhole, and a black-hole-associated profile is not automatically present near every black hole.

Environment similarity never creates creature identity or range. World/Reality/Setting/Place authority remains able to allow, constrain or veto presence. Unknown distribution stays unresolved rather than being filled by apparent fit.

No numeric ecological-fit, gap, rarity, discovery or encounter score is authorized.

## Ecological authoring boundary

CEW-14 adds ecological niches, not arbitrary monster counts. New profiles emphasize resource feeding, scavenging, colony/herd behavior and ecosystem interaction across artificial interiors, vacuum, low-gravity rock, gas-giant atmosphere, wreckage, megacity superstructure, black-hole proximity, wormhole margins and nebular material.

CEW-14 does not create or normalize creature types, supernatural templates, extraordinary adaptations, monster families or type-taxonomy repairs. Those remain **CEW-15** authority.

The three CEW-13 presets explicitly deferred as extraordinary/extreme-adaptation cases remain untouched here.

## Relationship and personhood boundary

CEW-09 remains the cognition/personhood authority for recovered source records. CEW-11 remains the relationship-pathway authority. CEW-10 remains Havalaea lineage authority.

For first-party CEW-14 profiles, nonsapience/personhood absence is explicitly authored only so the tranche can remain a wildlife expansion rather than silently creating person-level species. This does not establish trainability, tamability, ownership, companion eligibility or any live relationship state.

## Mutation boundary

CEW-14 authorizes no:

- `Multiversal-app` runtime mutation;
- application schema, UI or migration change;
- canonical Creature Definition creation or promotion;
- canonical stable-ID alias binding;
- canonical distribution population;
- campaign/scene/encounter placement;
- live relationship state;
- CEW-15 type-taxonomy mutation.

## Handoff

The strict successor is **CEW-15 — Monster, Extraordinary Creature & Creature-Type Gap Expansion**.

CEW-15 may consume CEW-02/08 taxonomy gaps, CEW-13 extraordinary deferrals, retained extraordinary source families and CEW-14's completed wildlife boundary. It must not retroactively reinterpret CEW-14's source records or wildlife profiles as canonical identity/distribution without separate authority.
