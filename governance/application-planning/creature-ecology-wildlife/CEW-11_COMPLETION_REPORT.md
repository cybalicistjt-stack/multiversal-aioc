# CEW-11 Completion Report

## Result

CEW-11 — **Mount, Pet, Familiar & Companion-System Crosswalk** establishes `CEW-REL-PATH-1.0`, a source-backed relationship-pathway classification seam that consumes the completed CCP systems without creating a second mount, pet, familiar, companion, training, care, combat, breeding, facility, or ecology system.

The crosswalk keeps mount, pet/companion, pack, service, work, travel-support, combat-participation/support, familiar, and supernatural-bond roles independent from base creature type, ecology, distribution, cognition/personhood, Havalaea lineage, NPC presentation, and live relationship state.

## Consent and non-inference

Sapient/person-level creatures require explicit voluntary consent for relationship roles. Physical suitability is not consent. NPC projection, Havalaea lineage, trainability, domestication, intelligence, magical type, ecological role, or apparent mechanical fit cannot silently establish a pathway.

The current **27** canonical Creature Definitions therefore receive **0** new explicit CEW-11 stable-ID pathway bindings and remain **27 unknown**. The five canonical Havalaea Creature Definitions likewise receive **0** explicit pathway bindings and remain **5 unknown**. This is deliberate evidence preservation, not missing-work backfill.
