## Purpose

MSS converts the retained magic/supernatural source corpus and completed PPIA-07 Rune Construction design into one governed application-runtime family. It covers spell/power resolution, Rune Construction, casting resources and strain, traditions, rituals, counterplay, spirits/divine relationships, portals and temporal/causal play without creating a second Action/Event, Character, Item, World/Reality, crafting or approval authority.

PPIA-07 semantics remain input authority. MSS must not fabricate universal mana, damage, healing, duration, failure, backlash or setting-law formulas where source/current owner rules do not support them; such behavior stays profile-bound, unresolved or GM-adjudicated.

## MSS-06 completed boundary

MSS-06 added source/profile-backed definitions for tradition families, schools/paths/sources, casting-profile composition, compatibility and lookup/projection metadata while reusing MSS-01 taxonomy, MSS-02 resource adapters, MSS-03 Action/Event resolution, MSS-04 Rune authority and MSS-05 proposal/provenance rules. It did not create a second Character capability ledger, resource ledger, spell-resolution engine, Item/Asset owner, World/Timeline owner or GM approval authority.

Traditions and schools remain composable descriptors, not mandatory mutually exclusive character classes. A supernatural capability may combine multiple supported traditions/sources/profiles when source/setting rules allow it. Where source evidence is incomplete or contradictory, the profile remains unresolved or source-local rather than inventing a universal rule.

RSR material remains provenance input. Owner-supported facts may be linked as source evidence; assistant-generated mana names/values, ritual effects, portal costs, backlash formulas, soul/pact systems and other recovered expansions remain proposal-only until separately accepted by their owning systems.

## MSS-07 completed boundary

MSS-07 binds rituals to explicit MSS-06 casting profiles, MSS-03 resolution profiles and MSS-02 resource profiles. Ritual phases are deterministically ordered and source/provenance-bound; GM-adjudicated and unresolved rituals remain non-ready. Execution proposals are non-mutating preparation metadata and cannot directly change Action/Event, Character, Item/Asset, World/Timeline or canon.
