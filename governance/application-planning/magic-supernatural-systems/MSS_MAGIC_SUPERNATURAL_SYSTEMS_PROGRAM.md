## Purpose

MSS converts the retained magic/supernatural source corpus and completed PPIA-07 Rune Construction design into one governed application-runtime family. It covers spell/power resolution, Rune Construction, casting resources and strain, traditions, rituals, counterplay, spirits/divine relationships, portals and temporal/causal play without creating a second Action/Event, Character, Item, World/Reality, crafting or approval authority.

PPIA-07 semantics remain input authority. MSS must not fabricate universal mana, damage, healing, duration, failure, backlash or setting-law formulas where source/current owner rules do not support them; such behavior stays profile-bound, unresolved or GM-adjudicated.

## MSS-12 active boundary

MSS-12 is the final internal MSS tranche. It packages completed MSS-01..11 foundations into source/profile-bound content-pack manifests and governed workbench surfaces, adds explicit evidence-based balance-review contracts and deterministic golden proofs, and closes the MSS program without replacing any predecessor or external owner authority.

### Content packs

A supernatural content pack is a deterministic manifest over existing MSS definitions, profiles, rules and source/provenance references. Pack entries identify their owning runtime/domain references and disposition. A pack does not create a second spell resolver, resource ledger, Character ledger, Item/Asset ledger, World/Timeline ledger or approval system. Source-authorized entries require explicit evidence; GM-adjudicated and unresolved material remains visibly non-authoritative.

### Workbench

Workbench contracts may browse, compose, inspect, compare, validate and assemble proposal material from authorized content. They cannot cast, resolve authoritative Action/Events, adjudicate hidden outcomes, reveal restricted content, publish canon, create branches, mutate inventories/resources or directly alter any owning domain. AI remains proposal/explanation-only.

### Balance and evidence review

Balance review records assumptions, evidence, comparison references, observed risks, validation notes and owner/GM disposition. MSS-12 defines no universal power score, fairness equation, encounter rating, equivalence formula or automatic approval threshold. Balance remains contextual to source/profile, owning system and intended play context.

### Golden proof

Golden fixtures must compose representative completed MSS seams deterministically and prove authority preservation: resources remain MSS-02-owned, supernatural resolution MSS-03-owned, authoring proposals MSS-05-governed, casting/ritual/counterplay/relationship/portal/temporal references remain with MSS-06..11, and external Character/Item/World/Campaign/visibility ownership is never duplicated. Equivalent normalized proof inputs must produce identical cross-platform receipts.

### Program closeout

MSS-12 completion must pass exact-head application repository health, self-hosted Linux and Windows governed validation and deterministic comparison, merge the exact validated candidate, then close MSS-12 and the MSS program `completed_verified`. The closeout selects `CCP-01` as `selected_not_started`; it does not start CCP implementation.
