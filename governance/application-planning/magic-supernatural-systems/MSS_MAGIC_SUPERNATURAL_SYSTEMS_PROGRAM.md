## Purpose

MSS converts the retained magic/supernatural source corpus and completed PPIA-07 Rune Construction design into one governed application-runtime family. It covers spell/power resolution, Rune Construction, casting resources and strain, traditions, rituals, counterplay, spirits/divine relationships, portals and temporal/causal play without creating a second Action/Event, Character, Item, World/Reality, crafting or approval authority.

PPIA-07 semantics remain input authority. MSS must not fabricate universal mana, damage, healing, duration, failure, backlash or setting-law formulas where source/current owner rules do not support them; such behavior stays profile-bound, unresolved or GM-adjudicated.
