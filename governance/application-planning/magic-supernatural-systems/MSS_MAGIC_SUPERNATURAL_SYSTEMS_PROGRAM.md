## MSS-12 final boundary

MSS-12 completed the program by adding deterministic content-pack manifests over existing MSS-01..11 definitions and external owner references, authorization-filtered workbench projections, non-mutating composition proposals, contextual evidence-based balance reviews and cross-authority golden proof receipts.

Workbench authorization occurs before references and counts are materialized, preventing hidden/restricted content leakage.
