# Application Implementation Roadmap — MRCS Interstitial Amendment — 2026-09-11

**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED FUTURE PLANNING; NOT STARTED  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner

## Decision

Insert **MRCS — Multiversal Rules & Content Studio** after MSAS-21 and before SMB-08.

Effective local order:

`CNI-01..13 → PCA-01..16 → MCS-01..21 → MCCS-01..21 → MAS-01..21 → MSAS-01..21 → MRCS-01..21 → SMB-08 → SMB-09`

## Purpose

MRCS productizes and expands the existing Multiversal Content Forge into the complete creator-facing environment for constructing rules and reusable content while preserving every owning gameplay/content domain.

MRCS is not a new canonical rules engine, not a second content database, and not authority to reopen completed game-rule programs such as CAB.

## Placement rationale

1. CNI establishes modular content/runtime contracts.
2. PCA provides production recipes, simulation, QA and reusable creator primitives.
3. MCS, MCCS, MAS and MSAS close the major specialist creator surfaces for maps, entities, adventures and audio.
4. MRCS then consumes those finished binding/preview surfaces and completes the generalized rules/content construction layer.
5. SMB-08 becomes the first large first-party content-library consumer of the complete creator-studio chain; SMB-09 follows for long-form campaign proof.

Putting MRCS immediately before SMB-08 avoids making earlier specialist studio implementations depend on a generalized construction studio while still ensuring substantial first-party content production does not begin before Content Forge has a complete governed product destination.

## Authority boundary

- Existing owner-domain rules/contracts remain canonical.
- CAB remains completed advancement/balance authority.
- Content Forge and `content-db` are existing foundations MRCS must converge on rather than duplicate.
- ARI remains resource/provenance/rights authority.
- CNI remains conditional-content/runtime authority.
- Action/Event and runtime owners retain live-state mutation authority.
- MCS/MCCS/MAS/MSAS retain their specialized authoring ownership.
- MRCS creates candidate definitions, governed authoring documents, validation/balance evidence, dependency-safe changes and versioned packs; acceptance/canonicalization still belongs to the owning path.

## Execution rule

MRCS contains 21 execution units. Every unit targets at most 24 active minutes under a healthy governed environment and must split before start if that target is not credible.

## Non-activation

This amendment creates no MRCS implementation authority, branch, runtime preflight, migration, release, provider activation or canonical rules/content mutation. Current ARI execution authority is unchanged.
