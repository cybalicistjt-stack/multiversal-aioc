## Experience target

The owner-approved product direction is to make legitimate map, tileset, token and audio assets **easier to use inside Multiversal than in conventional VTT workflows**.

Key outcomes include:

- upload/import → recognize → calibrate/confirm → **Make Playable**;
- consume supported structured scene metadata such as UVTT-class grid/wall/door/light data instead of retracing it;
- assisted grid detection plus user-correctable rotation/affine/perspective registration for ordinary images;
- tokenized Character/Creature/Vehicle play tied to actual Multiversal owner-domain state;
- meaningful cells, areas, rooms and gridless regions rather than pixels alone;
- governed encounters, hazards, items, objectives, doors, portals, triggers and environment features attached directly to semantic locations;
- fog, exploration memory, lighting, collision and profile-driven nonvisual/supernatural/technological sensor channels;
- levels/elevation plus aligned map-state families and Reality/Timeline/Plane overlays where governing systems allow them;
- Scene Audio Deck with semantic cues, prepared one-shots, intensity/mood controls and Manual/Suggest/authorized-Automatic modes;
- Scene Recipes that share semantic preparation and entitlement-safe provider references/fallback intent without redistributing commercial media;
- tactical-map use when helpful while theater-of-the-mind/mapless play remains first-class.

## Existing-owner relationships

- **A5 Scene** remains the Campaign-local semantic Scene/placement authority.
- **MAI** owns visual/map/tileset/geometry interoperability, provenance and semantic asset resolution.
- **AAI** owns audio source/provider capability negotiation, playback, semantic audio intent and entitlement/license handling.
- **ISE** owns the native interactive tabletop/canvas user experience that composes those owners.
- **World/Reality, Action/Event, Combat, Character, Creature, Item/Asset, Vehicle and permissions** remain their own canonical authorities.
- **VTI** later projects these governed structures into external VTTs at the deepest safe level each platform permits; it does not replace the native ISE experience.

## Nonauthorization

This amendment does not authorize:

- ISE implementation before its predecessors complete and it is explicitly selected/governed-started;
- copying or redistributing Czepeku, Syrinscape, TableTone or other commercial assets beyond their licenses/entitlements;
- unsupported provider scraping or emulation;
- automated Scene triggers bypassing consent, permissions, GM adjudication, Action/Event validation or owning state;
- treating pixels, token positions, generated geometry, visual effects or audio as canonical game truth by themselves;
- requiring a visual map or audio for play.
