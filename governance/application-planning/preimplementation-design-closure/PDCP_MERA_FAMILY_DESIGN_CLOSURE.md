# PDCP — MERA Family Design Closure

**Program:** MERA — Multiversal Engineering, Refit & Assembly  
**PDCP status:** DESIGN CLOSED FOR FAMILY REDUCTION  
**Historical baseline:** 24 tranches  
**Effective implementation/proof plan:** 10 tranches  
**Implementation authority:** none  
**OPS3 mutation:** none

## 1. Closure decision

MERA remains the governed player/GM engineering orchestration layer, but the historical 24-tranche plan repeated several concerns already owned by LSS, MIB-12, DPL/Profession, APW/D26 Project/Time, MIB-14/PPIA-04, ARI/PCA, Packet 07 and PCA-12/Packet 08.

The reduced family keeps only material engineering-specific application/schema/UI/runtime/integration/proof seams. Generic or canonical owner work is consumed through typed adapters and owner operations.

Effective surviving order:

`MERA-01 → MERA-03 → MERA-05 → MERA-06 → MERA-07 → MERA-10 → MERA-12 → MERA-13 → MERA-16 → MERA-24`

Stable milestones are preserved:

- `MERA-01` remains the family start/rotation gate;
- `MERA-24` remains the golden proof and MBES handoff gate;
- no roadmap DAG rewrite is required merely for the reduction.

## 2. Authority crosswalk

MERA consumes rather than duplicates these authorities:

- **PPIA-03 / D17 / Item-Asset owners:** Asset identity, ownership, custody, containment, condition, installation/modification history and lineage.
- **LSS:** recoverable-object decomposition, salvage, partial stripping, donor-component provenance, cannibalization lineage and extraction outputs.
- **MIB-12:** crafting, repair, refurbishment, remanufacture, fabrication and transformation transactions.
- **MIB-13 / Economy:** price, trade, market and service-economy truth.
- **MIB-14 + PPIA-04/F014:** vehicle/platform/base/mecha/starship definitions, configuration/operation foundations and domain authority.
- **DPL / Profession:** Character engineering/mechanic capability, learning, profession and work/life truth.
- **APW/D26 Project/Time:** durable work projects, tasks, phases, participants, prerequisites, campaign time and cancellation.
- **MRCS:** reusable component/interface/compatibility/work-recipe/engineering-profile definitions.
- **GPR + Action/Event:** reusable gameplay execution, replay and authoritative operation receipts.
- **ARI + PCA:** resource identity, derivative provenance, rights, common version/review/import/export infrastructure.
- **PCA-12 + PDCP Packet 08:** generic simulation, formal validation, graph/constraint/optimization/Monte-Carlo workbench machinery.
- **PDCP Packet 07:** proposal/preview/dry-run/commit/explanation/intervention/compensation semantics.
- **PDCP Packet 04:** reusable semantic affordance/Effect-composition rules.
- **Reduced MBES:** built-environment facilities, workshops, garages, hangars, infrastructure and settlement context; MBES consumes MERA engineering rather than MERA rebuilding settlement systems.

## 3. Core state distinctions

MERA must preserve these distinct states:

1. reusable engineering Definition/profile;
2. live Asset identity and owner-domain state;
3. installed configuration relation;
4. observed symptom/evidence;
5. suspected or isolated fault;
6. identified fault/known cause where authorized;
7. proposed configuration/work plan;
8. preview/dry-run/analysis result;
9. authorized work order / owner-operation request;
10. committed owner receipts and resulting live state;
11. calibration/test/acceptance evidence;
12. blueprint/preset/reference plan;
13. historical Event/provenance/recovery record.

A preview is never a mutation. A successful repair transaction is not automatically an acceptance result. A blueprint is not an installed configuration. A hidden fault is not revealed merely because an engineering screen exists.

## 4. Engineering-resolution contract

The engineering ladder remains:

`Asset → system → assembly → subassembly → component → interface/connection → governed network`

Resolution may stop at the highest source-supported level. Lower-level structure is never invented to satisfy a UI, solver or diagnostic routine. Refining/collapsing presentation does not create a second Asset identity or erase state/history.

A solver or simulation is evidence about the declared model, not canonical Asset state. `UNKNOWN`, timeout and unsupported dimensions remain inconclusive.

### MERA-06 — Isolation, Access, Safe Disassembly & Recovery Handoff

Retains historical `MERA-06` and carries MERA-facing integration residuals from historical `MERA-17/18`.

### MERA-07 — Repair, Wear, Maintenance & Emergency Engineering

Absorbs historical `MERA-07 + MERA-08 + MERA-20` and carries engineering-facing recovery residuals from historical `MERA-17/18`.

Implements profile-driven repair-class orchestration, wear/reliability/service-state interpretation, maintenance operations, emergency patch/bypass/substitute/jury-rig workflows and resulting owner-operation requests.

There is no universal reliability, wear, repair-quality, jury-rig penalty or emergency-bonus formula.

### MERA-10 — Dependency, Network, Failure & Graceful-Degradation Runtime

Absorbs historical `MERA-10 + MERA-11`.

Implements engineering-domain system/network topology over accepted definitions: dependency, redundancy, alternate paths, cascading failure, isolation, graceful degradation and setting-defined power/fuel/heat/fluid/ammunition/data/control/magical interfaces.

Unknown topology stays unknown. Generic graph/constraint analysis is PCA-12; MERA supplies engineering model adapters and interpretation.

### MERA-12 — Item, Weapon, Armor, Tool & Equipment Engineering Adapter Pack

Retains historical `MERA-12`.

Implements engineering-domain adapters over PPIA-03/D17/MIB-12/MRCS for modular ordinary Items/equipment, without inventing a second Item, crafting or equipment-definition system.

### MERA-13 — Vehicle, Mecha & Starship Engineering Adapter Pack

Absorbs historical `MERA-13 + MERA-14 + MERA-15`.

PPIA-04 already defines Vehicle, Mecha and Starship as one governed experience family with shared identity/configuration/system/resource/damage/repair/provenance semantics. MERA implements one engineering adapter surface with target-specific profile packs rather than three independent engines.

Distinct vehicle/mecha/starship rules remain in their owner definitions. Same names or visual similarity never create compatibility.

### MERA-16 — Machinery, Robotics, Constructs & Industrial-System Engineering Adapter Pack

Retains historical `MERA-16`.

This remains distinct from ordinary Item engineering and the PPIA-04 Vehicle/Mecha/Starship domain because machinery/robotics/construct/industrial integrations may bind different owner types, autonomy/control semantics and facility/network contexts.

### MERA-24 — Golden Cross-Domain Engineering Proof & MBES Handoff

Retains historical `MERA-24` and absorbs proof obligations from all removed standalone tranches.

The golden gate proves the entire reduced family, including owner absorptions, accessibility, permissions, provider-off operation, recovery/idempotency, provenance, replay, analysis boundaries and reduced MBES consumption.

## 7. Operation lifecycle

All mutating engineering flows follow this shape:

1. authorize viewer/actor and target;
2. pin owner versions and known state;
3. build engineering proposal/work plan;
4. evaluate known compatibility, prerequisites and unresolved data;
5. optionally preview/analyze noncanonically;
6. reauthorize/revalidate immediately before commit;
7. submit typed owner operations to LSS/MIB-12/Asset/Project/etc.;
8. collect authoritative receipts;
9. refresh engineering projections;
10. perform required calibration/test/acceptance as a separate governed step;
11. append/retain Event/provenance/recovery evidence.

Lost-response retry must use owner operation-status/idempotency semantics rather than blindly repeating consumption/install/repair operations.

## 8. Unknown, conflict and failure semantics

- unknown is not zero, absent, compatible, safe, available or unlimited;
- source conflict remains conflict until its owner resolves it;
- missing topology blocks only operations that require that topology;
- unavailable diagnostic evidence does not reveal hidden truth through an error message;
- unsupported solver dimensions produce unsupported/inconclusive findings;
- incompatible configuration fails before irreversible owner operations;
- partial operation success is represented by actual owner receipts, never by a guessed all-or-nothing UI state;
- recovery/compensation never deletes accepted history.

## 9. Permissions and visibility

Filtering occurs before:

- system/component lists;
- counts and readiness summaries;
- compatibility matrices;
- diagnostic graphs;
- network/dependency graphs;
- analysis model extraction;
- exports;
- optional-AI context.

Hidden component/system/fault existence may not leak through counts, capacity deltas, incompatible-option errors, test summaries or solver metrics.

## 10. Accessibility and provider independence

Every blocking engineering workflow has a semantic/nonvisual path. Drag, graph, diagram, exploded-view, animation, color and 3D presentation are optional enhancements, not authority or sole controls.

All meaningful states expose text/semantic equivalents. No paid/cloud AI, solver, CAD, simulation or visualization provider is required for blocking MERA workflows.

## 11. Replay, migration and versioning

Engineering proposals, blueprints and analysis receipts pin relevant definition/owner versions. Reopen/replay reports stale/incompatible versions explicitly. Migration never rewrites historical Events or silently converts unresolved compatibility into accepted compatibility.

Committed owner operations are replayed from owner receipts/Event history; MERA does not maintain a competing mutation ledger.
