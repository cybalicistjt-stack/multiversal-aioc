# MNCS Benchmark Capability Matrix

**Program:** Multiversal NPC & Creature Studio (MNCS)  
**Scope:** fictional TTRPG NPC and creature generation/construction only.  
**Clean-room:** capability requirements may be learned from public product documentation and lawful ordinary use. Multiversal does not copy proprietary code, authored content libraries, distinctive UI, private protocols or vendor implementation details.

## Benchmarked capability classes

### UNE — Universal NPC Emulator
Useful lessons: very fast NPC direction, relationship/context prompts, demeanor/identity prompts, and partial reroll rather than discarding an entire NPC.

**MNCS target:** preserve fast modular generation while attaching results to persistent campaign identity and selective field locking.

### GameMaster's Apprentice
Useful lessons: dense at-table micro-prompts, goals/stakes, traits and other compact GM cues.

**MNCS target:** five-second playable roleplay cards first; deeper dossiers only when useful.

### Perchance / Ambitious NPC Generator
Useful lessons: modular random tables, large combinatorial generation, partial reroll and extensible generator construction.

**MNCS target:** retain modular weighted recipes while adding semantic eligibility and deterministic receipts where requested.

### World Wizard NPC Builder
Useful lessons: campaign-context generation, role-first construction, faction/location/quest links, persistent campaign storage and manual editing after generation.

**MNCS target:** canonical World/Campaign constraints are applied before randomness and accepted results hand directly to owning Multiversal systems.

### MonsterShuffler
Useful lessons: compositional creature/NPC construction, base-plus-template/role layering, derived mechanics, editable results, reusable components and sharing patterns.

**MNCS target:** species/form/stage/template/role/environment/equipment composition with owner-domain mechanics and provenance instead of one system-specific challenge model.

### Kassoon and similar fast TTRPG generators
Useful lessons: immediate table-ready names, personality, background, equipment and rules data.

**MNCS target:** retain one-click utility while making depth optional and refusing to invent unsupported mechanics merely for completeness.

### Foundry VTT ecosystem NPC generators
Useful lessons: generation directly into usable runtime records, compendium reuse instead of copied rule text, structured or natural-language prompting in some tools, and lightweight emergency-NPC workflows.

**MNCS target:** accepted candidates flow directly to Scene/Encounter/social owners while Definition, placement and live instance remain distinct.

### Structured monster/NPC builders
Examples include popular D20 and Pathfinder-oriented homebrew builders.

Useful lessons: clone-from-existing, structured actions/traits/resources, calculated fields, validation, import/export and publication workflows.

**MNCS target:** pair structured rules-valid mechanics with persona, ecology, behavior, relationships and campaign continuity.

### Settlement/social-ecosystem generators
Useful lessons: generate households, work, businesses, relationships and locations together rather than producing unrelated NPCs one at a time.

**MNCS target:** household, crew, organization and population generation are first-class workflows, with summarized members promotable into stable individual identities.

### Scene/group generators
Useful lessons: generate several NPCs in one current situation, including NPC-to-NPC and PC-related links.

**MNCS target:** group generation creates explicit governed relationships rather than disconnected prose.

### Progressive procedural NPC methods
Useful lessons: create a quick NPC now, deepen later, and let continued play matter more than initial generation.

**MNCS target:** progressive resolution is a core identity contract and authoritative Events change lived history.

### Owner-supplied `NPC Suite` reference
The exact product identity was not unambiguously resolved. Governance therefore records no vendor-specific capability claim for that label until an exact source is identified.

## Multiversal differentiators

MNCS is successful only if it materially exceeds a conventional generator through:

1. progressive identity from extra/population member to major recurring entity;
2. world/campaign/context-valid generation;
3. first-class knowledge, belief and misinformation;
4. persistent relationships and attributable reputation, including party association;
5. profession, household and ordinary-life integration;
6. creature ecology and behavior equal in importance to combat mechanics;
7. population/group resolution that individualizes only what becomes relevant;
8. mechanics-on-demand without identity replacement;
9. direct governed runtime and MAS handoff plus Event-driven continuity;
10. useful operation with optional AI disabled.
