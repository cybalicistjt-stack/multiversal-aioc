## Purpose

MNCS is the creator-facing TTRPG studio for constructing and progressively deepening fictional NPCs and creatures from existing Character/NPC/Creature, Species/Form, Profession/Life, Relationship/Reputation, World/Environment, mechanics, group/population and presentation authorities.

It must support everything from a five-second passing NPC or roadside animal through recurring NPCs, major antagonists, intelligent nonhumans, households, crews, herds, populations and ecosystem cohorts without replacing identity when detail changes.

MNCS does **not** implement real-person reproduction, avatar scanning, photoreal-person reconstruction or real-person simulation.

## Core doctrine

### One identity, progressive resolution

NPC/person resolution may move among:

`population member → passing extra → minor NPC → recurring NPC → full NPC → major Character-grade NPC`

Creature resolution may move among:

`population → group/herd/pack/swarm → encounter member → persistent individual → named/sapient/major entity`

Changing detail does not replace identity, erase accepted facts, duplicate population accounting or rewrite authoritative Event history.

### Fast first, deep when useful

The studio supports both instant improv and deep construction. A creator may accept, lock, selectively regenerate or leave unresolved individual field families. Regeneration reports conflicts rather than silently replacing accepted/locked facts.

### Context before randomness

Generation consumes only authorized Campaign/World/Location/Environment/Settlement/Faction/Culture/Species/Form/Profession/Encounter context. Unknown or unsupported context remains unresolved. A generation result is a candidate until accepted by the proper owner workflow.

### Provider-neutral blocking path

Seeded/table/weighted/constraint-based generation, selection, mechanics composition and validation work without paid/cloud AI. Optional AI receives visibility-safe context and returns candidate material only.

### Layer separation

Definition, generated candidate, presentation profile, Campaign/Scene placement, live instance and playable conversion remain distinct. PPIA-02 and canonical entity owners remain authoritative.

## Party-association reputation requirement

MIB-09 remains authoritative for relationship/reputation mutation. MNCS must expose Campaign-scoped party-association influence without inventing a universal shared-party score.

The studio preserves separately inspectable:

- direct reputation;
- association-derived influence;
- faction/group-specific weighting and conditions;
- initiating Event/actor/party/Campaign attribution;
- trend/history/provenance;
- visibility/reveal state.

Party-association effects cannot leak across Campaigns or become account-global reputation. Joining/leaving changes future association context but does not erase historical consequences unless the owning rule says so.
