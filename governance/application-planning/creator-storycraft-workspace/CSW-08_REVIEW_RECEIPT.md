## Findings

1. Clone/adapt/template/fork/remix/transform are provenance relationships, not authority inheritance.
2. Every derivative has a new stable identity/version and records exact source snapshots.
3. Source and derivative evolve independently; source edits never silently propagate.
4. Source changes produce advisory compare/manual-adapt/rebase-candidate workflows only.
5. Campaign/runtime extraction is reauthorized, privacy-filtered and either creator-controlled, proposal-required or prohibited according to owning-domain authority.
6. Cross-Campaign/World/Project reuse requires independent source/destination authorization and never transfers permissions.
7. Inherited references are retained, remapped, detached, unresolved or destination-incompatible explicitly; protected target details never leak through failed mapping.
8. Multi-generation lineage preserves immediate source edges and sufficient ancestry/provenance without implying endorsement or truth.
9. Templates/starter kits create independent instances; template updates affect future instances only unless an existing instance explicitly reviews updates.
10. Compatibility findings are evidence-backed advisory projections unless an owning-domain incorporation action genuinely requires compatibility.
11. CSW-07 prose transforms pin exact document/branch/revision/span and cannot mutate source history.
12. CSW-05 structure transforms remain pre-authoritative until the existing governed D28 handoff is used.
13. Optional AI may propose adaptations/variants/remaps but remains authorization-filtered and candidate-only.
14. Non-AI cloning, mapping, diffs, templates and compatibility workflows remain useful.
15. Stable operation IDs, expected versions and stale-source handling make derivative creation/application recoverable and idempotent.
16. Mobile, keyboard and screen-reader users have complete list/text alternatives to graphs, drag/drop and side-by-side layouts.
