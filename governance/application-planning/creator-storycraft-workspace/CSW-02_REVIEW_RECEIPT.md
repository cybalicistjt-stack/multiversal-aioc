5. Project Memory graph/backlinks derive from explicit stable relationships, governed references and incorporation receipts; similarity can only propose candidates.
6. History references target-domain provenance/Event/version evidence rather than rewriting governed history as CSW truth.
7. Authorization/visibility filtering occurs before search counts facets rankings snippets pagination similarity graph topology degree/cardinality timeline aggregation and AI context.
8. Saved views store query/layout preferences, never authorization grants or stale result sets.
9. Duplicate/near-duplicate discovery is advisory and never silently merges identities or governed objects.
10. Open/unresolved/unused/dormant projections are attention candidates from the authorized graph, not correctness judgments.
11. Archive/rediscovery preserves references and cannot resurrect revoked Campaign-private material into Personal results.
12. Import/export preserves provenance/ID mapping without laundering imported material into authoritative target-domain content.
13. Recovery rebuilds projections from durable fragment/reference/provenance state using version/idempotency/status semantics; projections themselves are not truth.
14. Core Library/Story Bible organization remains useful without AI; AI suggestions remain advisory.
