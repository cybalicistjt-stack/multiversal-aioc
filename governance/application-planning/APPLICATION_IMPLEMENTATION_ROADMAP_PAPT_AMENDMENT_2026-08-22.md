## Purpose

Record the owner-approved Pixel Asset Production Toolkit (`PAPT`) as a governed parallel studio-tooling program without changing the currently selected application critical-path tranche or weakening existing authority boundaries.

## Critical-path preservation

This amendment does **not** change the base roadmap's selected current item:

`RSR-06 — LNG Language/Culture/Script Reconciliation`

The base critical-path forward order remains unchanged unless a later owner-approved roadmap revision changes it.

PAPT is a parallel preparation/tooling track. It may be selected independently when the owner chooses to execute it, but planning PAPT does not start it, does not pause RSR/MSS, and does not mark any other tranche complete or superseded.

## New program

Program: `governance/application-planning/pixel-asset-production-toolkit/PAPT_PIXEL_ASSET_PRODUCTION_TOOLKIT_PROGRAM.md`  
Backlog: `governance/application-planning/pixel-asset-production-toolkit/PAPT_PROGRAM_BACKLOG.json`

Program purpose: extend the completed Character Appearance Production Preparation (`CAPP`) pixel-art production architecture into a governed internal studio toolchain for character, creature, item/equipment/material, icon, environment/prop/cozy, tile/map/marker, sprite/animation, QA, derivative export, regeneration and review workflows.

## Authority and style rule

- CAPP remains Character Appearance Customizer/appearance-production authority and is `completed_verified` through CAPP-12.
- PAPT consumes CAPP; it does not replace or reopen CAPP.
- Pixel art is the current production-content asset language.
- Existing Multiversal application visual-language/design-token/accessibility authority governs UI/chrome and the broader cross-surface design system.
- Typography may retain higher-definition editorial/brand treatment; PAPT does not force interface typography or corporate/media surfaces into pixel resolution.
- AI-assisted/generated assets must remain provenance-bound and may not be represented as handcrafted/no-AI work when they are not.
- Generated assets do not create canon, mechanics, anatomy, ownership or source facts.

## Dependency-optimized PAPT order

Foundation:

`PAPT-01 → (PAPT-02 || PAPT-03)`

After the shared-foundation gate, the following may proceed in parallel where their owning source registries are ready:

`PAPT-04 || PAPT-05 || PAPT-06 || PAPT-07 || PAPT-08 || PAPT-09`

Then:

- `PAPT-10` sprite/animation assembly follows representative character/creature assets.
- `PAPT-11` fit/layer/anchor/occlusion QA follows character + equipment fixtures.
- `PAPT-12` portrait/token/derivative export follows character/creature assemblies and provenance.
- `PAPT-13` batch QA/contact-sheet/style lint begins after shared foundations and grows alongside the asset-family fan-out.
- `PAPT-14` production queue/regeneration follows provenance + QA + at least one operating forge.
- `PAPT-15` human review/correction/canon-commercial promotion follows provenance + QA and may mature in parallel with late asset-family work.
- `PAPT-16` integrated studio factory is the final program gate.

## NCG relationship

NCG's corporate/studio/franchise roadmap may schedule, fund and commercially use PAPT outputs, but GitHub/AIOC remains technical authority for PAPT. Commercial use remains subject to NCG rights/provenance, franchise/canon, public-claim, release and owner-approval gates.
