## Why this placement

PCA produces reusable local-first production recipes, procedural/spatial generator primitives, texture/material helpers and style-locked production infrastructure. MCS consumes those foundations to provide the actual map-creation product surface: world generation, artistic cartography, dungeon/tactical construction, precision finishing, semantic binding, structured export and atlas publishing.

SMB-08 and SMB-09 are the first large content/campaign consumers, so map-authoring capability should exist before they begin at scale.
