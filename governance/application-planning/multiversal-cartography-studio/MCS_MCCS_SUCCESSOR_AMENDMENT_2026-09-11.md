## Decision

The strict future successor after `MCS-21` becomes `MCCS-01` rather than `SMB-08`.

Effective segment:

`PCA-01..16 → MCS-01..21 → MCCS-01..21 → SMB-08`

This amendment supersedes the direct MCS-program/backlog successor statement that previously routed `MCS-21 → SMB-08`. MCS scope and its 21 tranches are otherwise unchanged.

## Dependency rationale

MCS and MCCS are sibling creator-facing interstitials consuming common ARI/PCA foundations but owning different product surfaces:

- MCS owns cartography/map-specific creation and semantic binding.
- MCCS owns Character/creature appearance creation, topology-aware customization and cross-output presentation.
- Neither becomes canonical owner-domain truth.
- SMB-08 receives both creator studios before first-party library production scales.

## Boundary

This does not start MCS or MCCS, modify the current work pointer, change the sealed ARI sequence, activate P3D, or authorize provider spend/release activity.
