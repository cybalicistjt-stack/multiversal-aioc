## Why this placement

PCA supplies reusable production recipes, character asset/rig/garment/LOD tooling, motion/facial helpers, style-locked generation and review primitives. MCS then supplies the governed map/cartography studio. MCCS follows those shared foundations and provides the entity-centric creator surface over completed CAPP/PPIA appearance authority plus PAPT/PCA/ARI capabilities.

SMB-08 and SMB-09 are major first-party library/campaign consumers. Character/creature visual authoring should exist before those programs scale content so they can produce and reuse portraits, tokens, sprites, creature variants and reference assets through one governed workflow rather than one-off pipelines.

SAA remains after SMB-09 and can consume consistent governed character/creature presentation assets.
