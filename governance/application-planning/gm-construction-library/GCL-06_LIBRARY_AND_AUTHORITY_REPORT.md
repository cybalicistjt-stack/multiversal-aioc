# GCL-06 Library and Authority Report

## Scope

GCL-06 builds reusable complications, escalations, reversals and twists from color/inconvenience through crisis. It also provides explicit derailment-recovery structures so unexpected player choices can redirect prepared material without being treated as errors to correct.

## Production breadth

- 340 templates.
- 17 complication families, 20 each.
- Five severity bands: color, inconvenience, pressure, setback, crisis.
- Five reusable forms represented throughout: complication, escalation, reversal, twist, recovery.
- Dedicated `derailment_recovery` family: 20 templates.
- GCL-18 starter-proof requirement: at least 100 complications/escalations; this tranche supplies 340.
- Deterministic dictionary-columnar archive.
- Archive SHA-256: `0448f8c1d5f9def4a4e252cb3d777f9541d274b35dc6c3fc73738392ff0c13d0`.

Families: color_signal, nuisance_inconvenience, resource_complication, time_complication, access_route_shift, environmental_change, opposition_change, third_party_arrival, information_reveal, assumption_reversal, relationship_shift, authority_intervention, objective_reframe, advantage_reversal, consequence_echo, cascading_crisis, derailment_recovery.

## Per-record structure

Every record exposes at least two trigger prompts; one or more escalation vectors; one or more reversal prompts; at least two response openings; at least two continuation vectors; one or more derailment-recovery prompts; one or more opportunity openings; controlled parameter slots; discovery and composition metadata; and explicit no-forced-outcome, no-mandatory-choice, no-live-state-mutation and no-guaranteed-consequence flags.

## Severity doctrine

`severity_band` is a narrative/operational scope label, not Challenge Rating and not a universal difficulty scalar. A crisis template may be large in fictional scope while still leaving multiple routes, retreats, compromises or partial objectives. Color templates may remain purely expressive.

## Player-agency and derailment recovery

The recovery contract treats divergence as input. Compatible prepared actors, locations, facts, clues or pressures may be rebound or deferred, but the GM-assistance layer may not secretly restore an abandoned branch, negate a player's meaningful choice, fabricate a mandatory correction, or close all alternate continuations.

## Information and twist fairness

`information_reveal` and `assumption_reversal` operate only on authorized evidence, established facts and explicitly unresolved unknowns. A twist cannot grant hidden-information access or retroactively rewrite established facts merely to surprise the players.

## Authority boundaries

- MV-IA-F005 and related Campaign/Scene/Session domains own live placement, reveal state and accepted history.
- MV-IA-F012 owns actual Encounter construction, participant/objective bindings, analysis and approval.
- PPIA-11/GCL-07 own difficulty/pressure methodology and shaping; GCL-06 pressure references are descriptive only.
- GCL-14 owns rewards, aftermath and consequence libraries; `consequence_echo` cannot mutate world state.
- GCL-06 has no runtime or canon authority.
- AI remains optional, proposal-only and authorization-filtered.

## Downstream readiness

Completing GCL-06 satisfies the remaining reusable-library dependency for GCL-07. GCL-09, GCL-10 and GCL-14 also gain their GCL-06 prerequisite once this tranche is completed_verified, subject to their other dependencies.
