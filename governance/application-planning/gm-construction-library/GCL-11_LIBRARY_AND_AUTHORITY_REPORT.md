# GCL-11 — Session Construction Kits: Library & Authority Report

## Result

GCL-11 materializes **144 deterministic session-construction kits** from 18 session families × 8 construction patterns. The library is deliberately parametric: breadth comes from governed composition rather than copied prose.

### Session families

Opening/kickoff; middle/momentum; finale/resolution; one-shot; short session; convention slot; hiatus return; absent player; extra player; split party; downtime-heavy; investigation-heavy; social-heavy; exploration-heavy; combat-heavy; travel-heavy; recovery/breathing-room; mixed-mode.

### Construction patterns

Cold-open arc; three-phase; four-phase; modular blocks; countdown window; rotating spotlight; parallel threads; recap-to-cliffhanger.

## What the kits provide

Every materialized kit exposes:

- session-scale construction goal and question shape;
- eight segment roles and eight replaceable component slots;
- timing-window options;
- attendance-adaptation prompts;
- explicit spotlight guidance;
- recovery/replanning modes when time or player direction changes;
- closure/handoff modes including partial, fail-forward, unresolved and early-stop endings;
- ready-to-use and construction-material projections;
- provenance and composition targets.

## MV-IA-F005 boundary

GCL-11 is **pre-authoritative preparation material**. MV-IA-F005 remains authoritative for Campaign, Scene and Session identities/state; membership and roles; Character-control grants; launch snapshots; Session launch/pause/resume/close; Event history; and authorization-filtered projections.

Therefore GCL-11 may not:

- create or mutate live Session state;
- create a launch snapshot or launch a Session;
- grant membership, role, observer access or Character control;
- mutate Scene/Campaign truth;
- append authoritative Events;
- reveal hidden information or infer protected counts;
- assert that a planned outcome, recap statement, clue, choice, clock or branch occurred in play.

Attendance variants treat absence, extra participants, late arrival, early departure, split groups and observers as **planning conditions only**. Actual eligibility and control remain F005 decisions.

## Other boundaries

- GCL-10 Adventure structures remain reusable proposal inputs, not silently incorporated Adventure truth.
- MV-IA-F012 owns real Encounter composition/analysis/live Encounter state.
- CSW-05 remains pre-authoritative narrative planning.
- A9 retains runtime clue/discovery/evidence/hypothesis/hidden-information authority for investigation-heavy sessions.
- APM-04 may consume kits only through explicit adapters and authorized context; GCL-11 never directs runtime AutoGM behavior.

## AI

AI is optional and proposal-only. It may suggest compatible kits, fill open slots, compress/expand a plan or propose alternate closures from authorized context. It may not generate an authoritative recap, infer hidden state, mutate Campaign/Scene/Session truth, grant participation/control, or launch play.

## Application non-interference

GCL-11 is a parallel governance/content tranche and does not alter `CURRENT_WORK_POINTER.json`, SEC implementation authority, application code, release/deployment state, or any parked/deferred application work.
