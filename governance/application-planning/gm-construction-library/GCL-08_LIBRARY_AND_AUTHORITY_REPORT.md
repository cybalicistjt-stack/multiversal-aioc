# GCL-08 — Creature & Adversary Scaling/Role Kits

## Result

GCL-08 materializes **120 deterministic role/transformation kits**: 20 adversary role families × six transformation intents. This exceeds the GCL-18 adversary transformation/scaling proof minimum of 50.

## Scaling doctrine

“Scaling” does not mean inventing a stronger stat block. GCL-08 first changes how an already-governed actor is assigned, positioned, timed, composed, telegraphed, paired, or connected to an objective. Numeric changes are allowed only when the owning source explicitly authorizes the exact parameter and range.

If the source does not contain the capability required by a role kit, the result is **not applicable**. The library must never synthesize a replacement power, action, reaction, immunity, resistance, weakness, phase, resource, statistic, or lore fact merely to satisfy a requested difficulty or role.

## Role families

Frontliner, defender, skirmisher, artillery, controller, support, ambusher, pursuer, harrier, sniper, disruptor, reinforcer, leader, bodyguard, objective guardian, hazard operator, scout, infiltrator, solo anchor, and partner pair.

## Transformation intents

- emphasize existing strength
- expose counterplay
- shift positioning
- shift timing/cadence
- redistribute composition
- soften failure/exit pressure

Every kit binds provenance, identifies relevant GCL-07 pressure dimensions, preserves counterplay/failure paths where applicable, is AI-optional and proposal-only, and leaves MV-IA-F012/PPIA-11 Encounter authority intact.
