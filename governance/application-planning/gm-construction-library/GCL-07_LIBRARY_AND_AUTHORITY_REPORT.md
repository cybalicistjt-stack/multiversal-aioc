# GCL-07 — Encounter Pressure & Difficulty-Shaping Library

## Result

GCL-07 materializes 144 deterministic advisory templates across all 12 PPIA-11 pressure dimensions. Each dimension receives 12 patterns: three that reduce pressure, three that increase pressure, three that make failure more survivable or recoverable, and three that reshape pressure instead of simply moving it up or down.

## Core doctrine

- No universal Challenge Rating, encounter level, threat score, weighted sum, or cross-domain power scalar.
- No claim that an encounter is balanced, fair, safe, winnable, optimal, or guaranteed.
- Every proposal names the affected pressure dimension and an explicit delta.
- Unknown or contradictory inputs remain unknown; `indeterminate` is a valid uncertainty result.
- Changes must expose material tradeoffs into other pressure dimensions instead of hiding them.
- GCL-07 may recommend changes to governed composition, timing/waves, environment/hazards, objectives/failure paths, encounter-local resources, or revealed information only where owning rules permit.
- GCL-07 does not invent creature abilities, boss phases, source mechanics, or canon. GCL-08 owns reusable adversary transformation kits.
- MV-IA-F012 retains Encounter composition, validation, comparison, simulation, approval, immutable snapshot and Scene-attachment authority.

## Coverage

12 pressure dimensions × 4 transformation intents × 3 pattern families = **144 records**.

The four intent families are `ease_pressure`, `increase_pressure`, `make_failure_tolerant`, and `reshape_pressure`. Every record is genre-neutral, deterministic/manual-capable, AI-optional, proposal-only, nonmutating and source-truth-preserving.
