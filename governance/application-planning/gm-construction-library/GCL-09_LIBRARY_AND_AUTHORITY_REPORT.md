# GCL-09 — Mystery, Investigation & Clue Construction Kits

**Work item:** GCL-09  
**Program:** GCL — GM Construction Library  
**Status:** IN PROGRESS — CANDIDATE LIBRARY BUILT  
**Consumes:** GCL-02/03/05/06, CSW-05, A9  
**Application implementation authority:** NONE

## Decision

GCL-09 supplies reusable pre-authoritative mystery and investigation construction kits. It does not create runtime clues, discover information, resolve hypotheses, assert objective truth, or expose protected clue existence.

The production library materializes **160 kits** from **20 mystery families × 8 construction patterns**.

### Mystery families

hidden identity; missing person; hidden location; missing object; concealed motive; secret method; timeline reconstruction; sabotage/tampering; infiltration/imposture; conspiracy network; corruption/abuse; betrayal/double-dealing; hidden history; false appearance; anomalous event; pattern/series; contested account; causal chain; locked access; layered mystery.

### Construction patterns

1. redundancy web;
2. orthogonal sources;
3. alternate route;
4. prerequisite gate;
5. misleading-but-fair evidence;
6. partial signal;
7. failure-resilient continuation;
8. hypothesis discriminator.

## CSW-05 boundary

GCL-09 composes with CSW-05 planned revelations, possible clue/source fragments, prerequisite knowledge, clue encounter routes, redundancy/alternate paths, misleading possibilities, and learning/not-learning consequences. Those relationships remain design intent only. GCL-09 cannot create D28/A5 identities or runtime state and cannot silently propagate into an Adventure, Campaign, Scene or Session.

## A9 boundary

A9 retains runtime authority for clue/discovery state and the separation of objective truth, observation, claim, evidence reference, hypothesis, conclusion and Player knowledge. GCL-09 templates preserve that vocabulary but do not instantiate those runtime records.

Protected clue/node/edge/source existence, cardinality, topology, timing and rank must be filtered before counts, search, layout, grouping, export, diagnostics, notification, microcopy, autocomplete, recommendation or AI context. Absence of visible evidence is not proof of protected absence.

## Investigation resilience

Failure-resistant design does not mean automatic success. A failed check/source/route can alter cost, timing, certainty or route, expose a different question, or leave a safe visible next option. It cannot silently hand over the hidden answer or force the intended solution.

No universal clue count is defined. A creator may intentionally design robust redundancy or a fragile single-source structure; the system may surface the risk but cannot claim a fixed number is sufficient proof.

## Fair misdirection

Misleading evidence is allowed only as a design pattern with discoverable limits, counter-signals, provenance weaknesses or alternate explanations. Deception may alter claims or beliefs but never rewrites objective truth.

## AI boundary

AI is optional and proposal-only. It may suggest candidate source types, alternate routes, fair counter-signals, hypothesis discriminators or failure paths from authorized context. It may not solve the mystery as authority, reveal protected facts, mutate runtime clue state, mark a hypothesis true, or bypass permission filtering.
