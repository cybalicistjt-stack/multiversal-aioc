# GCL-05 Objective, Stakes & Outcomes Library Authority Report

**Work item:** GCL-05 — Objectives, Stakes, Outcomes & Victory Conditions  
**Attempt:** GCL-05-attempt-001  
**Classification:** Governed reusable GM construction content  
**Runtime/canon authority:** none

## Result target

GCL-05 supplies **240 parameterized objective templates** across twelve families, twenty each: acquire/retrieve; protect/escort; escape/evade; survive/endure; reach/traverse; investigate/reveal; negotiate/secure agreement; disrupt/sabotage; control/hold; race/deadline; repair/restore/transform; and competing/multi-objective.

Every record makes resolution structure explicit instead of reducing victory to defeating opposition. Each record includes at least two success-definition prompts, two partial-success states, two failure/fail-forward states, two stakes prompts, one explicit time-condition prompt, two non-defeat outcomes, and one competing-priority prompt.

## Authority boundaries

A GCL-05 record is reusable framing, not live objective state. It cannot declare that an objective exists in a Campaign/Scene/Encounter, record completion or failure, predict or canonize an outcome, force a method, grant a reward, mutate aftermath/world state, or tune difficulty.

MV-IA-F012 owns Encounter-local objective binding, validation, analysis, approval and attachment. MV-IA-F005 and related Campaign/Scene/Session authorities own accepted live state/history. GCL-07 owns later difficulty/pressure shaping. GCL-14 owns rewards, aftermath and consequence libraries.

## Outcome doctrine

Full success, partial success, failure/fail-forward and non-defeat outcomes remain separate candidate states. Failure may open a new objective or changed situation, but GCL-05 does not guarantee recovery. Competing objectives remain independently legible rather than being collapsed into one hidden score.

Time conditions are also explicit. A record may ask whether a real clock exists and what event defines it, but it may not silently manufacture urgency or an automatic countdown.

## Deterministic library

Production content is stored in six deterministic dictionary-columnar JSON shards inside a deterministic tar.gz archive. All record values are explicit dictionary selections; compression is storage-only. The manifest binds the archive with SHA-256. Materialization into the GCL-01 grammar is deterministic and has no hidden defaults.

## AI

Zero AI is required. Optional AI may propose compatible wording, variants or open-slot bindings from authorized context; it cannot establish live success/failure, award consequences, invent rules, or publish/promote automatically.

## Validation

Repository health must verify exact 240-record and 12-family breadth; unique stable IDs and family prefixes; declared slot placeholders; all outcome/stakes/time/non-defeat/competing minimums; deterministic archive digest and reconstruction; genre-neutral structural strategy; live-objective, difficulty and aftermath nonauthority; and preservation of the application selector.
