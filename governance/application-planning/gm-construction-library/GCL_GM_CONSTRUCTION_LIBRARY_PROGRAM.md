# GCL — GM Construction Library Program

**Program ID:** GCL  
**Name:** GM Construction Library  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED — PLANNED PARALLEL PROGRAM  
**Owner/final authority:** John Brandon Turner  
**Approved:** 2026-08-23

## 1. Purpose

GCL builds the reusable creative substrate that lets Multiversal help a Game Master construct almost anything without forcing the GM to begin from a blank page or hunt through giant undifferentiated lists.

The program creates a large, parameterized library of hooks, situations, encounter structures, objectives, stakes, complications, difficulty-shaping levers, adversary transformations, mysteries, adventure/session/campaign architectures, NPC dramatic roles, rewards/consequences, genre transformations, discovery metadata and composition rules.

GCL is **not another Campaign Builder, Encounter Builder, Plot Lab, AutoGM or balance authority**. It supplies reusable construction material and advisory intelligence to the existing owning systems.

Controlling principle:

> **Keep the internal library broad and deep; keep the GM-facing experience intent-first, fast, remixable and easy to understand.**

- `CSW-05` Plot, Adventure and Narrative Design Lab;
- `MV-IA-F005` Campaign, Scene and Session Builder;
- `MV-IA-F012` Encounter Builder and Balance Lab;

- `APM-03` AutoGM Single Encounter Runner;

GCL does not create runtime Campaign truth, Scene truth, live Encounter state, canon, ownership, universal balance scores or hidden-information access merely by supplying a template.

## 3. Core product rules

### 3.1 No blank-page requirement

A GM should be able to begin from a need such as:

- start something;
- continue something;
- fill a missing piece;
- complicate;
- escalate;
- make easier;
- make harder;
- recover from an unexpected player choice;
- generate alternatives;
- create a complete bounded structure;
- remix an existing structure.

### 3.2 Intent-first discovery, not list dumping

The internal library may become very large. Normal GM interaction must not require scrolling a giant catalog.

Primary discovery dimensions include:

- what the GM is making: encounter, scene, quest/adventure, session, campaign, NPC situation, location situation or other supported construction;
- desired experience/tone;
- play emphasis;
- duration/scale;
- preparation depth;
- current need: inspiration, complete template, missing part, complication, escalation, easier, harder, alternative route or surprise;
- compatible World/Campaign/rules/source context where authorized.

Search, tags, filters and expert browsing remain available as secondary tools.

### 3.3 Ready-to-use and construction-material modes

Every mature reusable template should support two complementary projections:

1. **Ready to use** — enough bounded detail for rapid GM use.
2. **Construction material** — exposes replaceable parts so the GM can remix objectives, pressures, actors, clues, complications, consequences, genre expression and other compatible elements.

### 3.4 Parameterized breadth over duplicated prose

Prefer reusable structural grammars with controlled slots, constraints and transformation rules over thousands of nearly identical prose entries.

The same structural pattern may safely support many genres when the genre transformation changes expression rather than silently changing governing mechanics or canon.

### 3.5 Human authority and provenance

Generated or recommended material remains a proposal until the owning authoring/runtime domain accepts it. Every library record and derived proposal must preserve source/provenance and distinguish:

- source-backed fact;
- authored reusable template;
- GM-authored customization;
- AI-generated candidate;
- campaign-local adaptation;
- canonical/promoted content.

## 4. Reusable construction grammar

GCL-01 will formalize the schema, but the baseline reusable template may include:

- purpose/intended experience;
- applicable genres and tones;
- play modes;
- scale/duration;
- participant assumptions;
- prerequisites and required roles;
- required governed-object categories;
- optional substitutions;
- setup and trigger;
- objective and stakes;
- opposition/pressure;
- information state;
- escalation structure;
- complications;
- resolutions;
- partial-success and fail-forward paths;
- rewards, consequences and aftermath;
- difficulty/pressure levers;
- composition hooks;
- compatibility requirements;
- provenance and uncertainty;
- discovery metadata;
- AI/generation suitability;
- ready-to-use projection data;
- construction-material projection data.

A template does not need every field. Requiredness is template-family-specific.

## 5. Difficulty and scaling doctrine

GCL must not introduce a universal Challenge Rating or pretend one number proves difficulty, fairness, safety or winnability.

Difficulty shaping operates through independently visible pressure dimensions and source-backed transformations. Candidate levers include:

- opposition quantity/composition;
- action economy;
- survivability/recovery;
- output pressure;
- resources;
- terrain/environment;
- range/mobility/access;
- time pressure;
- objective pressure;
- information/uncertainty;
- hazards/external pressure;
- waves/reinforcements/escalation;
- retreat/bypass/negotiation alternatives;
- consequence stakes;
- mixed-scale interactions where governing rules exist.

Every recommendation inherits PPIA-11/F012 uncertainty, provenance and no-guarantee rules.

## 6. Adversary transformation doctrine

Creature/adversary scaling should prefer meaningful role/capability transformations over raw stat multiplication.

Representative transformation families include:

- weakened, injured, juvenile, inexperienced;
- standard, veteran, elite, champion, commander;
- minion, swarm, pack hunter;
- ambusher, defender, controller, skirmisher, artillery, support, disruptor, summoner, berserker, elusive;
- solo/boss conversion;
- environmentally empowered, corrupted, desperate or enraged variants where the governing source permits them.

A transformation may only change dimensions permitted by its owning mechanics and source context. GCL never fabricates new canon anatomy, powers or boss-only rules simply to hit a desired difficulty.

## 7. Program roadmap

### GCL-01 — Construction Taxonomy & Reusable Template Grammar
Define template families, shared metadata, compatibility, slot semantics, provenance, dual ready-to-use/construction-material projections, composition contracts and import/export shape.

### GCL-02 — Hook, Premise & Inciting-Situation Library
Build parameterized reasons for engagement, disruption, obligation, discovery, conflict and opportunity across genres without hard-coding one solution.

### GCL-03 — Situation & Scene Template Library
Build playable social, environmental, logistical, ceremonial, emergency, exploratory and mixed situations with actors, motives, pressures, opportunities and escalation paths.

### GCL-04 — Encounter Archetype Library
Build structural combat, social, investigation, exploration, stealth, chase, survival, hazard, puzzle/problem, travel, political and hybrid encounter archetypes.

### GCL-05 — Objectives, Stakes, Outcomes & Victory Conditions
Build reusable objectives, partial-success states, fail-forward states, competing objectives, time conditions and non-defeat outcomes.

### GCL-06 — Complication, Escalation, Reversal & Twist Library
Build controlled complication families from color/inconvenience through crisis, with compatibility and derailment-recovery use.

### GCL-07 — Encounter Pressure & Difficulty-Shaping Library
Translate PPIA-11/F012 factors into GM-facing advisory pressure levers, safer/easier/harder transformations, alternative difficulty shapes and uncertainty-aware recommendations.

### GCL-08 — Creature & Adversary Scaling/Role Kits
Build source-respecting adversary role transformations, solo/boss conversion checks, group-composition patterns and strength-reduction patterns without universal stat multipliers.

### GCL-09 — Mystery, Investigation & Clue Construction Kits
Build mystery archetypes, revelation/clue redundancy structures, misleading-but-fair evidence patterns, clue-source alternatives, information gates and failure-resistant investigation structures compatible with CSW-05/A9 boundaries.

### GCL-10 — Adventure Structure Library
Build reusable architectures such as heists, rescues, expeditions, sieges, mysteries, faction sandboxes, survival journeys, hunts, political crises, point crawls, branching dilemmas and other modular adventure skeletons.

### GCL-11 — Session Construction Kits
Build rapid session structures for openings, middles, finales, one-shots, short sessions, convention play, hiatus recovery, absent/extra players, split parties, downtime-heavy and play-mode-emphasis variants.

### GCL-12 — Campaign Architecture Library
Build campaign structures including episodic, serial, sandbox, faction/villain/exploration/mystery/political/military/survival/settlement/mercantile/academy/resistance/dynasty/generational/time-travel/multiversal/cozy/rotating-cast/asynchronous/anthology patterns.

### GCL-13 — NPC Dramatic Roles & Relationship Situations
Build reusable patron, rival, witness, suspect, gatekeeper, informant, protector, betrayer, authority, opportunist and other dramatic roles plus debts, secrets, conflicting obligations, grudges, trust and relationship-pressure situations.

### GCL-14 — Rewards, Aftermath & Consequence Library
Build non-item rewards and world-state consequences including information, access, favor, reputation, relationships, sanctuary, authority, opportunity, obligations, faction response, market/ecological/legal/social change and enemy adaptation.

### GCL-15 — Genre, Tone & Reskinning Grammar
Define transformations that safely express one structural template across fantasy, science fiction, horror, western, superhero, cyberpunk, cozy, historical, modern, multiversal and other supported genres without changing governing mechanics silently.

### GCL-16 — GM Discovery & Recommendation Layer
Define the intent-first browse/search/recommendation facets, compatibility filtering, quick-pick paths, expert browsing and explanation requirements that keep a very large internal library easy to access.

### GCL-17 — Composition Engine Design
Define deterministic/manual composition of compatible hooks + situations + objectives + actors/pressures + environments + complications + revelations + consequences, with replace-one-part/remix workflows and optional AI proposal boundaries.

### GCL-18 — Curated Starter Library & Proof Packs
Assemble and validate representative breadth packs large enough to prove the grammar, discovery model, composition contracts and downstream integrations before uncontrolled expansion.

## 10. Downstream integration contract

GCL outputs are intended to feed:

- CSW inspiration and guided authoring;
- Plot/Adventure/Narrative Lab seed and structure workflows;
- Campaign/Scene/Session preparation;
- Encounter Builder composition and advisory comparison;
- AutoGM encounter/campaign planning;
- future ISE/WCI assistance;
- GM Content Library browsing and search;
- optional AI assistance from authorized context.

Integration requires explicit adapters/contracts. No consumer may silently reinterpret a GCL template as live or canonical state.

## 11. AI boundary

The deterministic/manual GCL library must remain useful without AI.

Optional AI may:

- recommend compatible templates;
- fill explicitly open slots;
- generate variants;
- reskin expression;
- summarize alternatives;
- propose complications or consequences;
- explain why a pressure change may matter.

AI may not:

- mutate governed Campaign/Adventure/Encounter truth without explicit user action;
- hide provenance;
- claim objective balance or quality;
- invent missing governing mechanics as fact;
- access unauthorized private context;
- publish/promote generated content automatically.

## 12. Non-interference and activation rule

GCL is a parallel planning/content program. Its approval does **not** change the current application implementation selector, pause MSS or other critical-path programs, or grant implementation authority for application code.

`GCL-01` is the program's first work item and is **owner-approved ready to start**, but it is not started merely by this roadmap addition.

To avoid ambiguity with the application's canonical `Continue` behavior, begin GCL execution only when the owner explicitly selects GCL in context, for example `Continue GCL`, `Start GCL`, or equivalent unambiguous direction.

## 13. Completion rule

A GCL tranche is complete only when its declared governed artifacts, checks and review evidence exist. Library quantity alone is not completion: templates must satisfy the shared grammar, provenance, compatibility, authority and discoverability rules applicable to that tranche.
