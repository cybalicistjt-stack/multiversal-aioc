## Execution relationship

ENV-01..16 and CEW-01..16 are governed content/recovery/design tranches. They can advance in this project conversation while the separate software-development conversation advances the active application roadmap.

ENV should lead through the composition model and enough Habitat Signature vocabulary for CEW to consume. CEW source census/type/classification work may begin before ENV completes, but CEW must not freeze habitat semantics incompatible with ENV-15.

Neither program grants authority to mutate `Multiversal-app`. A future explicitly selected integration tranche must consume their finalized artifacts rather than redesigning them inside the application.

## Locked creature-system seams

Havalaea native-born animal lineages descended from the Time of Troubles remain distinct from later imported animals. Where owner/source authority establishes human-level intelligence/personhood, those animals retain their animal ecological identity and are eligible for existing Creature/NPC-system presentation.

Mount, pet/companion and familiar are pathway/relationship capabilities rather than base creature types. Existing CCP authorities remain controlling, including CCP-06 for mounts/work/travel and CCP-07 for familiars/supernatural bonds. Sapient/person-level creatures retain autonomy and consent requirements.
