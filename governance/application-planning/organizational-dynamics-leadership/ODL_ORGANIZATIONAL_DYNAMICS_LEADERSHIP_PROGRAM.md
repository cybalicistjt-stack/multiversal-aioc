# ODL — Organizational Dynamics & Leadership

**Program ID:** ODL  
**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED — PLANNED INTERSTITIAL  
**Activation:** after KFR-08  
**Successor:** SCL-01  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner  
**Approved:** 2026-08-27

## Purpose

ODL turns the recovered mine-note doctrine that social power has carrying cost into governed organization dynamics. Followers, staff, dependents, crews, businesses, bases, factions and other groups may create coordination, loyalty, succession, administrative and factional pressures as they grow, without imposing one universal organization-size cap or duplicating existing Organization truth.

ODL consumes existing Organization/Faction/Relationship/Reputation, Economy, Base/Vehicle, Project, DPL and WCI authorities and prepares reusable leadership/load semantics for SCL command play.

Recovered basis: RDC-04 / RD-05. The owner has explicitly directed that suitable recovered mine-note families be added to the roadmap.

## Tranches

1. **ODL-01 — Authority Crosswalk & Organization/Dependent Taxonomy**  
   Reconcile followers, staff, hirelings, dependents, crews, businesses, organizations, factions, settlements, households and command structures with their existing owners; define which concepts are projections/relations rather than new ledgers.

2. **ODL-02 — Capacity, Coordination & Load Dimensions**  
   Model profile-driven coordination span, communication burden, administrative load, dependency, autonomy, geographic spread and organizational complexity. No universal follower cap or single organization score is created.

3. **ODL-03 — Loyalty, Cohesion, Factionalism & Internal Politics**  
   Connect Relationships/Reputation and organization state to loyalty, trust, blocs, rivalries, competing goals, internal factions, morale and legitimacy while preserving hidden information and explicit event provenance.

4. **ODL-04 — Delegation, Roles, Authority, Communication & Succession**  
   Represent delegated roles, responsibility, authority boundaries, communication paths, succession, temporary command and vacancies without granting permissions merely because an organizational title exists.

5. **ODL-05 — Resources, Maintenance & Administrative Burden**  
   Connect payroll/support, facilities, logistics, upkeep, information flow, scheduling and management effort to Economy/Projects/Bases/Vehicles without creating duplicate resource ledgers.

6. **ODL-06 — Crisis, Coup, Fragmentation, Recombination & Recovery**  
   Support governed internal crises such as schism, defection, hostile takeover, coup pressure, leadership loss, fragmentation, mergers/recombination and recovery through explicit event/state changes rather than random punishment for growth.

7. **ODL-07 — Business, Base, Settlement, Faction, Crew, Family & SCL Integration**  
   Prove shared organizational dynamics across several owner domains and define the handoff to SCL command capacity, morale/cohesion and large-scale consequences.

8. **ODL-08 — GM Control, Simulation Depth & Advisory AI**  
   Provide configurable simulation depth, summaries, pressure explanations, scenario previews and proposal-only AI assistance. A campaign may simplify or disable detailed organization pressure without deleting source state.

9. **ODL-09 — Organizational-Life Golden Proof**  
   Prove a small group growing into a larger staffed organization, delegation, internal pressure, crisis/recovery and a handoff to strategic command while maintaining deterministic provenance and owner-domain authority.

## Invariants

- ODL does not replace canonical Organization, Faction, Relationship, Economy, Base, Vehicle, Project or Character state.
- Growth does not automatically cause failure; pressures are profile/context driven.
- Organizational title never grants system permission by implication.
- Hidden motives/factions are permission-filtered before search, counts, AI context or player projection.
- AI may explain or propose management responses but never appoint, remove, command or adjudicate members autonomously.
