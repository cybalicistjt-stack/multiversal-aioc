- `coordination-span` — profile bands `narrow`, `balanced`, `wide`;
- `communication-burden` — `light`, `moderate`, `heavy`;
- `administrative-load` — `light`, `moderate`, `heavy`;
- `dependency` — `low`, `mixed`, `high`;
- `autonomy` — `low`, `mixed`, `high`;
- `geographic-spread` — `local`, `distributed`, `dispersed`;
- `organizational-complexity` — `simple`, `layered`, `complex`.

These bands are **profile/context labels**, not universal numeric thresholds. Each accepted observation must carry an explicit existing owner authority/reference plus profile, context, provenance and visibility references. Missing or explicit-unknown evidence remains `unknown`; incompatible-only evidence remains `incompatible`; divergent visible bands, profiles, contexts or observed/incompatible combinations remain `conflict`. ODL does not auto-resolve canonical-owner contradictions.
