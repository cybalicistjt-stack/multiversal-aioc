## Purpose

ODL turns the recovered mine-note doctrine that social power has carrying cost into governed organization dynamics. Followers, staff, dependents, crews, businesses, bases, factions and other groups may create coordination, loyalty, succession, administrative and factional pressures as they grow, without imposing one universal organization-size cap or duplicating existing Organization truth.

ODL consumes existing Organization/Faction/Relationship/Reputation, Economy, Base/Vehicle, Project, DPL and WCI authorities and prepares reusable leadership/load semantics for SCL command play.

Recovered basis: RDC-04 / RD-05.

Its governed event kinds are `crisis`, `coup`, `split`, `fragmentation`, `recombination`, and `recovery`. Evidence states are `observed`, `explicit-unknown`, and `incompatible`; projection states are `resolved`, `unknown`, `conflict`, and `incompatible`.

Every accepted event observation carries organization/subject identity, a canonical event reference, owner authority/reference, profile/context, provenance and visibility references. An observed row additionally carries an opaque canonical owner event-state reference. ODL-06 defines no universal crisis phase, coup threshold, fragmentation trigger, recombination rule or recovery formula.

Visibility filtering occurs before event/state projection. Observations are reconciled by canonical event reference only. A visible event resolves only when compatible observed evidence agrees on event kind, owner event-state reference and profile/context tuple. Missing/explicit-unknown remains unknown, incompatible-only remains incompatible, and divergent or mixed visible evidence remains conflict. Owner contradictions are never auto-resolved.
