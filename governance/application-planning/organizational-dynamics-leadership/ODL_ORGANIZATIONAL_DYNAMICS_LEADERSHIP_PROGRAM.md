## Purpose

ODL turns the recovered mine-note doctrine that social power has carrying cost into governed organization dynamics. Followers, staff, dependents, crews, businesses, bases, factions and other groups may create coordination, loyalty, succession, administrative and factional pressures as they grow, without imposing one universal organization-size cap or duplicating existing Organization truth.

ODL consumes existing Organization/Faction/Relationship/Reputation, Economy, Base/Vehicle, Project, DPL and WCI authorities and prepares reusable leadership/load semantics for SCL command play.

## ODL-05 governed contract

ODL-05 adds one bounded read-only projection over exactly three owner-evidence kinds: `resource-position`, `maintenance-state`, and `administrative-burden`.

Observed `resource-position` evidence may carry the owner-provided qualitative bands `surplus`, `adequate`, or `constrained`; `maintenance-state` may carry `current`, `due`, or `deferred`; `administrative-burden` may carry `light`, `moderate`, or `heavy`. These labels are profile/context-scoped owner observations, not universal numeric thresholds, formulas, computed balances, spending limits, maintenance timers or automatic outcomes.

Every accepted observation requires an explicit subject reference, projection kind, evidence state, canonical owner authority/reference, profile/context references, provenance, visibility reference and viewer authorization. Observed evidence also requires a kind-compatible qualitative band.

`MIB-13` remains authoritative for Economy/trade orchestration and forbids a second balance or asset ledger. `MIB-14` remains authoritative for Base/Vehicle/platform owner truth. `APW-D26` remains Project authority. DPL, WCI and Permission/visibility owner seams remain unchanged. ODL-05 creates no canonical owner truth.

Authorization filtering occurs before projection state, counts, summaries, search, provenance, deterministic receipts or AI context. Hidden/private resource, maintenance, administrative, organization, role/delegation, Project, profile/context, provenance and permission evidence never enters derived output and hidden cardinality is never exposed.

After filtering, a projection kind resolves only when compatible visible observed evidence agrees on one qualitative band under one profile/context tuple. Missing or explicit-unknown evidence remains `unknown`; incompatible-only evidence remains `incompatible`; divergent visible bands, profiles or contexts, or mixed observed/incompatible evidence remains `conflict`. Canonical-owner contradictions are never auto-resolved.

Visible ODL-02 administrative/load pressure and ODL-04 role/delegation/authority-scope evidence may be carried only as separately labeled advisory context. They never create or advance ODL-05 state, consume resources, create maintenance failure, impose action restrictions, grant spending or maintenance authority, advance Project/time or confer system permission.

Maintenance `due` or `deferred` and all other ODL-05 states are explicit owner-provided observations. Wall-clock elapsed time and projected time never advance them. ODL-05 performs no resource balance or asset mutation, cost/spending execution, maintenance action, administrative outcome, owner mutation, Project/time advancement, relationship/faction mutation, command authority, permission grant or canonical write.

No durable ODL-05 persistence or duplicate resource, maintenance, administration, Project or permission ledger is introduced. Migration `0022` remains unreserved.

Exactly one current ODL-05 Validation Core profile is authorized. Acceptance/proof/profile RED must exist before production; final proof requires exact-head self-hosted Linux and Windows plus deterministic comparison with zero historical predecessor fanout.
