## Purpose

SSA turns the recovered mine-note requirement that spatial things should know what they mean into a governed authoring layer over completed MAI and ISE foundations. It covers structural relationships such as wall↔wall, door/window↔wall, room/floor/entrance relationships, caves and irregular/non-Euclidean spaces, plus world-scale functional spatial semantics such as migration corridors, sanctuaries, gathering/hunting/use zones and specialized research infrastructure.

SSA does not replace World, Scene, MAI, ISE, Exploration, Research, Creature ecology, Asset or permission authority. It supplies typed spatial relationships, authoring assistance and projections that those owners can consume.

Recovered basis: RDC-06 / RD-07 / RD-08 and RDC-07 / RD-14 from the recovered mine-note program. The owner has explicitly directed that suitable recovered mine-note families be placed on the roadmap.

## Tranches

1. **SSA-01 — Authority Crosswalk & Spatial Semantic Taxonomy**  
   Reconcile recovered structural/world-space concepts with MAI, ISE, World/Reality, Scene, Exploration, Research, Creature ecology and permission authorities. Define spatial entity/relation families, ownership boundaries, stable references and the difference between geometry, semantic structure and live state.

2. **SSA-02 — Structural Primitives, Attachments & Construction Relationships**  
   Define walls, openings, doors, windows, floors, ceilings/roofs, stairs, ramps, rooms, entrances, shafts and other structural primitives plus attachment/connectivity rules. A visual adjacency is never structural truth until confirmed or imported from governed semantic metadata.

3. **SSA-03 — Caves, Irregular Geometry & Non-Euclidean Topology**  
   Add chambers, passages, tunnels, shafts, vertical spaces, caverns, nested/stacked spaces, portals and authored non-Euclidean relationships without requiring CAD-style modeling. Preserve accessible nonvisual topology representations.

4. **SSA-04 — Functional Spatial Zones, Ecology & Recurring Use**  
   Represent migration corridors, seasonal paths, sanctuaries, nesting/gathering/hunting/use zones, protected areas, hazard fields, trade/use corridors and other persistent spatial behaviors as typed world relationships rather than spawn-table prose.

5. **SSA-05 — Research, Institutional & Restricted Site Infrastructure**  
   Represent research hubs, field sites, remote stations, restricted facilities, observatories, laboratories and specialized infrastructure with access/credential, hazard, ownership, Project and information relationships while leaving Research progression with its owning systems.

6. **SSA-06 — MAI/ISE Asset Mapping, Snapping & Semantic Construction Roles**  
   Map permitted tilesets, images, structured map imports and placeables into semantic construction roles; support attachment/snapping suggestions, replacement assets and imported wall/door/room metadata without turning pixels into game truth.

7. **SSA-07 — GM Construction Assistance & Low-Friction Spatial Authoring**  
   Provide recognize/suggest/confirm workflows for rooms, boundaries, openings, paths and structural roles; include fast manual correction, reusable construction kits and unusual-space authoring. Assistance remains proposal-first where certainty is insufficient.

8. **SSA-08 — Spatial State, History, Era & Event Change**  
   Let governed Events alter routes, structures, access, migration, sanctuaries, research sites and spatial availability across eras while preserving history and avoiding duplicate World ledgers.

9. **SSA-09 — Accessibility, Multiview Authoring, Performance & Recovery**  
   Prove keyboard/touch/pen and nonvisual authoring equivalents, large/complex maps, deep topology, undo/recovery, conflict handling and deterministic semantic serialization.

10. **SSA-10 — Cross-Scale Spatial Golden Proof**  
    Prove one end-to-end package from imported/assembled local structure through cave/room semantics, an interactable ISE Scene, world-scale migration/sanctuary/research relationships and era/state change, with provenance and owner-domain boundaries intact.

## Invariants

- World/Reality and Scene owners remain authoritative for their state.
- MAI owns visual/map asset interoperability; completed ISE owns the native playable Scene surface.
- Spatial semantics do not become a second Research, Creature, Project, Exploration or construction ledger.
- Generated/inferred structure or topology remains assistance until confirmed where source confidence is insufficient.
- Geometry, semantic structure, topology and live state remain distinct.
- Visual adjacency, proximity and pixels are never structural or topological truth by themselves.
- Non-Euclidean or vertical support cannot bypass permission, Transition, Portal, Reality or movement authority.
- Accessible nonvisual representations must exist for consequential spatial state.
