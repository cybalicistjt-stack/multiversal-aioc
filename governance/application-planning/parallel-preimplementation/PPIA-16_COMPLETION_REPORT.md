## Locked authority and evidence boundaries

Raw producer semantics remain visible and distinct: PASS/WARN/FAIL/BLOCK are not lifecycle states; PREPARED is not READY_TO_EXECUTE; UNDECLARED/DECLARED/PROVEN traceability states do not mean a work item is complete. Only `completed_verified` is complete.

Candidate-bound evidence is valid only for the exact candidate it names. When candidate identity changes, prior evidence becomes stale/historical and cannot silently satisfy the new candidate. Historical CI failures are not automatic current blockers. Findings retain source, scope, raw state and blocker semantics. Evidence receipts retain producer-defined digest and mutation flags rather than being reinterpreted by the console.

Generated Codex task capsules, fixture projections, scenario results, permission-scan PASS, synthetic CI UI images, static design lint, traceability PROVEN, reference probes, recovery probes and tool timings are evidence or aids only. They do not create gameplay, repository, owner, security-certification, accessibility-certification, product-recovery, product-performance or canonical-content authority.

Ambiguous mutation status forbids blind retry. The owning operation or repository status must be checked before retry, and any idempotency authority must come from the owning subsystem rather than console inference. Confirmation cannot create missing authority. Owner-gated operations remain nonexecuting until actual owner authority is present.
