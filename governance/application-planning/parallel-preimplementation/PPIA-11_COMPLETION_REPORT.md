## Blocking boundaries retained

- **No universal CR**, encounter level, difficulty score, threat number, universal balance number or cross-domain power scalar is created.
- No weighted sum is required to collapse the twenty factors or twelve pressure dimensions.
- Within-domain 8D-007 target bands are not promoted into cross-domain equivalence.
- Source mechanics and owning-domain state remain immutable under PPIA-11 analysis.
- Observations, benchmarks, authored methodology and recommendations remain distinct from source truth.
- Unknown/source-unspecified values are not replaced with zero, average, midpoint, normal, unlimited, compatible or safe defaults.
- Action economy, resources, environment, objectives and failure paths remain independently inspectable rather than being coerced into HP/damage equivalence.
- Mixed-scale analysis requires explicit governing interaction rules; absent rules may block as indeterminate.
- Rune SCI/CSL are not power, damage, success probability, failure probability or universal cost metrics.
- Map pixels, visual size, dungeon geometry and narrative importance do not create threat or numeric equivalence.
- Boss, wave, reinforcement, retreat, hazard and objective methodology organizes existing rules and encounter-local configuration; it does not invent missing source mechanics.
- Benchmark and simulation results are evidence, not guaranteed outcome predictions.
- No balanced, fair, safe, winnable, optimal or guaranteed certification is permitted.
- Automatic balance rewrite and automatic canonical benchmark promotion are prohibited.
- Permission filtering precedes every protected derivative.
- Historical evidence and assessment receipts remain immutable.
- AI has no irreversible encounter/balance authority.
