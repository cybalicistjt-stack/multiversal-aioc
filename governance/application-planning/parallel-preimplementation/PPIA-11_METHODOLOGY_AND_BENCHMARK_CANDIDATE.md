# PPIA-11 — Encounter Methodology & Benchmark Contract Candidate

**Work item:** PPIA-11 — Encounter & Balance Design Laboratory  
**Milestone:** Encounter Methodology & Benchmark Contract  
**State:** completion candidate for this milestone only  
**Runtime activation authorized:** No  
**STAGE-A-A2 activation authorized:** No

## 1. Purpose

This milestone turns the verified PPIA-11 source/design foundation into a repeatable human-facing encounter assessment and calibration procedure.

It does not replace MV-IA-F012. It deepens F012's existing Encounter Builder and Balance Lab contract, which already requires version-bound Encounter drafts, twelve independent pressure dimensions, explicit assumptions and omissions, uncertainty, scenario comparison, bounded deterministic simulation, human approval, permission-safe projections, and the prohibition on certified balance claims.

PPIA-11 adds:

- a thirteen-step encounter assessment/calibration sequence;
- a benchmark-encounter schema with explicit evidence/provenance classes;
- eighteen deterministic noncanonical methodology reference cases;
- complete coverage of the twenty verified PPIA-11 factor families;
- complete coverage of all twelve inherited F012 pressure dimensions;
- explicit handling of bosses/solos, mixed opposition, waves, reinforcements, hazards, objectives, retreat, vehicles and mixed scale;
- post-benchmark/playtest calibration that can revise recommendations without rewriting source truth.

## 2. Governing evidence boundary

Every material input remains one of the five verified classes:

1. source truth;
2. inherited contract;
3. observed benchmark;
4. authored methodology;
5. unresolved gap.

The benchmark schema and all eighteen initial reference cases are authored methodology. The cases are deliberately synthetic QA fixtures and are not canonical Creature, Item, Vehicle, Species, Rune, Scene or Encounter content.

8D-007 remains observed benchmark evidence. Its 18 domains, 36 fixtures, 24 deterministic scenarios and 72 recorded executions can supply reproducibility and within-domain comparison evidence. Its normalized target bands do not become cross-domain encounter power.

## 3. Inherited MV-IA-F012 pressure model

The methodology preserves the twelve F012 pressure dimensions independently:

1. durability-recovery;
2. sustained-output;
3. burst-spike;
4. action-economy;
5. control-denial;
6. mobility-position;
7. environment-hazard;
8. objective-time;
9. information-surprise;
10. attrition-resource;
11. reinforcement-escalation;
12. failure-path.

No weighted sum is required. PPIA-11 does not collapse these dimensions into a universal difficulty, threat, challenge-rating, fairness, safety, win-probability or optimality number.

## 4. Thirteen-step assessment sequence

`PPIA-11_ENCOUNTER_METHODOLOGY_CONTRACT_v0.1.0.json` defines `P11-M-001..013`:

1. Declare encounter intent, objectives and failure/partial-success paths.
2. Bind the exact authoritative rules/schema/pack/context/participant snapshot.
3. Classify encounter form and staged structure.
4. Validate authority, dependencies, compatibility and explicit interaction rules.
5. Classify every material input by the five evidence classes.
6. Inspect capabilities, output, survivability, action economy, resources and mobility.
7. Inspect environment, hazards, objectives, escalation, retreat and alternatives.
8. Project independent F012 pressure findings.
9. Select only valid peer/context comparators and record explicit deltas.
10. Optionally run bounded deterministic benchmark/simulation evidence.
11. Assign low, moderate, high or indeterminate uncertainty with named reasons.
12. Produce a factor-specific human-facing recommendation/proposed delta.
13. Calibrate later from governed benchmark/playtest evidence while preserving historical receipts and source truth.

A responsible result may be **indeterminate**. Fabricated precision is not a completion requirement.

## 5. Comparator contract

A comparison must retain:

- exact immutable snapshot identities and versions;
- compatible or explicitly bounded rules-profile context;
- compatible scale or an explicit cross-scale interaction rule;
- a named delta set;
- permission-safe inputs;
- factor/dimension limitations.

The methodology explicitly rejects:

- using within-domain 8D-007 bands as cross-domain equivalence;
- converting SCI/CSL into power;
- treating map pixels or visual size as threat;
- averaging mutually exclusive wave/reinforcement states;
- changing analysis-policy versions without recording the delta;
- using inaccessible hidden information in a lower-authority projection.

## 6. Recommendation contract

Recommendations are advisory and factor-specific. Supported recommendation classes include composition, wave/timing, environment/hazard, objective/failure-path, resource assumption, information/reveal, unresolved-rule resolution, benchmark request, or no-change-supported.

A recommendation cannot:

- mutate a reusable Definition;
- edit source mechanics;
- approve an Encounter;
- attach an Encounter to a Scene;
- change a live Session;
- automatically rewrite a Campaign-local draft;
- claim that the proposed result will be balanced, fair, safe, winnable, optimal or guaranteed.

Any consequential change remains a human/governed owning-workflow decision.

## 7. Benchmark Encounter schema

`PPIA-11_BENCHMARK_ENCOUNTER_SCHEMA_v0.1.0.json` requires every benchmark to retain exact versions and provenance for:

- benchmark identity/class/purpose;
- rules, schema and pack lock;
- context snapshot;
- sides and participant/object identities;
- objective;
- environment/hazards;
- waves/reinforcements;
- retreat/failure paths;
- evidence ledger;
- factor review;
- independent pressure-dimension review;
- comparator scope;
- deterministic protocol;
- uncertainty;
- expected methodology findings;
- prohibited claims;
- calibration hooks;
- provenance.

Deterministic script and regression replay must reproduce the same analysis result for identical immutable inputs. Seeded sampling must record seed and bound. Sensitivity sweeps must name every changed variable.

Benchmark execution is nonmutating.

## 8. Initial benchmark reference set

`PPIA-11_BENCHMARK_REFERENCE_CASES_v0.1.0.json` defines eighteen contiguous cases `PPIA11-BM-001..018`.

The set covers every verified encounter form, every PPIA-11 factor family, every F012 pressure dimension and all four uncertainty bands.

Required structures are explicitly exercised:

- mixed opposition;
- boss/solo;
- elite group;
- waves;
- reinforcements;
- hazards/traps;
- timed/objective encounters;
- pursuit/escape/retreat;
- vehicle/mecha/spacecraft;
- mixed scale with explicit interaction rules;
- mixed scale without interaction rules;
- social/noncombat pressure;
- hybrid pressure;
- explicit single-delta comparison;
- deterministic regression replay;
- hidden-wave projection safety;
- post-playtest calibration.

The mixed-scale-without-rule case must end **indeterminate/blocked** rather than inventing a conversion factor.

## 9. Boss, wave and reinforcement rule

Boss/solo analysis may identify concentrated-threat action-economy or control fragility. It may not add boss-only actions, phases, immunities or Resources unless an owning source/rules contract supplies them.

Waves and reinforcements are state changes. They are not automatically front-loaded into one total. Comparisons may inspect pre-trigger and post-trigger states as explicit immutable alternatives.

Hidden wave existence/count/timing remains absent from unauthorized projections.

## 10. Hazard, objective and retreat rule

Independent hazard pressure remains distinguishable from participant output.

Objective success/failure is not synonymous with defeating every opponent. Timed protection, retrieval, delay, escape, rescue, negotiation, investigation, bypass, surrender and retreat can be modeled only to the extent that governing context/rules support them.

Retreat and failure paths are first-class assessment inputs. Adding one as a recommendation is a proposal, not an automatic rules change.

## 11. Mixed-scale rule

Cross-scale analysis requires explicit interaction rules. Vehicle, mecha, spacecraft, Character, Creature, Item or other scales keep their owning mechanics.

If the evidence does not establish how two scales interact, the assessment may be indeterminate. PPIA-11 cannot solve the gap with a visual-size heuristic, a made-up conversion factor or a promoted within-domain target band.

## 12. Uncertainty

The four verified bands remain:

- low;
- moderate;
- high;
- indeterminate.

Uncertainty increases when material mechanics are source-unspecified, contradictory, hidden from the authorized analyst, dependent on unresolved interaction rules, or unsupported by comparable benchmark evidence.

Uncertainty is not a hidden numeric confidence score.

## 13. Calibration

Calibration may compare a prior immutable assessment with later governed deterministic benchmark evidence or authorized playtest observations.

A calibration record preserves:

- the prior assessment;
- later evidence;
- named context deltas;
- before/after recommendations;
- unchanged source truth;
- provenance/history.

Calibration never silently edits old receipts or source mechanics. A later recommendation can supersede an earlier recommendation without falsifying the historical record.

## 14. Permission, recovery and accessibility

Permission filtering occurs before protected benchmark input resolution, counts, pressure summaries, warnings, comparisons, export, diagnostics or AI context.

Player/observer-safe projections cannot reveal hidden participants, waves, triggers, objectives, tactics or internal pressure shapes that disclose them.

Authoritative Encounter draft/approval/attachment operations retain F012/F020/F021 expected-version, idempotency, reconnect and human-approval rules. PPIA-11 analysis does not create a bypass.

All pressure findings, uncertainty, comparator deltas, recommendations and calibration history require semantic text/table equivalents; charts remain optional presentation.

## 15. AI boundary

AI is optional. From the minimum authorized projection it may:

- explain evidence-linked warnings;
- summarize factor/dimension findings;
- compare explicit immutable alternatives;
- propose a bounded recommendation.

AI may not:

- invent missing mechanics;
- increase evidence confidence;
- mutate source or Campaign state;
- approve or attach an Encounter;
- certify balance or predict actual players as fact.
