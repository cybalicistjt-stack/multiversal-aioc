## Purpose

Turn the verified PPIA-11 foundation and Encounter Methodology & Benchmark Contract into implementation-ready Inspector semantics, governed operations, idempotent recovery behavior and deterministic reference cases without reopening source authority, creating a universal challenge scalar, or claiming PPIA-11 completion.

The candidate retains all **20 independently inspectable factor families**, all **12 inherited MV-IA-F012 pressure dimensions**, all four uncertainty bands, all thirteen assessment/calibration steps and all ten domain handoffs. Nothing in this milestone converts those dimensions into a weighted sum, CR, encounter level, threat number, difficulty score, power score or guaranteed-balance label.

## Inspector projection

The contract defines **16 projection groups**. They expose encounter identity/intent; exact participant/context versions; capabilities/output/survivability; action economy/resources; mobility/environment; objectives/hazards/failure paths; mixed-scale/boss/wave structure; evidence/provenance/assumptions; the complete factor ledger; the complete independent pressure ledger; uncertainty/gaps; benchmark selection/comparison; scenario deltas/sensitivity; advisory recommendations; calibration history; and replay/recovery/accessibility diagnostics.

Permission filtering occurs before reference resolution and before protected counts, aggregates, factor or pressure derivatives, benchmark selection, charts, deltas, recommendations, export, diagnostics or AI context. Unauthorized hidden waves or reinforcements cannot leak through counts, missing labels, comparator choices or derived summaries. Revocation invalidates protected caches and recomputation uses the caller's current minimum authorized projection.

Charts and graphs are nonauthoritative. Semantic list/table/linear projections retain the same authorized meaning and operations. Low, moderate, high and indeterminate uncertainty remain first-class states. Missing mixed-scale interaction rules yield `indeterminate_blocked` rather than fabricated conversion.

## Governed action surface

The matrix defines **24 actions**:

- **12 permission-filtered reads** for the encounter snapshot, factor ledger, pressure ledger, uncertainty, benchmark candidates/comparison, sensitivity deltas, evidence/provenance, recommendations, calibration history, replay diagnostics and authorized hidden structure;
- **8 nonmutating analysis/proposal operations** for deterministic assessment, benchmark comparison, scenario deltas, replay, encounter-adjustment proposals, benchmark annotations, calibration proposals and disclosure preview;
- **4 narrowly scoped authoritative writes** for accepting an advisory adjustment into an authorized encounter draft/configuration, recording a playtest observation, recording a calibration outcome, and curating an explicitly noncanonical QA benchmark.

Analysis never mutates. Proposals are nonauthoritative. The four writes cannot edit source truth or owning-domain mechanics and cannot promote a benchmark to canon.

## Mutation and recovery protocol

Every write binds `P11-MUT-001` and requires explicit authorization, `expected_version` and a stable `operation_id`.

After an ambiguous service result, the client queries operation status and current version before retrying. Compatible duplicate operation IDs converge on the prior result/status. Conflicting reuse fails safely without mutation. Blind retry and offline authoritative mutation are prohibited.

An accepted advisory adjustment changes only the authorized encounter draft/configuration boundary. Playtest observations are observed evidence, not source truth. Calibration changes future authored advisory interpretation while historical evidence and prior assessment receipts remain immutable. A curated QA benchmark remains labeled noncanonical and provenance-backed.

AI may summarize, compare, replay or propose from the authorized projection only. It cannot approve, attach, mutate, publish canonical truth or certify an encounter as balanced, fair, safe, winnable, optimal or guaranteed.

## Deterministic reference corpus

The corpus resolves **42 deterministic cases**:

- 18 inherited noncanonical benchmark methodology fixtures, preserving their encounter forms, factor/pressure coverage, uncertainty and expected disposition;
- 24 local Inspector/action/recovery cases.

The local cases explicitly prove:

- hidden reinforcement is filtered before derivatives and benchmark selection;
- authorized hidden-wave inspection remains permission scoped;
- unknown source values increase uncertainty without defaults;
- mixed-scale analysis without a governing interaction rule blocks as indeterminate;
- incompatible benchmark versions are rejected;
- comparison never normalizes to a universal scalar;
- charts and semantic nonvisual projections preserve parity;
- deterministic replay reproduces identical pinned inputs while version changes invalidate stale replay equivalence;
- accepted adjustments change encounter drafts only;
- AI proposals cannot auto-apply;
- compatible duplicate operation IDs converge and conflicting reuse fails;
- ambiguous mutation results recover through operation status/current version before safe retry;
- calibration changes future advisory interpretation only;
- permission revocation purges protected cached derivatives;
- export and AI context filter hidden state before derivatives;
- playtest observations remain evidence rather than source truth;
- QA benchmark curation remains noncanonical;
- sensitivity sweeps change only named variables;
- benchmark annotation proposals cannot canonically promote;
- recommendations remain evidence/provenance/uncertainty backed;
- all 20 factors and all 12 pressure dimensions remain independently inspectable;
- intent, hazards, objectives, retreat/bypass/partial-success and failure paths remain explicit.
