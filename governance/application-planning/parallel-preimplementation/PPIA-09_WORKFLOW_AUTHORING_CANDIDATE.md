**Status:** implementation-ready design contract candidate — **NOT PPIA-09 COMPLETE**

This milestone composes the verified PPIA-09 foundation and inspector/action/reference contracts into **18 end-to-end Investigation/Mystery workflows**. **15 workflows perform authoritative mutation** and 3 are read-only Player review, recovery/accessibility, or cross-domain audit paths.

## End-to-end experience

The 18 workflows cover: Investigation setup and hidden GM truth framing; clue-definition and Campaign-clue instantiation; discovery and analysis; observations, claims and owning-domain evidence; source-quality annotations; Player/GM hypotheses and typed semantic connections; questions, leads and research; timeline/alibi authoring; contradiction/false-lead/corruption review; revelation dependency authoring and read-only solvability review; stall recovery and dynamic clue delivery; reveal/withhold/revoke control; Player knowledge-safe clue-board review; GM dossier/reference-mystery authoring; conclusion/resolution/history; proposal-only AI review and acceptance; reconnect/idempotent recovery and semantic nonvisual operation; and cross-domain evidence/chronology/permission audit.

## Locked boundaries

- Objective truth, GM solution/conclusion, clues, observations, claims, evidence, hypotheses and Player knowledge remain separately typed. Player deductions are never auto-promoted to fact.
- Permission filtering occurs before hidden existence, counts, labels, relationships, search, diagnostics, exports, notifications, realtime payloads or AI context are resolved.
- Confidence, relevance, authenticity and source reliability are attributable assessments. There is **no mandatory universal numeric probability scale** and these annotations are not objective truth probability.
- Contradiction review never automatically adjudicates truth. False leads and corrupted evidence retain attribution and never silently rewrite objective truth.
- Solvability, redundancy and stall diagnostics are read-only authoring findings over explicit authored routes. They never resolve the case, invent a route, create a clue, or mutate truth/reveal state.
- The source-grounded **at least two places** guidance remains an important-information redundancy warning. It is not a universal clue-count law, mandatory fairness threshold, or guarantee of success.
- Evidence uses stable `ownerDomain` / `objectId` / `objectVersion` references. PPIA-09 does not take ownership of Items, Creatures/NPCs, Vehicles, Settings, Locations or Events.
- PPIA-12 retains World/Setting chronology authority; hidden world chronology does not automatically become Player-known case chronology.
- Investigation, Research, Knowledge, Perception, Insight, Forensics and similar resolution mechanics remain owning rules-domain behavior; PPIA-09 does not invent universal DC, XP, time or success formulas.
- Every authoritative mutation requires `expected_version` plus `operation_id`; ambiguous outcomes require operation-status/current-version lookup before retry.
- Spatial clue-board layout remains presentation state. Typed semantic edges, ordered lists/tables and screen-reader-ready **semantic nonvisual** equivalents preserve the same meaning.
- AI-generated organization, theories, connections, clues or mysteries remain proposal-only until explicitly accepted through the governed acceptance action.
