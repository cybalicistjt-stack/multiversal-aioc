## 1. Authority boundary

PPIA-05 deepens the already-approved Character, Universal Object, Creature/NPC, permission, recovery, and accessibility contracts around species, forms, biology, morphology, senses, movement, Adaptations, and transformations. It does not turn every species-related statement into biology, does not promote mixed ability rows merely because a dataset name contains `species` or `innate`, and does not replace the owning Character or Ability systems.

Use authority in this order:

1. current repository governance and Internal Alpha contracts, especially MV-IA-F004, F002, F006/F007, F020, F021, and F022;
2. the governed 8E-009 CSV-first 20-dataset / 19,199-row reconciliation and its ability-domain routing contracts;
3. retained Species/Form/Biology source PDFs for exact source truth;
4. PPIA-01 source-fact / source-absence / inference / recommendation distinctions and the existing Shapeshifter pricing analysis;
5. PPIA-02 Creature/NPC form, transformation, ecology, and playable-conversion contracts where creature source is involved;
6. PPIA-03 generic Asset/equipment/symbiote/cybernetic semantics only where a biological modification is actually modeled as an Asset or installed object;
7. world/environment sources as supporting context, never automatic proof that an environment perk is an innate biological Adaptation.

The earlier obsolete 487-object semantic-parse database is not restored as Species/Form content authority.

- Environmental Adaptations: 20 detailed / 19 pricing-only;
- Utility Transformations: 20 detailed / 18 pricing-only;

Name similarity and tier are review hints only. PPIA-05 must not collapse a pricing-only listing into a detailed Ability merely because names or tiers look related. Exact source evidence or an explicitly governed recommendation is required.

This is especially important for biological-looking names such as body weapons, hide, gills, lungs, camouflage, extra limbs, environmental immunity, and alternate forms: a name alone cannot establish exact mechanics.

## 7. Source examples that shape the taxonomy

The retained PDFs demonstrate several distinct species/form patterns that must not be flattened:

- `Humans vAlpha.PDF`, `Dwarves vAlpha.PDF`, `Elves vAlpha.PDF`, and similar Species files separate physical appearance/biology from culture, philosophy, belief, adventuring, and NPC examples.
- `Oaran Species.PDF` mixes physiological facts, reproductive/sex traits, habitat affinity, appearance, and social/cultural material in one prose document; the application must preserve those distinctions instead of treating every paragraph as intrinsic mechanics.
- `Mythragara vAlpha2.PDF` contains explicit shapeshifting mechanics, form availability, mental/sensory shifts, limitations, magic interaction, and sample Form traits.
- `Suula.PDF` contains a dedicated Adaptation system with active Adaptations, XP-purchased Adaptations, swapping, learning, incompatibilities, extremophile biology, and visible markings.
- `Kola-Ha Bioengineering.PDF` defines biological `Forms` as a bioengineering system that can be physical, sensory, psychic, or environmental and may range from temporary Adaptations to permanent symbiotic evolution. Those Forms are not generic equipment or an automatic baseline trait of every species.
- `Traiga vAlpha2.PDF`, `ManyToms vAlpha.PDF`, `Moravi vAlpha.PDF`, `Nekrons vAlpha2.PDF`, and others contain explicit variants/subspecies/adaptations that require source-backed relationship identity.
- `Species Perks.PDF` and `Species Perks(1).PDF` include both strongly biological and clearly learned/social/generalized perks. `Species perk` therefore means source ownership/eligibility, not necessarily innate physiology.

## 8. Existing Internal Alpha and PPIA authority

### MV-IA-F004 — Character Creation and Advancement

F004 is implementation-ready and explicitly requires Characters to select permitted **species and forms** through the Universal Object Experience. Species, forms, Abilities, Actions, Effects, Conditions, Resources, equipment, and other choices remain reusable governed Definitions; a Character stores stable references and accepted state rather than editing those Definitions.

F004 also requires authoritative prerequisite/grant/cost/compatibility validation, version-pinned packs, history-preserving advancement/correction, and role-safe recovery.

### MV-IA-F002 — Universal Object Experience

Species, Forms, traits, variants, and source/provenance records use the same stable-ID browse, inspect, compare, relationship, and constrained-selection patterns as other governed objects. Inspector presentation does not grant mutation authority.

### PPIA-02 — Creature & NPC Experience

PPIA-02 already distinguishes source variants, stages, type/affinity modifiers, forms/transformations, Campaign-local overrides, live runtime state, and playable conversion. A runtime transformation changes authorized live form state, not the underlying reusable Definition. Playable creature conversion hands into Character/species construction rather than relabeling a monster instance.

### F006/F007 — Action and combat resolution

When a transformation, adaptation switch, biological attack, or form change is an Action, its runtime mutation goes through the authoritative Action/proposal/approval/event pipeline. UI state does not directly mutate body state or Resources.

### F020 / F021

Permissions filter before projection/aggregation/search and reconnect reauthorizes. Hidden forms, unrevealed weaknesses, private Character biological information, GM-only transformations, and source diagnostics cannot leak through counts, compare targets, validation messages, exports, notifications, or AI context.

### F022 — Accessibility and adaptive interface

Species/Form selection, body-plan inspection, transformation, Adaptation management, and difference comparison require keyboard, touch, screen-reader, high-zoom/reflow, reduced-motion, and noncolor equivalents. Appearance or anatomy cannot be communicated solely by image, color, animation, or spatial manipulation.

## 9. Culture-versus-biology boundary

Species source files frequently contain culture, society, philosophy, belief, professions, history, world identity, and behavioral guidance beside physical/biological sections.

PPIA-05 therefore requires a first-class separation:

- **biology/source physiology** — body plan, anatomy, senses, movement, lifespan, reproductive/sex traits where sourced, innate resistances/vulnerabilities, biological capabilities;
- **species eligibility/grants** — source-defined options available because of Species/Form identity, which may be biological, learned, cultural, mystical, technological, or mixed;
- **culture/social context** — beliefs, customs, roles, social organization, history, philosophy, language practices, and world-specific identity;
- **Character-authored identity** — individual appearance, identity, history, expression, and choices within source/rules constraints;
- **runtime state** — current form, active Adaptation, injury/Condition, temporary Effect, equipment, environment, and current Resources.

A culture statement never becomes immutable biology merely because it appears in a Species PDF. A learned perk never becomes an organ or innate sense merely because its prerequisite is a Species.

## 10. Initial identity/state problem space

The PPIA-05 user experience must keep at least these layers distinguishable:

1. reusable Species Definition;
2. source-backed lineage/subspecies/variant relationship;
3. reusable Form / alternate-form Definition;
4. biological trait / species perk / innate Ability Definition;
5. morphology/body-plan/anatomy Definition facts;
6. Character species/form selection and granted biological build state;
7. current Character body/form state;
8. active transformation and Adaptation state;
9. senses/movement/physiology state;
10. biological compatibility, limits, vulnerabilities, and source-defined constraints;
11. bioengineering/symbiosis/biological-modification state where explicitly sourced;
12. knowledge/reveal/visibility projection of biological truth;
13. provenance/history/recovery.

Culture, equipment, ordinary learned skills, temporary Effects/Conditions, Campaign environment, and presentation/appearance are related facets but are not silently merged into those biological layers.

## 13. Foundation boundary and next substep

This inventory does not modify raw CSVs or PDFs, promote mixed ability rows, auto-pair Shapeshifter pricing rows, invent Species/Form relationships, convert culture into biology, invent missing mechanics, implement runtime code, activate STAGE-A-A2, or authorize release/deployment/tester/paid/credential operations.

The next bounded PPIA-05 step is to formalize the 13-layer identity/state model into a machine-verifiable taxonomy with presentation profiles, biology/culture/source guardrails, transformation/Adaptation invariants, permission/recovery/accessibility requirements, and explicit F004/PPIA-02/PPIA-03/PPIA-06/PPIA-08/PPIA-11/PPIA-12 handoffs.
