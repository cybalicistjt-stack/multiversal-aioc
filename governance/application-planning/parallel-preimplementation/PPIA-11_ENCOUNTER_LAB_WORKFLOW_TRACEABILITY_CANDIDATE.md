## Purpose

Integrate the verified PPIA-11 source/design foundation, Encounter Methodology & Benchmark Contract, and Encounter Lab Inspector / Action / Reference contracts into bounded human-governed end-to-end workflows with complete deterministic traceability.

**No universal CR**, difficulty score, threat number, encounter level or power scalar is created by this milestone. It does not collapse the twenty factors or twelve inherited MV-IA-F012 pressure dimensions into a weighted balance score. It does not rewrite source mechanics, fill unknown values with defaults, canonically promote benchmark observations, or certify an encounter as balanced, fair, safe, winnable, optimal or guaranteed.

## Integrated workflow surface

The package defines **14 end-to-end workflows**:

1. author and assess an encounter baseline;
2. select and compare a valid benchmark;
3. run a bounded benchmark and deterministic replay;
4. run a named-variable sensitivity delta;
5. propose and human-accept an advisory encounter-draft adjustment;
6. inspect hidden waves and preview authorized disclosure;
7. assess permission-safe snapshots containing unknowns;
8. block invalid mixed-scale analysis or incompatible comparators;
9. perform accessible, version-pinned replay;
10. apply an idempotent encounter-draft adjustment;
11. recover an ambiguous write and record calibration;
12. handle permission revocation, safe export and playtest observation;
13. curate an explicitly noncanonical QA benchmark; and
14. perform human recommendation review including a valid no-change outcome.

Five workflows may invoke the narrow `P11-MUT-001` write boundary. Nine are read/analysis-only. No write edits owning-domain source truth or mechanics.

## Human-governed mutation boundary

The five mutation workflows use `P11-MUT-001`. Every authoritative write requires explicit authorization, `expected_version`, and a stable `operation_id`.

After an ambiguous result, the client queries operation status and current version before any retry. Compatible duplicate operation IDs converge on the prior result/status. Conflicting operation-ID reuse fails without mutation. Offline authoritative mutation and blind retry are prohibited.

Writes remain limited to:

- an authorized encounter draft/configuration;
- a provenance-backed playtest observation;
- a calibration outcome that changes future authored advisory interpretation only; or
- an explicitly noncanonical QA benchmark.

Historical evidence and prior assessment receipts remain immutable.

## Permission, hidden-state and accessibility rules

Permission filtering occurs before protected reference resolution, counts, aggregates, factor or pressure derivatives, benchmark selection, charts, deltas, recommendations, export, diagnostics and AI context.

Hidden waves or reinforcements cannot leak through counts, missing labels, comparator selection, uncertainty metadata or downstream derivatives. Revocation invalidates protected cached derivatives and recomputation uses the caller's current minimum authorized projection.

Charts and graphs remain nonauthoritative. Semantic list/table/linear projections preserve the same authorized meaning, uncertainty, provenance and available operations.

## Comparison, simulation and uncertainty

Benchmark selection requires compatible rules profile, scale/interaction context, exact versions, explicit delta set and permission-safe inputs. Incompatible comparators are rejected with a reason.

Simulation and replay are bounded deterministic evidence tools. They do not predict actual players and do not become source canon. Sensitivity workflows pin a baseline and change only named variables.

Unknown or source-unspecified values remain unknown. Missing source mechanics, inaccessible hidden inputs, materially incomparable benchmarks or unresolved cross-scale interaction rules increase uncertainty. `indeterminate` and `indeterminate_blocked` are valid terminal outcomes and are preferred to fabricated precision.

## Authority and AI boundary

All ten PPIA-11 domain handoffs are exercised while ownership remains with the Creature/NPC, Item/Equipment, Vehicle/Mecha/Starship, Species/Form/Biology, Rune Construction, Campaign/Scene/Session, Action Resolution, Permissions, Recovery and 8D-007 Balance Harness domains.

AI may summarize, compare, replay or propose from the authorized projection only. AI cannot approve, attach, mutate, canonically promote, reveal hidden state or certify balance.
