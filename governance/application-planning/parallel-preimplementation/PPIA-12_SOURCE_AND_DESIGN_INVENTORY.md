## Source-grounded patterns that shape the model

### Nested setting structure is real, but relationships require evidence

- `Havalaea.PDF` describes Vertigon as being at the heart of Havalaea and describes the rest of the world as divided into major zones. `Vertigon.PDF` independently presents Vertigon as a city spread across the living surface of Havalaea. This is strong source evidence for a World → City relationship.
- The same standard must apply elsewhere: shared names, shared files, thematic similarity, or geographic plausibility are review hints only and cannot create hierarchy or membership.

### A setting entity may be larger or smaller than a planet

- `Black Vegas.PDF` is explicitly a spaceborne city-station in the Chronica Branch.
- `The Antiquaria.PDF` describes a semi-transdimensional generation ship with built-on cities and the Coalescence.
- `The Rakuuta Road.PDF` describes pathways connecting realities rather than a single world.
- `The layers of the multiverse.PDF`, `New Branches Info.PDF`, and `Elemental Planes.PDF` describe cosmological/branch/plane structures.
- Therefore World/Setting authoring must support a typed hierarchy rather than assuming every setting is a planet with continent/country/city descendants.

### Setting-local mechanics must remain scoped

- `Musical Reality Gameplay.PDF` explicitly applies mechanics to certain musical realities.
- `New Branches Info.PDF` defines branch-specific gameplay mechanics.
- `The Chaos v2.PDF`, `The Gray Sands.PDF`, and `Elemental Planes.PDF` contain unusual local physical/environmental behavior.
- These rules may be attached to their governed setting scope; their presence in a setting source does not make them universal core mechanics.

### Source documents can mix facets and revision states

- `The Empire.PDF` combines governance, economy, law, regional oversight, organizations, and examples of regulation zones; it is not reducible to a single World record.
- `Noir City(1).PDF` combines districts, landmarks, factions, and local setting details.
- `Skoaltarra v2(1).PDF` combines realm description, themes, culture/species context, and campaign-use guidance.
- `Stratebrait.PDF` explicitly contains older/current/new material and asks for reconciliation. PPIA-12 must preserve unresolved revision/conflict provenance rather than silently choosing or blending facts.

### Environment templates are not world facts

- The eight environment-template PDFs describe reusable archetypes, hazards, resources, and environmental abilities/effects. A World or Region may reference or specialize such an environment, but the template does not prove that any particular setting contains it.
- Existing Feature Bible exploration contracts separately define Travel, Discovery, Environment, navigation, campaign-specific discovery, hidden reveal, and provenance. PPIA-12 should reference these capabilities rather than duplicate them as setting identity.

### Worldbuilding aids do not create canon automatically

- `Worldbuilding.PDF` is guidance about geography, culture, economy, and other authoring concerns.
- `World Creation tables.PDF` is a random-generation aid. Rolls, suggestions, or AI-generated output from these aids are authoring proposals until a governed author accepts them into a setting definition.

## Existing governed design surface to preserve

- UI Design Bible World Builder scope already names Worlds, Regions, Settlements, Factions, Organizations, Religions, Languages, Calendars, environmental data, hierarchy, maps, cross references, and validation.
- Feature Bible exploration contracts already define setting-independent Travel, Discovery, Environment, navigation/fog-of-war behavior, permission-safe hidden discoveries, world/location references, and campaign-specific discovery state.
- PPIA-12 owns setting structure, scoped setting extensions, reference mappings, and authoring contracts. It must hand Campaign/Scene instantiation and live authoring depth to PPIA-08; generic Creature/NPC, Item/Inventory, Vehicle, Species/Biology, appearance, and encounter/balance semantics remain with their owning domains.

## Foundation guardrails

1. **Source scope before universal scope.** A rule, Species, Creature, Item, Vehicle, Ability, environment, faction, culture, calendar, or historical claim found in a setting source remains setting-scoped unless separate authority promotes it.
2. **Definition is not Campaign state.** Reusable World/Setting Definition data is distinct from a Campaign instance, current Scene, discovered/revealed state, destroyed/changed locations, local faction state, or current timeline branch.
3. **Typed hierarchy, not planet assumptions.** Worlds, realms, branches, planes, cities, stations, ships, routes, zones, districts, regions, and other setting entities require explicit type and source-backed relations.
4. **No hierarchy by co-occurrence.** Same document, same name, proximity, matching theme, or shared faction does not create parent/child, membership, route, compatibility, or chronology.
5. **Environment template is not instantiation.** Reusable environment documents become setting facts only through explicit source-backed attachment or authoring.
6. **World-local object extension does not replace owning object domain.** PPIA-12 may scope availability, provenance, variant/extension metadata, and world-local rules around Species/Creatures/Items/Vehicles/Abilities, but their core definitions stay with PPIA-02/03/04/05 and Ability/rules owners.
7. **Authoring aids create proposals.** Random tables, AI assistance, templates, and inferred cross-references cannot silently create canonical facts.
8. **Unknown stays unknown.** Missing geography, population, parentage, chronology, connectivity, compatibility, or mechanics must not become a default value.
9. **Conflicts stay visible.** Contradictory or revision-heavy source material remains separately attributed until governed resolution.
10. **Permission before aggregation.** Hidden locations, secrets, undiscovered routes, unrevealed factions/history, and GM-only setting facts are filtered before search, counts, maps, summaries, exports, diagnostics, notifications, or AI context.
11. **Accessible non-map path required.** Hierarchy, location relations, route/connectivity, environment, and reveal state must have textual/nonvisual representations; maps cannot be the sole authoritative or operable surface.
12. **No runtime activation.** This foundation does not activate STAGE-A-A2 or mutate application runtime.
