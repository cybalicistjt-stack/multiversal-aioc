# PPIA-11 — Encounter & Balance Design Laboratory Source and Design Inventory

**Status:** FOUNDATION CANDIDATE SOURCE BOUNDARY  
**Runtime/STAGE-A-A2 activation:** No

## 1. Purpose

This inventory establishes what PPIA-11 may legitimately use before encounter methodology is designed. It intentionally separates recovered/source mechanics, completed inherited contracts, verified benchmark observations, PPIA-11-authored methodology and unresolved gaps.

## 2. Governed balance evidence

The starting quantitative/behavioral anchor is the completed **8D-007 Golden Test Corpus and Balance Harness**.

Verified bounded evidence:

- 20 source datasets / 19,199 promoted records in the governed source corpus;
- 18 representative coverage domains;
- 36 deterministic golden fixtures, selected as bounded first/last eligible representatives;
- 24 deterministic scenario definitions;
- 72 scenario executions in completion governance;
- observation areas: output, cost, resource efficiency, survivability, action economy, range, duration, reliability, compatibility and complexity;
- 36 non-destructive recommendations;
- 7 mutation-sensitivity cases;
- zero installation residue, zero blocking observations and no source-truth mutation at completion.

The peer-group/target-band contract is explicitly **within-domain deterministic comparison**. It is not evidence for one cross-domain power number.

The 24 scenario IDs cover install/uninstall, activation/use, equipment/output, ammunition loading/consumption, software execution, attach/detach, life support, attunement, movement, power/action, power/movement, service, production, grant/revoke, effect resolution, prepare/cast, bind/unbind, store/release, place/remove and trigger/resolve cycles. These are benchmark building blocks, not a complete Encounter simulator.

## 3. Inherited completed experience contracts

### PPIA-02 — Creature & NPC Experience

The completed packet provides permission-safe creature/NPC Definition, placement, live-instance, encounter-preparation and runtime boundaries. Its encounter-balance output is advisory. It explicitly prohibits inventing CR, stat blocks or mechanics for incomplete/deferred creature evidence.

### PPIA-03 — Items, Equipment & Inventory

The implementation-ready specification preserves Item Definition/Asset Instance separation, equipment/readiness, ammunition/charges/consumption, durability/condition, special Assets, provenance and Action-coupled atomic resource mutation. The retained direct Item surface is 9 governed CSV datasets / 5,389 rows and 13 PDFs / 218 pages, but PPIA-11 inherits the completed contract rather than reclassifying those records. Numerical balance conclusions explicitly route to PPIA-11.

### PPIA-04 — Vehicles, Mecha & Starships

The implementation-ready specification covers 3 governed CSV datasets / 5,628 rows and 24 retained PDFs / 608 pages, while preserving vehicle Definition/instance/configuration, crew/stations, systems, semantic movement/environment, Resources, damage and Action/resource atomicity. Source-unspecified capacity or capability remains unknown. Cross-scale encounter analysis cannot treat vehicles, mecha or spacecraft as numerically equivalent to characters without governing interaction rules.

### PPIA-05 — Species, Forms & Character Biology

The implementation-ready specification preserves Species/Form identity, transformations, Adaptations, senses, movement, physiology, compatibility and vulnerabilities. Its direct basis is 29 PDFs / 654 pages plus 6 supporting environment/Adaptation PDFs / 233 pages, with governed mixed Ability surfaces explicitly protected from automatic biological interpretation. PPIA-11 receives encounter-calibration responsibility but not biology authority.

### PPIA-07 — Rune Construction

The Rune system is a governed compositional design. Its **SCI measures structural/cognitive complexity, not power**; CSL is a structural stability-attention signal and is not failure probability, damage or a balance formula. No universal rune mana/cost formula exists. PPIA-11 may use rune benchmark evidence and owning-rule outputs, but may not convert SCI/CSL to threat.

### PPIA-08 — Campaign / Scene / Session Authoring

PPIA-08 gives PPIA-11 the authored encounter context: Campaign/Scene/Session snapshots, Encounter references, objectives, hazards, semantic locations, environment, triggers and placement. Map pixels and dungeon geometry do not determine Encounter legality, threat or balance. PPIA-11 retains final encounter/balance calibration authority while PPIA-08 retains Scene/Session state.

## 4. Five evidence classes

1. **source_truth** — recovered/retained owning-domain facts and rules; immutable to PPIA-11.
2. **inherited_contract** — completed governed contracts defining identities, projections, actions and handoffs.
3. **observed_benchmark** — deterministic fixture/scenario results, observations and target-band comparisons; evidence, not canon.
4. **authored_methodology** — PPIA-11 encounter-analysis vocabulary, uncertainty, handbook and calibration procedure; must be labeled as design.
5. **unresolved_gap** — explicit missing/unknown/conflicted information; never silently defaulted.

## 5. Foundation encounter-factor design surface

PPIA-11 may author methodology that separately inspects:

- encounter identity and intent;
- participants, sides and roles;
- capability profile;
- output pressure;
- survivability/recovery;
- action economy;
- resource pressure;
- range/mobility/access;
- environment/terrain;
- objectives/time/failure conditions;
- hazards/traps/external pressure;
- mixed composition and scale;
- boss/elite/solo structure;
- waves/reinforcements/escalation;
- retreat/disengagement/alternative resolution;
- information and uncertainty;
- benchmark fixture/scenario evidence;
- observed results and valid peer/target-band comparisons;
- uncertainty bands and calibration;
- provenance/recommendation history.

These factors are not automatically weighted and do not produce a universal challenge number.

## 6. Explicit gaps and non-assumptions

The inspected evidence does **not** establish:

- one universal CR/encounter-level/power-score system;
- one formula converting HP, damage, action count, Resources, environment and objectives to one scalar;
- cross-scale equivalence among character, creature, vehicle, mecha and spacecraft encounters;
- a universal party-composition model;
- a universal distance/resource/action economy;
- boss-only mechanics, phase mechanics or reinforcement rules independent of owning rules;
- guaranteed encounter outcomes or guaranteed balance;
- automatic source rewrites from benchmark observations;
- authority for AI to alter canonical rules or accepted game state.

Those gaps become explicit uncertainty, calibration needs or later owner/rules decisions rather than fabricated defaults.
