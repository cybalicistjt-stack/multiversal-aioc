## Purpose

This milestone converts the verified PPIA-15 Foundation coverage-gap model into a stable nonduplicate regression library. It does not inflate coverage by repackaging inherited cases and it does not create application, permission, mutation, Pack or canonical-content authority.

## Locked surface

- 24 stable expanded regression scenario contracts.
- All 18 required awkward families are represented.
- Ordinary GM modification remains a protected inherited baseline; PPIA-15 adds only the stale-version composition, not a clone of the baseline case.
- 12 Inspector projection groups.
- 20 action/reference contracts.
- 40 new synthetic noncanonical IAR cases.
- 32 verified Foundation cases remain inherited, producing 72 effective PPIA-15 cases at this milestone.
- P15-GAP-001 / inherited P14-GAP-001 / P13-GAP-001 / MV-IA-F024 remains open and unsupported.

## Scenario-library rule

A scenario is additive only when a material test dimension changes what may be authoritative, visible, recoverable or prohibited. A new title, ID, packaging wrapper, viewport or execution copy is not additive by itself.

Every stable scenario records its nearest inherited anchors, Foundation case binding, material differential, governing domains, role/context/channel/device/interaction/accessibility/connection dimensions, synthetic fixture identity, operation, deterministic authoritative expectation, Inspector projection group, action/reference contracts, recovery/conflict expectation, provenance class and forbidden outcomes.

## Inspector projection boundary

The 12 projection groups cover stable object identity, permission/reveal/entitlement state, version conflict, Campaign-local provenance, source-only Definitions, Vehicle relations, Relationship secrets, crafting/approval recovery, bounded inventory scale, dense Creature/NPC targeting, unusual morphology and offline/recovery/source-gap states.

Permission and entitlement filtering always precede protected object resolution and all derivatives. Hidden and missing remain externally equivalent when existence is protected. Visual and nonvisual forms preserve the same authorized meaning and disclosure ceiling.

## Action/reference boundary

PPIA-15 creates no mutation protocol. Its actions are reads, local-only state handling, recovery/status lookups, conflict inspection, or presentations of already-governed owning-domain commands. Any upstream mutation remains subject to the owning domain's authorization, expected-version, operation-ID, idempotency and recovery rules.

`status-unknown` is not failure. Blind retry of an ambiguous mutation remains forbidden. Accepted durable Event and derived projection remain distinct. Offline/local state never fabricates authoritative mutation.

## Scale and accessibility

The 512-record inventory and 128-record Creature/NPC fixtures are deterministic case-local QA inputs only. They are not supported-capacity, performance, release or product promises.

The IAR corpus explicitly exercises keyboard-only, screen-reader/nonvisual, phone/touch, high-zoom/text-scale, reduced-motion and noncolor paths where the awkward workflow materially changes task sequencing or disclosure risk.

## PPIA-11 boundary

Encounter/analysis scenarios may report bounded uncertainty or an indeterminate result. They may not certify balanced, fair, safe, winnable, optimal or guaranteed outcomes.

## F024 source-gap boundary
