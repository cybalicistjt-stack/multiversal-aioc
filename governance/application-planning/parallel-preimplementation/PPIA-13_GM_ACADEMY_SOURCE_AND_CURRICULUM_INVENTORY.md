## Purpose

This bounded milestone inserts a curated GM-learning layer between the verified PPIA-13 Foundation and the Teaching Library / Inspector-Action-Reference milestone.

The owner wants the teaching library to draw **a little** from the GM Academy sources — Foundations, Intermediate, Advanced, Worldbuilding and GMing Multiversal — without turning normal onboarding into a mandatory textbook or inventing missing Multiversal lesson content.

Six project-source PDFs were reviewed visually and textually:

Filename, page count and SHA-256 provenance are locked in `PPIA-13_GM_ACADEMY_SOURCE_MANIFEST_v0.1.0.json`.

## Curriculum extraction

The Academy index contains **53 top-level modules**:

- 9 Foundations modules;
- 8 Intermediate modules;
- 8 Advanced modules;
- 10 Worldbuilding modules;
- 18 Multiversal GMing & System Mastery modules.

The first four tracks have developed PDF material. The Multiversal track exists only as an outline in `Academy index.PDF`; it is **not** treated as developed lesson content.

The initial teaching-library adoption is deliberately curated to **24 source-backed modules** across the four developed tracks. The remaining developed modules stay available as optional deep-dive/reference candidates. Academy completion never gates normal application use.

## Delivery model

The GM Academy is an optional progressive track:

`GM Foundations → Intermediate GM → Advanced GM → Worldbuilding → GMing Multiversal`

It may also surface as short contextual tips, checklists or examples on relevant GM/Creator screens. A contextual excerpt should be short and dismissible; a user-invoked deep dive may open the larger module while preserving return context.

The Academy teaches **how to GM**. PPIA-13 core teaching teaches **how to use Multiversal**. When combined, content must distinguish GM-craft guidance from canonical Multiversal mechanics and application behavior.

## Initial curated source-backed material

The first library pass may draw from:

- **Foundations:** Game Mastering 101; narrative design; player psychology and engagement; improvisation/storytelling; mechanics/rule systems; session planning/encounter design.
- **Intermediate:** advanced GM techniques; campaign/story arcs; immersive NPCs/villains; Session Zero/player expectations; pacing/tension/player investment; conflict resolution/GM authority.
- **Advanced:** epic/multi-group play; horror/mystery/psychological play; sandbox vs narrative campaigns; advanced combat/tactical encounters; collaborative/shared GMing.
- **Worldbuilding:** fundamentals; lore/mythology; civilization design; history/politics; magic systems; alien/non-human cultures; maps/regions/cities.

This selection is an adoption priority, not a declaration that the unselected Academy material is invalid.

## Multiversal outline boundary

The 18 `MULTIVERSAL GMING & SYSTEM MASTERY` headings are an owner-approved curriculum scaffold: Multiversal system mastery, advanced combat, customizing/hacking, progression/player choices, sandbox play, inter-reality travel and causal complexity, narrative entanglement, side content, improvisation, pacing, faction play, economics/politics, horror/psychological play, warfare, ethical dilemmas, multiplayer events, long-term engagement and creating a living Multiversal setting.

They are **outline-only**. User-facing lessons require grounding in canonical Multiversal repository/game-framework sources. Unsupported claims remain explicit gaps rather than being filled from generic RPG knowledge.

Priority grounding domains include DB-004/canonical rules, PPIA-07 Rune Magic, PPIA-08 Campaign/Scene/Session authoring, PPIA-09 Investigation/Mystery, PPIA-11 Encounter Lab, canonical Species/setting sources, F006 approval semantics, F025 onboarding/help and current AI governance.

## World Creation tables boundary

`World Creation tables.PDF` can support optional synthetic exercises and examples. Random results are prompts/drafts only. They do not automatically become canonical setting truth or bypass the owning content-authority workflow.
