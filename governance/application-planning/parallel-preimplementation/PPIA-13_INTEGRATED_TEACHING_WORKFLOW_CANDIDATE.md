## Authority and safety

Teaching is explanatory. It does not create or modify gameplay mechanics, Campaign truth, Character truth, permissions, entitlement, Pack lifecycle, canonical content or production state.

Every derivative is permission-filtered before discovery, search, counts, ranking, autocomplete, examples, tutorial branches, diagnostics, export, notifications or optional AI context.

Persisted teaching state is limited to progress, presentation preferences and bookmarks. All authoritative teaching-state writes use `P13-MUT-001` with authorization, `expected_version`, `operation_id`, stale-write rejection, idempotent operation status, and a durable persistence receipt. Offline changes are local/pending only.

## GM Academy and Multiversal grounding

GM Academy is optional and never capability-gating. The 24 curated developed-source modules may provide GM craft, checklists and contextual guidance. They cannot override Multiversal rules or feature authority.

The 18 `GMing Multiversal & System Mastery` headings remain curriculum scaffolding. A factual Multiversal lesson may render only when canonical grounding sources support the claim. Unsupported headings remain explicit gaps rather than being filled from generic RPG knowledge.

World Creation table output and tutorial-Campaign material remain synthetic/noncanonical until separately promoted by an owning content authority.

## Pack and microcopy boundaries

`P13-GAP-001` remains unresolved: the referenced F024 Pack Lifecycle feature packet is absent. Teaching may explain evidenced Pack/source/version/lock/entitlement context but may not invent lifecycle commands or migration semantics.

PPIA-14 owns final state-by-state error/recovery/permission microcopy. PPIA-13 owns concept teaching, evidence boundaries and safe handoff categories only.

## Accessibility and optional AI

Every required workflow preserves equivalent keyboard, touch, screen-reader, mobile single-focus, high-zoom, reduced-motion and noncolor meaning, actions and completion evidence. Optional AI receives only the already-filtered teaching projection; a zero-AI path provides the same required facts and actions.
