2. Hidden state cannot be inferred from result counts, disabled controls, missing-topic language, prerequisites, diagnostics, ranking, or examples.
3. Local draft/autosave, submitted command, accepted durable Event, displayed projection, and ambiguous network outcome remain distinct taught states.
4. Tutorial-Campaign and World Creation exercise output remain synthetic/noncanonical unless separately promoted by the owning content authority.
5. The unresolved F024 Pack Lifecycle packet remains an explicit source gap; only already-evidenced Pack/source/version/lock/entitlement/compatibility facts may be taught.
6. PPIA-14 owns complete final denial/error/recovery/permission microcopy.
7. Mobile, keyboard, touch, screen-reader, high-zoom, reduced-motion, noncolor, and zero-AI paths preserve equivalent required meaning and actions.
8. No application runtime, STAGE-A-A2, release, deployment, tester access, paid service, or production credential is activated.
