## Locked boundaries

- Relationship edges are directional. Reciprocal state requires a second explicit edge or explicit paired-edge contract.
- Relationship, standing/reputation, influence, social status, mood, intent and stance remain distinct. Membership, rank, office and permission remain distinct.
- Objective truth, belief, claim, rumor, knowledge and secret remain separately typed. Deception can affect belief without rewriting objective truth; Persuasion is not mind control.
- Permission filtering occurs before hidden existence, topology, counts, labels, search, suggestions, exports, notifications, realtime, diagnostics or AI context.
- There is **no universal relationship, standing/reputation, influence or social DC scale**. Source/profile-specific scale authority must be explicit.
- Faction standing change requires an attributable source Event and a plausible information path.
- Influence is not standing. Membership/rank/office/service access/permission do not automatically imply one another.
- Persistent Social Mode consequences use one accepted **atomic Event group or none**. Owning domains retain their own state; reversible correction uses compensating Events rather than history deletion.
- External Item/NPC/Asset/Resource/Location/Project/Contract references use stable ownerDomain/object/version identity and never transfer ownership into PPIA-10.
- Every authoritative mutation follows the shared authorization + `expected_version` + `operation_id` protocol; ambiguous results require status/current-version lookup before retry.
- Graph/canvas position is nonauthoritative. Ordered/list/table **semantic nonvisual** equivalents preserve the same authorized meaning and actions.
- AI-generated social/faction material is **proposal-only**. Review itself changes nothing; only an explicit authorized accepted-diff mutation may enter normal governed flows.
