# PPIA-12 — Dominix Escalation Owner Clarification

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Authority:** direct owner setting clarification  
**Owning domain:** PPIA-12 World & Setting authority  
**Purpose:** preserve setting presentation and escalation semantics without inventing mechanics

## Owner clarification

Dominix in the **FMR** is **crazy and loud**, but that is **not the universal everyday presentation of Dominix**.

Across Dominix, **everyone has the potential** to **spiral out**. Dominix is not a setting where it is difficult for an ordinary person to find themselves inside a story. A person can become caught up in events, the **story can escalate**, and their **abilities can escalate alongside it**.

That possibility is broadly present, but constant escalation is not ordinary daily life. The **great majority** of people are **trying to live around that** possibility, manage their lives despite it, or **avoid that** escalation altogether.

The FMR therefore represents a place/context where Dominix's escalation potential is especially visible, intense, crazy and loud. It must not be used to flatten the whole setting into permanent maximal chaos.

## Interpretation boundaries

- The potential to spiral out is broadly available; it is not restricted by this ruling to a rare chosen class of people.
- Broad potential does not mean every person is currently escalating.
- Ordinary people, ordinary routines and attempts to avoid escalation are important parts of the setting texture.
- Story escalation and ability escalation can accompany one another, but this ruling **does not invent a universal escalation formula**, automatic advancement rate, numeric trigger, power ladder or guaranteed outcome.
- FMR presentation may be used as a high-intensity example, not as the universal baseline for every Dominix place, scene or NPC.
- Existing Dominix chronology, language-stage visibility and other governed setting authorities remain intact.

## Downstream use

World/Setting authoring, GM construction, encounter/adventure generation, NPC portrayal, AI context and future application integrations should preserve this distinction: Dominix makes extraordinary escalation possible for essentially anyone, while most people are still trying to conduct ordinary life without being swept into it.
