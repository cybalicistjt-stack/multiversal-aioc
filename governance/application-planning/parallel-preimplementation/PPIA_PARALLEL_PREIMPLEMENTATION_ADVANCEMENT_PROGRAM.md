## Purpose

PPIA governed substantial project-advancing work that could be completed without a repository-capable Codex checkout while STAGE-A-A2 and other exact-checkout work waited for their required execution surface.

PPIA was a parallel preparation/content/design/acceptance track. Its completion does not replace, supersede, activate, or complete STAGE-A-A2, the Design Standards exact-byte ingestion track, the Apple track, a later Stage A item, Internal Alpha release, deployment, or public release.

The program was intentionally large enough to remain useful beyond the immediate pre-Sunday gap. Its outputs reduce later implementation stops, reinterpretation, missing-content discovery, fixture creation, UX ambiguity, and acceptance-design work.

## Operating rules

1. Source and repository evidence control. Do not invent canonical game facts merely to fill a gap.
2. Separate source-backed completion from authored proposal, inference, synthetic QA material, and unresolved unknowns.
3. Preserve stable IDs, provenance, Definition/Variant/Placement/Live Instance/Snapshot/Projection distinctions, permissions, hidden-information boundaries, and source-only/noncanonical status.
4. No application runtime mutation, A2 activation, new runtime dependency, deployment, paid service, credential use, release authorization, tester activation, or canonical content promotion is implied by PPIA.
5. Each tranche may be decomposed into bounded work packages. Completion requires its declared deliverables and evidence, not merely discussion.
6. Prefer work that becomes an implementation input, fixture, acceptance corpus, authoring standard, UX contract, content repair register, or governed reference package.
7. When a tranche depends on unavailable exact source bytes, record the source gap rather than reconstructing the missing material.
8. Use `PPIA_PROGRAM_BACKLOG.json` for tranche completion state and exact ordering. The roadmap remains the milestone/dependency authority rather than an activity log.

## Tranches

### PPIA-01 — Content Quality & Missing-Information Closure

Audit canonical game content for records that exist but are incomplete, thin, inconsistent, unresolved, orphaned, duplicated, ambiguously named, or difficult for later UI/rules presentation.

Coverage includes Abilities, Items, Creatures/NPCs, Species, Vehicles, Environments/Adaptations, Settings, Rules, Effects, Conditions, Resources, Progressions, and cross-domain relationships.

Required outputs:
- domain-by-domain content-quality inventory;
- gap taxonomy separating source absence, recoverable omission, ambiguity, conflict, orphaned relation, presentation deficit, and authored-proposal opportunity;
- prioritized repair backlog;
- source-backed repairs where evidence is sufficient;
- explicit unresolved register where evidence is not sufficient;
- traceability to source/provenance and affected future feature surfaces.

### PPIA-02 — Creature & NPC Experience
