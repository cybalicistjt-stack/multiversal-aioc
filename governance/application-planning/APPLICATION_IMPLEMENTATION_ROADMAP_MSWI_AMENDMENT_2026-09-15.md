## Owner direction

The combined research study covering The Planet Crafter, FOURSAVENT, GRAFT, DREADMOOR, NECROPOLIS, Nivalis Nights, Elderfeast, Sirens, Notmads, Punk Runner, Divinity, Billie Bust Up, JOIN US and Bloody Hell Hotel is to become one Multiversal-native implementation family rather than remain informal research.

## OPS3 placement

OPS3 was rechecked before registration.

- `operations/BOOTSTRAP.md` remains the only operational door.
- `operations/CURRENT.json` remains the only mutable selector of current work.
- The active product frontier is not changed by this amendment.
- At registration time, `MAS-01` remains `selected_not_started`, with no implementation authority until a subsequent owner `Continue` governed-starts it.
- MSWI is future planned work only and receives no current work item, implementation branch or implementation authority.
- Cross-program placement is recorded in `ROADMAP_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.json`, which is consulted by the OPS3 successor rule before future selection.

## Effective future placement

The new future sequence at this portion of the roadmap is:

`GPR → MERA → MBES → MSWI → SMB-08 → SMB-09`

MSWI has a hard dependency on MBES completion and explicit start requirements for the relevant GPR, MNCS, DPL, MIB, Project and World/Environment/Organization contracts. `SMB-08` now explicitly requires the `MSWI-18` golden proof.

Legacy `activation_after` and `successor` fields remain compatibility/planning projections and cannot override the dependency graph or `CURRENT.json`.

## Why MSWI is separate

The benchmark study exposed an integration gap rather than a need to recreate existing verticals.

Already-owned capabilities remain with their established programs/domains:

- DPL: profession activities, enterprise/staffing/growth, arts/performance, cybernetics/symbiotes/biotech and life-simulation foundations;
- MNCS: creature ecology, habitat/lifecycle/populations and ecology change;
- GPR: reusable gameplay patterns and loop composition;
- MERA: engineering/refit/assembly;
- MBES: construction, utilities, logistics, production, settlement and built-environment reactive-world hooks;
- MCS/MAS/MCCS/MSAS/MRCS: their existing map, adventure, presentation, audio and rules/content authoring domains.

MSWI owns only the cross-domain systemic-worldplay integration layer and diagnostics needed to make those capabilities react to one another in persistent, attributable play.

## Execution-family design

The family contains `MSWI-01` through `MSWI-18` in strict order.

The program backlog records:

- 24-minute tranche execution target;
- 8-minute minimum closeout reserve;
- 14–16 minute ordinary active implementation estimates;
- split-before-start when the reserve is not credible;
- selected-tranche dependency-closure-only preload;
- one-`Continue` completion through validation, verified closeout and next selection once a tranche is future governed-started;
- no unchanged deterministic retries.

These planning constraints support the OPS3 critical-path discipline but do not themselves grant authority.
