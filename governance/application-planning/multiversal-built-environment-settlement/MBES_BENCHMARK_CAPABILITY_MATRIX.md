## Purpose

This matrix extracts public capability classes from housing, construction, survival, factory, colony and city-building games. It is planning evidence only. Vendor behavior is not Multiversal source authority, and public availability is not permission to copy protected implementations, assets, UI expression, maps or data.

The MBES target is broader than any one benchmark: the same governed system must scale from personal rooms to bases, production sites, settlements, cities and regional networks while remaining coupled to Multiversal World/Environment, Character, Project, Economy, Inventory, Organization and strategic consequence owners.

| Hostile-environment construction | Subnautica, ONI, NMS | site conditions change required materials, sealing, life support, maintenance and survivability |
| Resident integration | RimWorld, Dwarf Fortress, Stardew | housing, workplaces and services matter because residents/crews actually use them; MBES references Character/NPC truth rather than cloning it |

| World-context variation | NMS, Subnautica, ONI, Against the Storm | the same blueprint can have different viability/cost/consequences under different environmental conditions |

## Owner-directed Oara synthesis

The owner added a required reference case on 2026-09-11: **Oara has planetary sentience that responds to pollution and over-development.**

The benchmark lesson from Eco and other consequence-driven builders is therefore generalized rather than copied as one ecological meter:

1. MBES emits explicit development/externality observations from governed construction and operations.
2. World/Environment authority interprets those observations under the active world profile.
3. The owning world can return consequences/responses through governed Action/Event/World seams.
4. Oara uses this generic pathway for planetary-sentience responses.
5. Another world may ignore the same signal, respond only biologically, invoke magical/spiritual consequences, alter weather, trigger political consequences or use an entirely different owner-defined model.

Recovered Oara material contains machinery/pollution concern signals, but incomplete recovered text is not used to invent exact sentience thresholds or reactions.

## Negative benchmark lessons

MBES deliberately avoids several common limitations:

1. **One fixed grid is insufficient.** Structure placement may be snapped, modular or freeform according to the authored system and scale.
2. **Maximum-detail simulation everywhere is unnecessary.** Summary/detail resolution must preserve identity and expand only when play needs it.
3. **A room name is not a rule.** Function derives from accepted capabilities and owner-defined requirements.
4. **Modern-city assumptions are not universal.** Zoning, roads, sewage, electrical power, taxation or labor markets exist only where the setting/rules use them.
5. **Automation cannot become arbitrary scripting.** Condition→action logic stays bounded by owner operations, permissions and budgets.
6. **Pollution is not one universal scalar.** Externalities are typed owner inputs; worlds define their own carrying capacity and reactions.
7. **Construction geometry cannot silently mutate World truth.** MCS drawings/previews and MBES proposals require explicit governed commits.
8. **Blueprint sharing cannot erase rights/provenance.** ARI identity and licensing/use capability remain attached.
9. **Residents are not settlement resources.** Character/NPC autonomy, permissions, relationships and canonical state stay with their owners.
10. **The system is gameplay, not professional engineering software.** Structural/environment calculations are rules abstractions, not real-world safety guidance.

## Clean-room restrictions

This study follows PCM clean-room rules:

- use public documentation, public demos and lawful ordinary-use descriptions to identify problem definitions, workflows and capability requirements;
- do not decompile, reverse engineer, scrape protected services, recover private protocols or copy proprietary source;
- do not copy protected game assets, maps, building kits, save files, distinctive UI expression or sample settlements;
- any third-party code, SDK, asset or dataset proposed for direct incorporation requires separate ARI rights/license/provenance review;
- product names remain planning provenance and do not become Multiversal product identity.
