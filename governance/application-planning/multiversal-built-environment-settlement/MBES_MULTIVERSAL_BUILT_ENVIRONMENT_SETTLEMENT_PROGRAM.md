## Purpose

MBES is the player/GM built-environment and settlement gameplay/orchestration family. It turns accepted facility, map, material, Project/time, engineering, economy, organization and World/Environment contracts into playable construction, habitation, infrastructure and settlement development without becoming a duplicate owner ledger.

## Product doctrine

### Definition is not construction

A blueprint, MRCS definition, MCS drawing, preview placement, simulation output or AI suggestion is not a completed structure.

The governed lifecycle is:

`accepted definitions + owner-backed site/context + authorized Project/resources → proposal/preview → owner validation → committed operations/Events → built-environment projection → optional typed MSWI consequence routing`

### Variable resolution without duplicate identity

MBES may represent built environments at:

`settlement/district → site/parcel → structure → level/zone → space/room → component/fixture → connection/network`

Packet-06 multi-resolution semantics apply. Refinement/collapse changes active detail, not canonical identity or historical truth.

### Capability-derived spaces

Rooms/facilities derive capability from accepted components, network access, environment conditions, staffing/access prerequisites and governing definitions. Labels alone never grant mechanical capability.

### One generic owner per substrate

MBES consumes rather than duplicates:

- MERA engineering topology/interfaces/dependency/failure/repair;
- GPR + Action/Event generic runtime/replay;
- PCA-12/Packet-08 simulation/formal/flow/capacity analysis;
- Packet-07 preview/commit/explanation/intervention/recovery;
- MCS map/terrain/interior authoring;
- MIB-12 crafting/transformation/repair transactions;
- MIB-13/Economy prices/markets/contracts/property/economic truth;
- ICF production/cultivation truth;
- Character/MNCS/ODL/DPL people/household/jobs/staffing truth;
- reduced MSWI cross-domain consequence propagation.

MBES implements the built-environment schemas, adapters, UX and proof that remain after those absorptions.

## Reactive-world requirement

Construction and facility operation may emit explicit attributable externality observations/Events such as pollution, waste, heat, noise, habitat disruption, resource depletion, density, land conversion or hydrological alteration where definitions support them.

World/Environment/Reality owners decide whether and how the world responds. Oara's planetary sentience remains a required golden case demonstrating that equivalent development can produce a different owner-authorized response from a non-Oara reference world without any Oara-only MBES engine path.
