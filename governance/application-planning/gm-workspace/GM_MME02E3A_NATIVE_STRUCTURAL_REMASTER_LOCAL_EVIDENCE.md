## What actually changed: first native structural remaster

Under the existing Map Maker a new **Structural Map Remaster · E3-A** panel creates a **new editable copy** of the authored *The Old Vault*. It is not a static overlaid picture.

The Old Vault reference Entry Level had six similar rectangular rooms and a straight central passage. The remastered **new map ID** has **seven function-specific named chambers** surrounding an octagonal focal hall: Upper Shrine, Cartographer's Library, Garrison and Armory, Sanctum of Relics, Sealed Treasury, South Gate Vestibule and Octagonal Hall of the Watch. Actual source room polygons, wall segments/openings, doorway point features, **seven passage polylines**, irregular surrounding foundation and four central source columns are authored in native MMHT geometry. **23 small decorative image references** are attached separately to the copied draft, maintaining source-room anchors. No rules-based collision/cover/light/loot is inferred from these decorative images.

Crucial **cross-floor authority guard**: source `layout:stair:33` on level 0 is retained because level −1 and level +1 refer to its stable ID. Original 40 other-level source features and all three floor definitions are unchanged; the original reference's 73 feature records are unchanged after building and returning. New geometry has its own independent copy map ID and revision, rather than silently changing an existing GM Scene's source revision. Map Maker's own document validation accepts the copy and **Export draft JSON** contains its actual editable structure and decorative references.

The working asset library grows from **36 to 48** original project-generated concept sprites across four sheets, with **12 new architectural basics** (vault doors, arches, plinth, ruined wall, stairs, roofs, gate, bridge, balcony, fountain); not an invented 212-item pack. The project must still review rights/provenance and production art suitability. Small decoration placement is maintained rather than repeating E2's oversized objects.
