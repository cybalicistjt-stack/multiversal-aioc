## Comparable-tool criterion — not yet met

Current generated illustration is **not yet comparable** in asset richness/composition to polished work achievable in Inkarnate, Dungeondraft or Dungeon Alchemist. Verified official capabilities: Inkarnate's textured brush/stamp catalog/layers (https://help.inkarnate.com/articles/4018103-brush-tool, https://help.inkarnate.com/articles/2431733-stamp-tool); Dungeondraft built-in lighting/asset packs (https://dungeondraft.net/); Dungeon Alchemist furnished lit rooms and VTT export (https://www.dungeonalchemist.com/). Do not copy their copyrighted artwork or assert our graphics have parity.
