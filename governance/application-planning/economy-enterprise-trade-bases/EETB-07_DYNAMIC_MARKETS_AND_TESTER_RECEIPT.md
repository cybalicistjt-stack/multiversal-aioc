## Limits
No autonomous simulation, automatic demand reset, autonomous NPCs, automatic supply contracts, physical logistics, travel execution, or production access enforcement. GM direct ledger and supplier actions are deliberate overrides; editable GM snapshot timestamps are not cryptographic/authenticated and older transactions without timestamps are not retroactively locked. One-hour guard is not travel proof, and repeated profitable trades after campaign time may remain possible. Multi-unit forecast uses current marginal prices rather than simulating each unit's price changes; live forecasts do not enforce player-only information visibility, reports mark information stale separately. GM can change demand policy; intentional rule authoring not prevented. No separate investment/monthly event payout engine yet. Legacy subset import/reset can reject incompatible dynamic references; use full save. Direct file launch/localStorage persistence not independently tested; JSON fallback verified. Separate settlement building list unresolved. Source extraction retained, not published in public repo.
