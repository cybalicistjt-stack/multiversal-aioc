## Purpose

WCI takes the most useful problems addressed by worldbuilding/campaign platforms and solves them through Multiversal's existing canonical entities and systems instead of building a parallel article database.

It is not a clone of any vendor. Articles, timelines, family trees, diplomacy views, campaign dashboards and writing tools are projections/workspaces over governed World, Character, relationship, Adventure/Project, Event and creator content.

WCI consumes SSA's typed structural/world-space relationships where relevant. KFR follows WCI to give knowledge/familiarity an explicit governed relation rather than overloading notes, articles or Character prose.
