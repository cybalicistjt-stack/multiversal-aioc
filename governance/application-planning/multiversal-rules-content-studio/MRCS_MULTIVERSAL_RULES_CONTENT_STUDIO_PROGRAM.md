## Purpose

MRCS is the creator-facing environment for constructing governed reusable rules/content definitions through the existing Content Forge doctrine. It is not a canonical rules engine, runtime, simulator, specialist creator studio, or second content database.

Governing flow:

`owner-domain contracts → MRCS definition authoring → candidate definition graph → validation/evidence/provenance → governed content pack → reduced GPR/domain runtime consumption`

Definitions, drafts, previews, variants, templates, forms, placements, live instances and projections remain distinct.

## Thirteen bounded tranches

### MRCS-01 — Studio Workspace, Content Registry & Schema-Aware Forms
Implement the workspace shell, governed content-type registry, schema-driven forms, progressive disclosure, definition/instance distinctions, owner routing and draft-state model. Absorbs historical 01+02.

### MRCS-03 — Guided Forge Interviews, Templates, Clone/Fork & Advisory Authoring
Productize guided interviews, templates, lineage-safe clone/fork, save/resume, decide-later and optional advisory AI over the shared schema model.

### MRCS-04 — Rule Atom, Formula, Predicate, Selector & Expression Workbench
Implement bounded typed expressions, linting, references, preview and accessible structured alternatives without unrestricted scripting.

### MRCS-05 — Mechanical & Advancement Definition Authoring
Author Actions, Effects, Conditions, Resources, durations/triggers, Abilities/trees/prestige/prerequisites, Attributes, Skills, Knowledges, Proficiencies and advancement packages while preserving CAB and owner semantics. Absorbs historical 05+06+07 and remains a DAG milestone.

### MRCS-08 — Social, Identity, Species, Form & Template Package Authoring
Author Profession, Background, Culture, Faction, social, Species, Form, Archetype, Template, Variant and Trait packages with explicit inheritance/reference/compatibility behavior. Absorbs historical 08+09.

### MRCS-11 — Magic, Power & Casting-System Authoring
Author spells, powers, casting methods, resources and magic/supernatural-system packages over existing owners without creating a parallel magic runtime.

### MRCS-12 — Creature, Monster, NPC Role, Behavior & Combat-Package Authoring
Author creature/NPC taxonomy, behavior, action, defense, resource, habitat and role packages without becoming an NPC runtime or encounter instance owner.

### MRCS-13 — Item, Equipment, Vehicle, Mount, Companion, Construct & Modular-Component Authoring
Author equipment/loadout and platform/component definition families, slots, compatibility, actions/resources and linked entity references. Absorbs historical 10+13 and remains the MERA rotation milestone.

### MRCS-14 — Environment, Hazard, Encounter, Reward, Loot, Economy & World-Content Authoring
Author reusable environment/hazard/terrain/world definitions plus encounter/challenge/reward/loot/economy/service definitions without placing World state or executing encounters/economies. Absorbs historical 14+15 and remains the MBES rotation milestone.

### MRCS-16 — System Extension, House Rule, Scope, Compatibility & Override Authoring
Implement explicit core/setting/campaign/adventure/playtest scopes, precedence, compatibility, conflict diagnostics, enable/disable and rollback semantics.

### MRCS-17 — Dependency, Impact, Safe Refactoring, Balance & Evidence Diagnostics
Implement reference browsing, impact analysis, governed refactoring, owner-model/CAB harness adapters and balance evidence interpretation. Generic simulation/formal analysis stays with PCA-12/Packet 08. Absorbs historical 17+18.

### MRCS-19 — Import, Repair, Migration, Pack, Version, Review & Publication Lifecycle
Implement rule/content field mapping, legacy repair, migration, batch edit, pack compilation, dependency manifests, semantic versions, diffs, approvals, install/update/rollback and publication boundaries. Generic provenance/rights/version/review/import-export stays ARI/PCA. Absorbs historical 19+20.

### MRCS-21 — Golden Cross-Domain Definition Construction & Runtime Consumption Proof
Prove the complete reduced family over original/right-cleared Multiversal content, including guided/advanced parity, dependency-safe change, CAB/domain evidence, migration/pack lifecycle, runtime consumption, accessibility, local-first operation and ownership preservation.
