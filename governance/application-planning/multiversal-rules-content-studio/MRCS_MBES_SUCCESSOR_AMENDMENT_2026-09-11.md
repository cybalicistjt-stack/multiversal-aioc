# MRCS → MBES Successor Amendment — 2026-09-11

**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED FUTURE SUCCESSOR AMENDMENT  
**Implementation authority:** none

MRCS remains unchanged in scope and execution order, but its future successor is now MBES-01 rather than SMB-08.

Effective future sequence:

`MSAS-01..21 → MRCS-01..21 → MBES-01..24 → SMB-08`

Rationale: MRCS provides the rules/content/blueprint construction surface; MBES consumes those definitions in actual player/GM construction, environment-engineering and settlement gameplay before first-party library production begins.

This amendment does not activate MRCS or MBES and does not change current ARI work.
