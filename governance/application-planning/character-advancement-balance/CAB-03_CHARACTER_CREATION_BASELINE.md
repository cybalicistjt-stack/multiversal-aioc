# CAB-03 — Character-Creation Baseline

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Tranche:** CAB-03  
**State:** `completed_verified` candidate pending owner questionnaire and merge  
**Owner/final authority:** John Brandon Turner  

## 1. Purpose

Establish the supported starting-character baseline without reviving Ability Points, without mixing starting wealth into advancement XP, and without prematurely deciding tier architecture or final prices owned by later CAB tranches.

CAB-03 consumes:

- CAB-01 AP retirement;
- CAB-02 XP-economy reconstruction;
- CAB-02 owner decisions separating XP from ordinary assets and starting wealth;
- current Character creation/advancement architecture;
- recovered owner-authored Character Creation rules and creation PDFs;
- later source PDFs for attributes, backgrounds, health, knowledge, skills and proficiencies.

## 2. Authority-weighted source findings

### 2.1 Starting advancement XP

The recovered Character Creation source states repeatedly that a new Character starts with **1,300 XP**. In the underlying owner conversation, the owner explicitly corrected an assistant's incorrect 10,000-XP answer with: `Starting xp is 1300.`

**CAB-03 classification:** RECOVERED EXPLICIT OWNER RULE. No stronger source contradicts 1,300 XP as the normal starting advancement pool.

### 2.2 Starting wealth

CAB-Q02-01/Q02-04 now establish:

- advancement XP and starting wealth/equipment are separate budgets;
- there is no ordinary XP-to-credits conversion at creation;
- starting money/wealth is determined by GM/Campaign creation policy.

Historical Character Creation text that converts XP to Credits is therefore superseded.

### 2.3 Free starting foundation

The recovered owner-authored Character Creation rules specify the following free creation foundation:

- Character concept and identity cost no XP;
- base Species selection is free;
- Homeworld Type core benefits are free;
- Cultural Influence core benefits are free;
- Early Life Profession core benefits are free;
- five Ability Trees are selected free;
- Tier 1 access is free in those five trees;
- five Tier 1 Abilities are free from those starting trees;
- Tier 1 Knowledges may be granted free through background/source rules;
- unspent starting XP may be carried into play.

Subspecies, optional species/background features and additional capabilities may carry XP costs where their owning records say so.

**CAB-03 classification:** RETAINED RECOVERED OWNER-AUTHORED BASELINE, subject to the normal eligibility/prerequisite rules later governed by CAB-04/CAB-09.

### 2.4 Attributes

The Character Creation source and `Attribute Selection.PDF` contain an internally contradictory three-mode system:

- Regular: stated 12-point pool, 0 XP in the later Character Creation document;
- High: stated 20-point pool, 300 XP;
- Hyper: stated 30-point pool, 600 XP;
- the same document also says an additional +1 attribute costs 250 XP.

The templates do not reconcile with their stated pools:

- Regular Physical/Mental templates allocate 10, not 12;
- Regular Balanced allocates 16;
- High Physical/Mental templates allocate 14, not 20;
- High Balanced allocates 28;
- Hyper Physical/Mental templates allocate 22, not 30;
- Hyper Balanced allocates 42.

The example characters also use totals that differ from some templates.

The bundle prices also create an extreme price contradiction: buying 20 or 30 starting points for 300/600 XP cannot coexist coherently with a generic 250 XP per +1 rule without an explicit, very large creation discount.

**CAB-03 classification:** CREATION BASELINE CONFLICT. The three paid Attribute Modes cannot be accepted as written.

**CAB recommendation:** use one free starting allocation of **12 attribute points** on top of base 10 plus Species modifiers; treat Physical/Mental/Balanced as optional allocation guidance rather than separate mechanical bundles. Additional permanent attribute increases remain XP-purchased, with final pricing and caps owned by CAB-10/CAB-06.

### 2.5 Background

Recovered owner-authored rules establish three free core background choices:

1. Homeworld Type;
2. Cultural Influence;
3. Early Life Profession.

Optional background upgrades are XP-priced, commonly using 200 / 400 / 600 / 1,200 XP bands in the creation material. An earlier owner-authored version says up to **1,800 XP** may be spent customizing background, while the later rewritten Character Creation PDF omits that cap.

**CAB-03 classification:** CORE FREE BACKGROUND STRUCTURE RETAINED; 1,800-XP CATEGORY CAP CONFLICT/UNCERTAIN.

**CAB recommendation:** do not impose a separate universal 1,800-XP background cap. Background purchases are limited by the Character's available advancement XP, prerequisites, campaign policy and source eligibility. A campaign profile may impose a category cap if desired.

### 2.6 Health

`Health XP-buy 5-27-25.PDF` is later than the February Character Creation source and explicitly defines starting HP as:

`max(10, Constitution)`

with Species HP modifiers applied afterward. It also offers creation-only optional max-HP purchases of +5/+10/+15/+20 HP for 300/750/1,250/2,000 XP.

The concept of a creation-only durability purchase is source-supported, but the exact prices have not been tested against CAB's later calibration framework and the +20 option cannot be purchased from the normal 1,300-XP pool without an explicitly higher-start creation policy.

**CAB-03 classification:** SOURCE-SUPPORTED CREATION MECHANIC, PRICE CALIBRATION PENDING.

**CAB recommendation:** retain `max(10, Constitution)` plus Species modifiers as the starting HP baseline. Retain the possibility of spending creation XP for additional permanent HP, but mark the listed prices provisional until CAB-06/CAB-10-style breadth/value review.

### 2.7 Skills, Knowledges and proficiencies

The creation material permits XP spending in all three areas, but its simple creation prices conflict with specialized source documents:

- Character Creation says Skills improve at 250 XP per rank;
- `Skills 11-14-24.PDF` gives a progressive Skill Point schedule beginning at 100 XP and rising sharply;
- Character Creation says higher Knowledge tiers cost 500 XP each;
- Knowledge source material uses a tier model with its own progression language;
- Character Creation says proficiencies cost 750 XP each, while `Weapon Proficiency.PDF` separately defines proficiency/mastery states and XP-priced advanced options.

**CAB-03 classification:** SPEND CATEGORIES ARE VALID; FINAL PRICE/RANK ECONOMY IS NOT.

CAB-03 therefore allows these categories in the creation ledger but does not canonize their legacy numeric prices. CAB-10 owns reconciliation.

### 2.8 Ability Trees, tiers and Abilities

CAB-03 retains the creation grants of five starting trees, free Tier 1 access in those trees, and five free Tier 1 Abilities. It does **not** establish the recovered 1,000-XP extra-tree price or 1,000/2,500/5,000/10,000 tier-unlock schedule as final current rules; CAB-04/05/06 own those mechanics and prices.

Any creation-only eligibility rules for innate magic, biological features, advanced professions, special archetypes or setting-specific capabilities remain eligibility rules, not extra creation currency. CAB-09 owns final acquisition semantics.

### 2.9 Spells and magic

CAB-02 established that the structured spell corpus states 0 direct XP to learn the spell itself and instead points XP toward spellcasting capacity/slots under archetype rules. Older creation text saying to buy spells directly is therefore not accepted as a universal creation rule.

Starting spells may be granted by a tree, Ability, archetype or other owning rule. Final spellcasting progression economics remain deferred.

### 2.10 Equipment and ordinary assets

No ordinary equipment purchase uses advancement XP at creation. Starting inventory, money and asset limits are provided by GM/Campaign creation policy or an owning rules profile.

Character-bound progression objects that are mechanically part of the Character rather than ordinary transferable property may still have XP costs where explicitly governed.

## 3. Recommended default creation baseline

The following is the CAB-03 recommended default game profile, with owner-gated conflicts called out separately below.

### Free/granted foundation

1. Start all six attributes at 10.
2. Apply Species modifiers.
3. Select a base Species free.
4. Select Homeworld Type, Cultural Influence and Early Life Profession core packages free.
5. Allocate a single free 12-point attribute pool. **Owner confirmation required.**
6. Starting HP = `max(10, Constitution)` plus Species modifiers.
7. Select five eligible Ability Trees free.
8. Gain Tier 1 access in those five trees.
9. Select five free eligible Tier 1 Abilities from those trees.
10. Receive any source-defined free Tier 1 Knowledges, starter spells, proficiencies, Actions, Resources or other grants from Species/background/tree/Ability records.
11. Starting money/equipment budget is set separately by GM/Campaign creation policy.

### Starting advancement budget

- Default starting advancement XP: **1,300 XP**.
- XP is not money.
- XP need not be fully spent during creation.
- Unspent XP remains on the Character for later advancement.

### XP-purchased creation options

Creation XP may be spent on eligible permanent Character development including, as governed by its owning source and later CAB calibration:

- Subspecies or optional Species features;
- background upgrades;
- additional attribute increases;
- optional permanent starting HP increases;
- additional Abilities or tree/tier access;
- Skills;
- Knowledge advancement;
- proficiencies/mastery where applicable;
- spellcasting capacity or other magic progression where applicable;
- other explicitly Character-bound progression.

It is not spent on ordinary starting gear, vehicles, drones, refits or upkeep.

## 4. Creation ledger model

CAB-03 recommends that the Character creation transaction visibly separates:

- `granted_free` — core free selection or source grant;
- `selected_free` — free choice within an allowed creation allowance;
- `xp_purchased` — permanent Character advancement bought from starting XP;
- `campaign_granted` — GM/Campaign starting grant that does not consume XP;
- `wealth_purchased` — ordinary asset/equipment bought from the separate Campaign wealth budget;
- `deferred` — valid option intentionally left unpurchased;
- `unavailable` — prerequisite/eligibility/entitlement/policy prevents selection.

Every result retains stable source IDs and grant reasons under the current Character architecture.

## 6. Boundaries

CAB-03 does not:

- price or reprice all creation options;
- define final tier access;
- decide post-creation acquisition eligibility;
- define Intelligence/Wisdom learning-speed mechanics;
- define XP award pacing;
- define respec/refund economics;
- authorize application implementation.
