## 1. Purpose

Establish the governed starting-Character baseline without reviving Ability Points, mixing starting wealth into advancement XP, or prematurely finalizing numeric economies owned by later CAB tranches.

CAB-03 consumes CAB-01 AP retirement, CAB-02 XP-economy reconstruction and owner decisions, current Character creation/advancement architecture, recovered owner-authored Character Creation rules, and specialized attribute/background/health/knowledge/skill/proficiency sources.

## 2. Governed normal-start baseline

### Advancement XP

- **1,300 XP is the current normal-start default advancement grant.**
- A GM/Campaign creation policy may explicitly choose another starting XP grant for veteran, high-power, low-power, unusual, or special campaigns.
- A Campaign override changes the starting grant only; it does not silently change Ability, attribute, Skill, tier, or other advancement prices.
- The 1,300-XP default is deliberately **recalibratable**. CAB-06, CAB-14, CAB-16 and CAB-21 must test whether it remains appropriate after the advancement system is better calibrated.
- Unspent starting XP carries forward into play.

The recovered Character Creation source repeatedly states 1,300 XP, and the owner explicitly corrected an earlier assistant-generated 10,000-XP claim. The 10,000-XP claim is rejected.

### Starting wealth and equipment

- Starting money/equipment is a **separate GM/Campaign-defined creation budget**.
- There is no ordinary XP-to-money or money-to-XP conversion at creation.
- Ordinary equipment, vehicles, drones, refits and upkeep do not consume Character XP.
- Character-bound progression objects may use XP only where an owning rule explicitly makes them permanent Character advancement rather than ordinary transferable property.

### Species and background foundation

The following normal creation foundation is free unless a specific owning rule says otherwise:

- base Species selection;
- Homeworld Type core benefits;
- Cultural Influence core benefits;
- Early Life Profession core benefits.

Subspecies, optional Species features, background upgrades, setting-linked developments, and other non-core options may carry XP costs where their owning rules provide them.

There is **no universal 1,800-XP background customization cap**. Optional background purchases use available XP and obey source eligibility/prerequisites. Campaign policy may impose a category cap where desired.

### Attributes

Normal/default creation uses:

1. begin each of the six core attributes at 10;
2. apply explicit Species modifiers;
3. allocate one **free 12-point attribute pool**;
4. purchase any further permanent increases using the later-governed attribute XP economy.

Physical / Mental / Balanced may remain as example distributions or guidance, not separate current numeric economies.

The recovered Regular / High / Hyper concept may survive as **optional alternate Campaign starting-power packages**, but the recovered numeric packages are invalid as written. Their point totals, examples and 300/600-XP bundle prices conflict with the same source's 250-XP-per-+1 language and create potential bulk-purchase arbitrage. Before any Regular/High/Hyper package becomes usable, CAB-06/CAB-10 must redesign its point totals, XP price, starting-XP relationship and limits, and CAB-14/CAB-21 must benchmark the result against the normal-start profile.

### Starting HP

- Base starting HP is `max(10, Constitution)` plus explicit Species modifiers and other governed grants.
- Characters may invest starting advancement XP in additional **permanent HP** during creation.
- The recovered +5 / +10 / +15 / +20 HP purchase concept is retained.
- The recovered 300 / 750 / 1,250 / 2,000-XP prices are provisional and must be recalibrated in CAB-06 against attributes, defenses, resistances, healing, action economy and other durability options.

### Ability Trees and Abilities

The supported normal-start grants are:

- choose **five eligible Ability Trees free**;
- gain **Tier 1 access free** in those five trees;
- choose **five eligible Tier-1 Abilities free** from those starting trees.

These are creation grants, not XP purchases. CAB-03 does not finalize extra-tree prices, higher-tier unlock prices, prior-tier count gates, or general Ability prices; CAB-04/05/06 own those questions.

Creation-only access restrictions for innate magic, biological capabilities, advanced professions, special archetypes and setting-specific abilities remain eligibility/acquisition rules rather than extra currencies. CAB-09 owns final acquisition semantics.

### Knowledges, Skills and proficiencies

These remain valid advancement categories at creation where allowed by source rules, but CAB-03 does not canonize conflicting legacy prices.

- Character Creation gives simplified Skill, Knowledge and proficiency prices.
- Specialized Skill/Knowledge/proficiency sources use different progression structures.
- CAB-10 owns reconciliation of those economies.

Source-defined free Tier-1 Knowledges, proficiencies or other grants remain free grants rather than charges against starting XP.

### Magic and spells

CAB-02 established that the structured spell corpus states 0 direct XP to learn the spell itself and points XP toward spellcasting capacity/slots under owning archetype rules. Older creation language that treats every spell as a general direct-XP purchase is therefore not a universal rule.

Starting spells may be granted by a tree, Ability, archetype, Species, background or other owning rule. Final spellcasting progression economics remain deferred to CAB-04/CAB-06 and owning magic rules.

## 3. Creation ledger model

Character creation must visibly distinguish:

- `granted_free` — automatic core/source grant;
- `selected_free` — a free choice inside a creation allowance;
- `xp_purchased` — permanent Character advancement bought from starting XP;
- `campaign_granted` — GM/Campaign starting grant that does not consume XP;
- `wealth_purchased` — ordinary asset/equipment bought from the separate wealth budget;
- `deferred` — valid option intentionally left unpurchased;
- `unavailable` — prerequisite, eligibility, entitlement or Campaign policy prevents selection.

Every result retains stable source IDs, provenance, grant reason and accepted Character-history evidence under the current Character architecture.

## 4. Normal-start summary

A normal new Character therefore receives:

1. 1,300 starting advancement XP, subject to later CAB recalibration and explicit Campaign override;
2. attributes starting at 10, then Species modifiers, then one free 12-point allocation pool;
3. free base Species;
4. free Homeworld Type, Cultural Influence and Early Life Profession core selections;
5. starting HP of `max(10, Constitution)` plus governed modifiers/grants;
6. five free eligible Ability Trees;
7. free Tier-1 access in those trees;
8. five free eligible Tier-1 Abilities;
9. source-defined free Knowledges, spells, proficiencies, Actions, Resources or other grants;
10. a separate GM/Campaign-defined starting money/equipment budget;
11. the ability to spend starting XP on eligible permanent Character development;
12. carry-forward of unspent XP into play.

## 5. XP-purchased creation options

Subject to later pricing and eligibility rules, starting XP may be spent on permanent Character development including:

- Subspecies or optional Species features;
- background upgrades;
- additional attribute increases;
- creation-only permanent HP increases;
- additional Abilities or tree/tier access;
- Skills;
- Knowledge advancement;
- proficiencies/mastery;
- spellcasting capacity or other magic progression;
- other explicitly Character-bound progression.

It is not spent on ordinary starting gear or other mundane assets.
