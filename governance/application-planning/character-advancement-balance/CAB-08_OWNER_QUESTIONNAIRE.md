# CAB-08 — Owner Questionnaire

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Purpose:** resolve six stacking/synergy policy choices that materially affect CAB-09+ acquisition, modifier, corpus-audit, and balance work. Unanswered items remain unresolved and do not silently default.

## CAB-Q08-01 — Reapplying the same named mechanical effect

**Decision impact:** VERY HIGH  
**CAB recommendation:** **A**.

A. **Recommended:** the same named mechanical effect does not increase its magnitude by repeated application unless its owning rule explicitly says it stacks. While applications overlap, the strongest applicable magnitude controls. A valid reapplication of a duration-based effect may refresh duration rather than multiply magnitude. Explicit counters/charges/marks and explicitly stackable effects remain exceptions.

B. The same effect stacks whenever it comes from a different Character/source.

C. The same effect always stacks unless its record explicitly forbids stacking.

D. Defer to CAB-13.

**Reasoning:** explicit stack rules exist but are rare. A noncompounding default prevents accidental same-effect engines while preserving authored cumulative systems.

## CAB-Q08-02 — Different numerical modifiers affecting the same statistic

**Decision impact:** VERY HIGH  
**CAB recommendation:** **A**.

A. **Recommended:** use explicit **interaction groups**. Different compatible groups may combine. Effects in the same noncumulative group follow that group's rule rather than stacking solely because their names/sources differ. For an ordinary modifier group, default to **strongest beneficial modifier + strongest detrimental modifier** while overlapping. Explicitly cumulative mechanics use additive/capped-additive modes.

B. Every differently named Ability stacks numerically with every other differently named Ability.

C. No numerical bonuses to the same statistic ever stack, regardless of mechanic/source.

D. Use source family as the stacking rule: different trees/families stack, same tree/family does not.

E. Defer to CAB-10.

**Reasoning:** source provenance is not mechanical identity. Interaction groups allow deliberate combinations without unlimited bonus aggregation.

## CAB-Q08-03 — Advantage and Disadvantage

**Decision impact:** HIGH  
**CAB recommendation:** **A**.

A. **Recommended:** Advantage and Disadvantage are **binary noncompounding states**. Multiple Advantage sources do not create double/triple Advantage; multiple Disadvantage sources do not escalate. If at least one applicable source of each exists, they cancel to a normal roll unless an explicit rule gives priority or uses a different mechanic.

B. Multiple Advantage/Disadvantage sources accumulate additional dice.

C. Count the number of Advantage and Disadvantage sources and use whichever side has more.

D. Defer until CAB-10.

**Reasoning:** the corpus uses Advantage/Disadvantage hundreds of times but does not provide a sufficiently explicit universal stacking rule. Binary resolution avoids a hidden escalating dice economy.

## CAB-Q08-04 — Resistance, Immunity, and overlapping defenses

**Decision impact:** VERY HIGH  
**CAB recommendation:** **A**.

A. **Recommended:** repeated Resistance to the same damage/effect type does not compound; multiple Resistances do not become Immunity; applicable Immunity supersedes ordinary Resistance; Resistances to different types coexist independently. Flat damage reduction and other defense mechanics use separate interaction groups. Duplicate Vulnerability likewise does not compound unless explicitly authored.

B. Multiple same-type Resistances stack multiplicatively.

C. Two Resistances to the same type become Immunity.

D. Defer to CAB-13/15.

**Reasoning:** this preserves meaningful defensive breadth without accidental exponential damage negation.

---

## CAB-Q08-05 — Full transformations/forms

**Decision impact:** VERY HIGH  
**CAB recommendation:** **A**.

A. **Recommended:** one **full replacement transformation/form** is active at a time by default. Activating another replaces/ends the previous full form unless an explicit rule authorizes nesting. Narrow augmentations/overlays may coexist only when compatible; native/passive traits remain unless the form explicitly replaces or suppresses them.

B. All transformations/forms may stack unless explicitly prohibited.

C. Transformations never coexist with any outside passive or augmentation.

D. Defer to CAB-13.

**Reasoning:** the corpus contains many multi-benefit forms. Default exclusivity for full-body replacement forms prevents accidental Avatar-on-Avatar power bundles while retaining augmentations and native traits where appropriate.

---

## CAB-Q08-06 — Multiple multipliers affecting the same quantity

**Decision impact:** VERY HIGH  
**CAB recommendation:** **A**.

A. **Recommended:** multipliers in the same mechanical multiplier group do **not** sequentially multiply one another by default; the strongest applicable multiplier controls. Flat/additive modifiers resolve under their own groups first unless the owning rule states another order. Deliberate compound multiplication must explicitly define order/limits and receive manual high-risk review.

B. All legal multipliers multiply sequentially (for example, x2 and x2 becomes x4).

C. Convert all multipliers into additive percentage bonuses.

D. Defer to CAB-13.

**Reasoning:** silent sequential multiplication is one of the fastest ways for individually reasonable effects to become exponential combinations.

## Already established and not being re-asked

- Breadth of known Abilities is not itself imbalance.
- Source family/provenance is not automatically a stacking type.
- Explicit authoritative stack/non-stack text is preserved.
- CAB-07 anti-recursion applies to action/reaction multiplier chains.
- Closed trigger/resource/grant cycles without an external bound are manual-review conditions.
- Missing interaction semantics may remain `balance_unresolved_stacking` rather than being invented.
- CAB-13 will inspect actual high-risk combinations; CAB-14/15 will benchmark equal-XP and multidimensional results.
