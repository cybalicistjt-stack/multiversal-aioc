# CAB-08 — Owner Questionnaire

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Status:** `resolved_by_owner_2026-09-05`  
**Decision artifact:** `CAB-08_OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-09-05.md`

The owner approved all six CAB-08 recommendations:

- **CAB-Q08-01: A** — same named mechanical effects are noncompounding by default; strongest magnitude controls and valid reapplication may refresh duration unless explicit stacking is authored.
- **CAB-Q08-02: A** — numerical modifiers use mechanical interaction groups; compatible groups may combine, while an ordinary noncumulative group uses strongest benefit plus strongest penalty.
- **CAB-Q08-03: A** — Advantage/Disadvantage are binary noncompounding states; applicable sources of both cancel to a normal roll unless an explicit rule establishes priority or another mechanic.
- **CAB-Q08-04: A** — same-type Resistance does not compound or become Immunity; Immunity supersedes ordinary Resistance; different types coexist; other defense mechanics use their own interaction groups.
- **CAB-Q08-05: A** — one full replacement form is active by default; explicit nesting and compatible narrow augmentations remain possible.
- **CAB-Q08-06: A** — same-group multipliers do not sequentially multiply by default; strongest applies unless deliberate compound multiplication explicitly defines order/limits and passes manual review.

The owner also authorized a standing CAB recommendation delegation for CAB-09 through CAB-23. See `CAB-08_OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-09-05.md` for its scope and safeguards.

All six questions are resolved. CAB-09 is released.
