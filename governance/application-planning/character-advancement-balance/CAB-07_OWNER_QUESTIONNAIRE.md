# CAB-07 — Owner Questionnaire

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Purpose:** resolve five action-economy policy choices that materially affect CAB-08 stacking/synergy and later Ability repricing. Unanswered items remain unresolved and do not silently default.

## CAB-Q07-01 — Default combat action envelope

**Decision impact:** VERY HIGH  
**CAB recommendation:** **A**.

A. **Recommended:** before explicit exceptions, a Character normally has **1 Action + 1 Bonus Action + movement on their turn, and 1 Reaction per round**. Free Actions require explicit rules; Passive effects consume no activation action. Owning multiple Abilities that use the same channel does not increase the number of uses of that channel.

B. Use 1 Action + movement only; remove Bonus Action as a universal channel and translate existing Bonus-Action content later.

C. Give every Character 2 general Actions per turn and eliminate the Action/Bonus Action split.

D. Another default envelope you specify.

**Reasoning:** the recovered corpus uses Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, and movement language extensively, especially martial content. Option A preserves existing vocabulary and creates a clear throughput baseline without inventing Character Levels or slot systems.

## CAB-Q07-02 — Anti-recursion rule for extra Actions/Reactions

**Decision impact:** VERY HIGH  
**CAB recommendation:** **A**.

A. **Recommended:** action-multiplier effects do **not recursively generate or refresh the same multiplier class by default**. An extra Action normally cannot activate another Ability whose primary purpose is to grant/refresh Actions or Reactions; extra-Reaction loops and unbounded summon/action chains are likewise blocked unless an explicit owning rule defines a finite recursion limit, frequency, resource cost, allowed action subset, and passes manual balance review.

B. Allow recursive action/reaction chains whenever all component Abilities are individually legal.

C. Ban every Ability that grants an extra Action or Reaction.

D. Permit recursion only for Tier-5/Apex Abilities.

**Reasoning:** action multiplication compounds rather than adds. A default anti-recursion rule prevents accidental infinite or explosive loops while preserving deliberately authored finite combos.

## CAB-Q07-03 — Consequential Free Action / no-action effects

**Decision impact:** HIGH  
**CAB recommendation:** **A**.

A. **Recommended:** a mechanically consequential Free Action or no-action effect must carry an explicit trigger, frequency, resource, state, or other meaningful limiter. Calling something a Free Action never authorizes unlimited repetition of the same consequential effect in one turn. Trivial interactions may remain repeatable when their owning rules permit.

B. Anything labeled Free Action can be repeated without limit unless its own record says otherwise.

C. Remove Free Action as a mechanical category entirely.

D. Treat every Free Action as once per turn by universal rule.

**Reasoning:** Option A preserves free-action design space while preventing a missing frequency field from becoming an infinite mechanical engine.

---

## CAB-Q07-04 — Summons, companions, familiars, mounts, drones, and minions

**Decision impact:** VERY HIGH  
**CAB recommendation:** **A**.

A. **Recommended:** do not penalize a companion merely for existing. Instead, count **independent meaningful combat actions** as action-economy multiplication. Command costs, shared action pools, restricted action lists, hard frequency/resource limits, and short duration mitigate that burden. Persistent autonomous full turns are manual-review effects and receive appropriate direct/reference value.

B. Every companion/minion counts as a full extra Action regardless of what it can do.

C. Ignore companion/minion action economy when pricing Character advancement; balance those entities separately only.

D. Prohibit independent combat turns for all player-controlled companions.

**Reasoning:** Multiversal intentionally supports mounts, pets, familiars, drones, creatures, and NPC-like companions. Option A measures what actually changes encounter throughput rather than banning or overpricing the whole concept.

---

## CAB-Q07-05 — Default authoring posture for action multiplication

**Decision impact:** VERY HIGH  
**CAB recommendation:** **A**.

A. **Recommended:** action compression and bounded additional attacks are legitimate design tools; **full unrestricted extra Actions, additional Reactions, and ally Action grants should be uncommon and explicitly limited/priced**. Additional Reactions must state count/refresh window; ally Action grants must state allowed action types and frequency; no multiplier implicitly grants Bonus Actions or Reactions unless stated.

B. Extra Actions/Reactions/ally grants should be ordinary low-tier mechanics whenever their XP price is high enough.

C. Reserve every extra Action/Reation/ally grant for Tier 5 only.

D. Ban full extra Actions but allow unlimited additional Reactions and ally Action grants.

**Reasoning:** Option A regulates throughput by actual effect burden and authored limits, not by tier alone. It keeps high-impact mechanics available without allowing them to become routine cheap additions.
