# CAB-07 — Owner Questionnaire

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Status:** `resolved_by_owner_2026-09-05`  
**Decision artifact:** `CAB-07_OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-09-05.md`

All five CAB-07 recommendations were explicitly approved by the owner.

- **CAB-Q07-01:** A — default combat envelope is 1 Action + 1 Bonus Action + movement on turn, and 1 Reaction per round, before explicit exceptions.
- **CAB-Q07-02:** A — action/reaction multiplier effects do not recursively generate or refresh the same multiplier class by default; deliberate finite exceptions must be explicit and manually reviewed.
- **CAB-Q07-03:** A — mechanically consequential Free/No-Action effects require a meaningful trigger, frequency, resource, state, or other limiter; the label does not authorize unlimited repetition.
- **CAB-Q07-04:** A — companions/minions are evaluated by meaningful independent combat action economy, not existence alone; persistent autonomous full turns are manual-review effects.
- **CAB-Q07-05:** A — action compression and bounded additional attacks are legitimate; full unrestricted extra Actions, additional Reactions, and ally Action grants remain uncommon, explicit, limited, and appropriately priced.

These decisions are controlling CAB authority and release CAB-08 — Stacking, Synergy & Power Multiplication to execute.
