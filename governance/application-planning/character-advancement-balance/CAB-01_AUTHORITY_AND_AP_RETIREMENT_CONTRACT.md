# CAB-01 — Authority, Source Census & AP Retirement Contract

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Tranche:** CAB-01  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner  
**State:** in_progress pending source-census closeout  

## 1. Owner decision

Ability Points (`AP`) are **deprecated** for current Multiversal character progression.

Effective for CAB and later rules work:

- AP is not a current spendable advancement currency.
- AP does not purchase abilities, attributes, skills, proficiencies, trees, tiers, species/environmental capabilities, training, transformations or other Character progression.
- AP thresholds derived from cumulative XP do not govern current advancement.
- AP balance tables, AP unlock costs and AP award schedules are historical/deprecated mechanical evidence.
- A legacy source using AP may still preserve non-AP design intent such as prerequisite structure, progression depth, acquisition fiction, relative ordering, specialization, training, respec or historical comparison. CAB must extract those concepts without reviving AP.
- If a legacy record has both AP and XP fields, CAB treats the AP component as deprecated and separately evaluates the XP component for current-rule relevance.
- No later CAB tranche may silently reintroduce AP. Reintroduction would require a new explicit owner decision superseding this contract.

## 2. Current advancement currency

XP is the ordinary spendable Character advancement currency for the working CAB model.

CAB-01 does **not** establish final XP prices, starting XP, XP reward rates, tier-unlock costs, refund rules, conversion rules or campaign pacing. Those are owned by later CAB tranches.

## 3. Authority hierarchy for CAB

CAB evaluates advancement evidence in this order:

1. explicit current owner decisions recorded in CAB;
2. current Character/advancement architecture and owning-domain contracts;
3. source-backed current game rules with stable provenance;
4. recovered legacy game rules, clearly labeled historical/deprecated where superseded;
5. authored balance/design proposals and synthetic QA material;
6. inference, which may identify a question or recommendation but may not create a rule.

A later file, generated package, UI packet, example build or old discussion does not override an explicit owner decision merely because it contains more detail.

## 4. Preserved current Character architecture

CAB inherits the current Character workflow semantics unless an explicit later CAB conflict is established:

- advancement awards are authoritative events;
- a Player prepares an advancement proposal;
- costs, prerequisites, limits, entitlement, policy and Character version are authoritatively validated;
- required GM decisions are recorded;
- accepted advancement commits exactly once;
- before/after state, cost ledger, calculation evidence and source/provenance are retained;
- correction/respec adds new evidence and does not erase accepted history;
- Definitions remain reusable governed content while Character selections and balances are owned Character state.

## 5. Preserved balance architecture

CAB inherits the completed PPIA-11 balance boundaries:

- no universal Challenge Rating, Character Level or one-dimensional power scalar;
- no guaranteed-balance certification;
- capability profiles, action economy, survivability, output, resources, range/mobility, environment, objectives, timers, information, hazards and other pressures remain separately inspectable;
- uncertainty remains explicit;
- post-playtest calibration remains necessary;
- permission filtering and source truth precede derived analysis.

## 6. CAB-01 exclusions

CAB-01 deliberately does not decide:

- the canonical starting Character XP budget;
- whether any historical XP cost is retained unchanged;
- final tier-entry mechanics;
- final five-tier definitions;
- XP award amounts;
- XP-to-credit conversion;
- acquisition rules for species, environment, innate magic or other special families;
- stacking limits;
- attribute/skill/proficiency repricing;
- ability repricing.

Those questions remain sequenced to CAB-02 and later tranches.
