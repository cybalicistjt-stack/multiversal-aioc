# CAB-15 — Multidimensional Character Balance

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Tranche:** CAB-15

## Purpose

Define how CAB evaluates Character balance without Character Level, CR, one weighted power score, equal-damage assumptions, or guaranteed-balance claims.

## Balance profile

Each Character/benchmark is described independently across at least these dimensions:

1. durability / mitigation;
2. recovery / regeneration;
3. sustained output;
4. burst / spike;
5. action economy / throughput;
6. control / denial;
7. mobility / position;
8. information / surprise;
9. attrition / resource endurance;
10. ally support / enabling;
11. challenge bypass / automatic resolution;
12. stacking / synergy / multiplication;
13. independent entities / companions;
14. breadth / flexibility;
15. dependencies / counterplay;
16. environment / context sensitivity.

This extends PPIA-11's independent pressure methodology into Character-focused comparison. Dimensions remain separate; no mandatory weighted sum exists.

## Evidence states

Each dimension records:

- evidence/source IDs and exact versions;
- current scenario/context;
- observed or projected strengths;
- costs/resources/frequencies/durations;
- dependencies and counterplay;
- comparator deltas where available;
- uncertainty: low / moderate / high / indeterminate.

An assessment may be indeterminate. Missing mechanics are never replaced with fabricated precision.

## Comparison method

At equal paid XP or another explicit comparator condition:

- identify dimensions where A materially exceeds B;
- identify dimensions where B materially exceeds A;
- record contextual/dependency differences;
- identify simultaneous dominance across multiple relevant dimensions;
- identify whether dominance is purchased, granted/RAV, dependency-based, or caused by unresolved/underpriced mechanics.

A build is not problematic merely because it is excellent in its intended specialty. A balance concern is stronger when one option/build simultaneously dominates several encounter-relevant dimensions while retaining broad availability, low resource cost, low counterplay, and no meaningful opportunity cost.

## Descriptive findings

Supported dimension findings are `limited`, `ordinary`, `strong`, `exceptional`, or `indeterminate`, always comparator/context-specific. These labels are not mathematical tiers and cannot be summed into a universal power value.

For regression/QA, a benchmark can additionally record concrete deltas such as attacks/round, expected damage in a defined fixture, effective HP under a named damage profile, resource uses, movement/range, targets affected, or check bonuses. Those measurements remain local to the fixture.

## RAV and paid XP

Paid XP is the progression investment axis. RAV exposes mechanically valuable zero-debit grants. Neither becomes a universal power score.

A Character with equal paid XP but materially higher RAV is not automatically invalid; the difference must be understood as Species/Campaign/grant context and tested against the campaign's intended starting and encounter assumptions.

## Breadth

Breadth/flexibility is itself one dimension. CAB does not impose a known-Ability cap, readied slots, or diminishing-return breadth tax merely because a veteran owns many options. Simultaneous throughput, stacking, resources, and context determine how much breadth matters in one scene/turn.

## High-risk escalation

A profile routes to CAB-13/CAB-22 review when:

- action/resource/trigger recursion is possible;
- one mechanic substantially raises several dimensions at once;
- broad immunity/recovery removes intended pressure without meaningful counterplay;
- independent entities multiply meaningful actions;
- automatic success/bypass erases broad challenge families;
- deterministic evaluation is blocked by missing timing/interaction semantics.

## Adopted decisions

Under standing owner delegation:

- the 16-dimension Character Balance Profile is the normal CAB evaluation surface;
- no required weighted sum or universal score exists;
- descriptive bands are local/comparator-specific and not additive;
- equal paid XP, RAV and actual current-state dependencies are displayed separately;
- specialization is allowed; simultaneous low-cost dominance across many dimensions is the main warning pattern;
- breadth is measured but not globally taxed;
- uncertainty/indeterminate results are first-class.

No delegation guardrail triggered.
