# CAB-02 — Owner Decisions

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Source:** Owner responses to `CAB-02_OWNER_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`  
**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Authority:** explicit owner decision  

## CAB-Q02-01 — Ordinary assets paid in XP

**Decision:** Option C, with a Character-creation clarification.

- Ordinary transferable equipment, vehicles, drones, refits and upkeep use their owning credits/resources/economy rather than Character XP.
- XP remains reserved for permanent Character-bound progression or an explicitly exceptional advancement sacrifice.
- The prior Character-creation exception that allowed XP to function as ordinary starting money may be removed.
- Starting money/wealth is instead determined by the GM/Campaign creation policy.

## CAB-Q02-02 — Percentage XP modifiers

**Decision:** Generally Option A, with an explicit learning-speed requirement.

- Ordinary global percentage XP multipliers are not part of the default progression economy.
- Training, facilities or other ordinary content should not multiply all future XP gained.
- CAB must still provide a meaningful way for high Intelligence and/or Wisdom to represent faster learning.
- CAB-02 does not choose the exact mechanic. The mechanism must be evaluated in later advancement/attribute/pacing work without silently turning Intelligence or Wisdom into an unrestricted global XP multiplier.

**Required later routing:**
- CAB-04 — Advancement Architecture: determine where learning-speed modifiers attach in the advancement process.
- CAB-10 — Attributes, Skills & Proficiencies: determine the roles of Intelligence and Wisdom and prevent double-counting/broad-stat dominance.
- CAB-16/CAB-17 — Progression Pacing / XP Award Framework: verify the chosen learning-speed rule does not create runaway long-campaign XP divergence or distort award pacing.

## CAB-Q02-03 — Hazard/trap XP values

**Decision:** Option B.

- Existing hazard/trap XP values are retained as source benchmark/reference values.
- They are not automatic universal payouts.
- CAB-17 will determine actual award semantics so meaningful resolution can include avoidance, survival, disabling, negotiation, circumvention or other accomplishment rather than requiring a mechanical defeat tag.

## CAB-Q02-04 — Character-creation XP/wealth conversion

**Decision:** Option A.

- Advancement XP and starting money/equipment wealth are separate budgets.
- No ordinary XP-to-credits conversion is used at Character creation.
- Starting money/wealth is determined by the GM/Campaign creation policy rather than derived from XP.

## CAB-03 handoff consequences

CAB-03 must therefore build the Character-creation baseline with:

1. one starting advancement-XP budget or governed starting-XP policy;
2. a separate GM/Campaign-defined starting money/wealth policy;
3. no AP;
4. no ordinary XP-to-money conversion;
5. no legacy asset-upkeep XP in the creation model;
6. no ordinary percentage XP multiplier;
7. explicit free/granted versus XP-purchased Character selections;
8. Intelligence/Wisdom faster-learning support left as a required later advancement mechanic, not solved through creation wealth or a blanket creation bonus.

## Supersession

These decisions resolve all four CAB-02 questionnaire items. Earlier questionnaire language remains provenance but is no longer unresolved.
