# CAB-10 — Attribute, Skill, Knowledge & Proficiency Source Audit

`Attribute Selection.PDF` starts all six Attributes at 10 before Species modifiers and contains legacy Regular/High/Hyper packages. CAB-03 supersedes those for the normal start and the source gives no trustworthy post-creation +1 schedule.

`Skills 11-14-24.PDF` defines d20 Skill checks and 20 Skill Points. Its progressive list runs from 100 XP at point 1 to 11,260 at 10, 42,265 at 19, and 75,000 at 20, but does not clearly prove whether each figure is marginal or cumulative. Wholesale marginal inheritance would be inconsistent with CAB-06.

`Knowledge 2-24-25.PDF` defines five Knowledge tiers, +1 relevant-check bonus per tier, similarity, prerequisites, research, teaching, and faster task completion. Its legacy teaching mechanism can generate `1d6 × 10 XP`; CAB-02/CAB-04 already reject that as spendable-XP generation, so mentoring is converted to learning progress/time.

`Weapon Proficiency.PDF` defines Unproficient, Proficient, Expertise, Mastery and places specialized category abilities at Expertise+; it does not supply a complete universal base-state XP ladder.

Across the bounded 4,816 Ability records, 189 records contain concrete Attribute requirements after aggregate placeholders are excluded. Skill/Knowledge/proficiency requirements also appear throughout prerequisite and Special Rules fields.

Conclusion: retain the six-Attribute, 20-rank Skill, five-tier Knowledge, and four-state base Proficiency structures; normalize economics; preserve legacy prices as provenance.
