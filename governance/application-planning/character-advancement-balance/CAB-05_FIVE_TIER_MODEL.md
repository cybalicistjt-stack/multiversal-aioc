# CAB-05 — Five-Tier Model

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Tranche:** CAB-05  
**State:** `completed_verified` — owner policy resolved  
**Owner/final authority:** John Brandon Turner

## Purpose

Define what Multiversal's five Ability tiers mean, how tier/depth access relates to prerequisites and XP, how short/sparse and Prestige progressions are treated, and how tiers remain useful without becoming a universal power score.

## Governing result

Multiversal uses five authoritative numeric tiers as **relative developmental depth within the owning progression scope**:

| Tier | Shared descriptor | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Foundation** | Entry capability, basic control, literacy, fundamental technique, or first reliable expression of the progression. |
| 2 | **Developed** | Expanded/specialized capability and reliable use beyond fundamentals. |
| 3 | **Advanced** | Mature specialized competence, signature technique, stronger combination, or meaningful increase in scope/control. |
| 4 | **Expert** | High-leverage, elite, transformative, or highly refined capability within the progression. |
| 5 | **Apex** | Capstone or identity-defining developmental depth for that progression. |

Source-local labels such as Legendary, Mastery, Elite, Basic, Foundational, or domain-specific terms remain valid provenance/display labels.

Tier is **not** a universal XP price, Character Level, CR, damage band, rarity band, or encounter-power scalar.

## Evidence

Bounded CAB corpus:

- 4,816 Ability records total;
- 3,917 with numeric Tier 1–5;
- 179 tiered progression groups;
- 158 with all five tiers;
- 21 partial/sparse/alternate-start groups.

Direct Ability XP medians rise by tier but overlap heavily:

| Tier | Numeric records | Median direct XP | Observed range |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 272 | 250 | 20–3,000 |
| 2 | 264 | 700 | 40–7,500 |
| 3 | 270 | 1,500 | 60–15,000 |
| 4 | 226 | 3,000 | 110–25,000 |
| 5 | 215 | 5,000 | 200–40,000 |

This supports depth correlation while rejecting tier as an exact price or power score.

## Progression scope

Tier access belongs to an explicit stable progression scope such as an Ability Tree, independent branch/path, Prestige progression, or another governed progression graph.

Unlocking a tier in one independent scope does not silently unlock the same tier in another.

## Ordinary tier access

Owner-approved default for an ordinary purchasable tiered Ability progression:

1. Tier 1 access is established when the progression is legitimately opened.
2. Tiers 2–5 normally require a **one-time XP tier-access purchase** after non-cost access conditions are satisfied.
3. Individual Abilities retain their own XP prices unless granted/free.
4. CAB-06 calibrates tier-access prices; recovered legacy schedules are not automatically retained.
5. Explicit special progression modes may instead use prerequisite-only, grant, milestone, sequential, innate, special-acquisition, or another governed access rule.

There remains **no universal prior-tier quantity ladder**. Tree/path-specific mastery requirements may still be authored where genuinely needed.

## Short and capped progressions

Ordinary progressions may intentionally cap before Tier 5.

A well-formed progression normally uses contiguous tiers from its declared starting tier through its declared cap. An unexplained internal gap is a content-quality/provenance warning unless explicitly intentional. CAB never invents filler Abilities to populate tiers.

## Prestige progression exception

Owner-approved Prestige rule:

- **Prestige classes/progressions may legitimately begin at Tier 3.**
- Tiers 1–2 are not missing for a Prestige progression whose declared starting tier is 3.
- Prestige entry requires explicit prerequisites/eligibility, such as prior Abilities, Skills, Knowledges, proficiencies, Attributes, achievements, milestones, faction/order status, narrative accomplishments, or other governed requirements.
- Starting at Tier 3 does not waive those requirements.
- Prestige progressions should normally remain contiguous from Tier 3 through their declared cap unless an explicit owning rule states otherwise.
- CAB-09 owns detailed Prestige eligibility/acquisition; CAB-12 owns structural audit.

## Tier 5

Tier 5 means **Apex/capstone developmental depth inside the owning progression**.

It does not require every Tier-5 option to be world-shaping, equally expensive, equally damaging, equally rare, or equivalent in encounter power. Actual effect burden and multidimensional balance remain separately calibrated.

## Grants and creation

CAB-04 grant semantics remain controlling. A free grant waives cost; source-backed eligibility may be established by the source; unrelated prerequisites/tier/learning/exclusion gates require explicit bypass authority.

CAB-03 creation remains controlling:

- five starting Ability Trees free;
- Tier-1 access in those trees free;
- five eligible Tier-1 Abilities free;
- extra-tree and higher-tier prices are CAB-06 work.

## Entitlement boundary

Product/free-user access to the first two tiers is an entitlement rule only and has no authority to redefine tier meaning or Character XP balance.
