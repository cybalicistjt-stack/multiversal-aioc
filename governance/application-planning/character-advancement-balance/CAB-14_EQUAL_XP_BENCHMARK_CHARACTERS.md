# CAB-14 — Equal-XP Benchmark Characters

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Tranche:** CAB-14

## Purpose

Define deterministic, synthetic Character benchmark fixtures for comparing what equal paid advancement XP can produce without pretending equal XP means equal damage or collapsing Character capability into one power score.

The fixtures are noncanonical methodology objects. They do not create canon Characters, source Abilities, or guaranteed-balanced builds.

## Benchmark budgets

CAB-14 uses four paid-advancement checkpoints:

- **1,300 XP** — normal creation/start budget;
- **5,000 XP** — early developed Character;
- **15,000 XP** — established specialist/multirole Character;
- **40,000 XP** — veteran/high-development Character.

A benchmark reports paid XP and **Reference Advancement Value (RAV)** separately. Free Species/creation/milestone grants do not consume paid XP but remain visible through RAV. This prevents both `free = worth zero` and `free = hidden paid debt` errors.

## Common comparison baseline

Unless a case intentionally tests a grant/acquisition difference, benchmark Characters share the CAB-03 common creation envelope: normal Attribute baseline, equivalent free tree/T1 selection opportunity, same Campaign assumptions, and no hidden asset-wealth conversion.

Differences in Species, innate grants, external dependencies, companions, environment, or Campaign awards must be explicit scenario variables and their RAV/dependency state must be shown.

## Synthetic benchmark archetypes

The following fixtures are methodology roles, not classes:

1. **Focused Martial Specialist** — concentrates XP in one combat progression, relevant Attribute/Skill/Proficiency and bounded active techniques.
2. **Broad Generalist** — buys several low/mid-depth progressions, Skills and Knowledges; tests breadth without simultaneous-power multipliers.
3. **Skill/Knowledge Specialist** — concentrates in high Skill ranks, Knowledge tiers and enabling Attributes; tests noncombat/problem-solving pressure.
4. **Magic/Capacity Specialist** — invests in magic/archetype access and capacity rather than per-spell XP; tests resource-limited flexibility.
5. **Companion Controller** — invests in relationship/command/support capability and an explicit companion action model; tests independent action economy.
6. **Prestige Entrant** — satisfies an advanced entry package and pays legitimate T3 Prestige access plus separate first Ability.
7. **Defense/Recovery Specialist** — invests in survivability, mitigation and recovery without action multiplication.
8. **Throughput Multiplier Specialist** — deliberately includes a reviewed extra-action/reaction/compression mechanic to test CAB-07/CAB-13 risk controls.

## Validity rules

Every benchmark must record:

- paid XP ledger by component;
- RAV and zero-debit grants separately;
- acquisition/eligibility assumptions;
- Attributes, Skills, Knowledges, Proficiencies, tree/tier access and Abilities at stable identity/version;
- action-economy channels and any multiplier;
- Resources, frequencies, durations and dependencies;
- stacking/interaction groups;
- companion/independent-turn assumptions;
- environment/context assumptions;
- unresolved source mechanics;
- benchmark purpose and comparator.

A benchmark with a material unresolved mechanic can still be retained, but its result is `indeterminate_for_affected_dimensions` rather than repaired by invention.

## Equal-XP comparison rule

Equal paid XP supports **peer comparison**, not equivalence certification. Two equal-XP Characters may intentionally differ sharply in:

- sustained output;
- burst;
- durability/recovery;
- control;
- mobility;
- investigation/social/problem-solving breadth;
- ally support;
- environment sensitivity;
- resource endurance;
- action compression;
- challenge bypass.

Balance concern arises when one build dominates too many materially relevant dimensions simultaneously without compensating limits, dependencies, costs, or counterplay.

## Benchmark test families

### Same budget / different specialization
Compare all eight synthetic roles at each checkpoint. Record dimension-by-dimension deltas, not a winner score.

### Same role / increasing budget
Track how one role changes from 1,300 → 5,000 → 15,000 → 40,000 paid XP. This exposes discontinuities from tier unlocks, expensive Attributes, action multipliers, or broad grants.

### Grant/RAV sensitivity
Hold paid XP constant while adding a governed free Species/milestone grant. Record RAV and dimension deltas; do not charge retroactive XP.

### Dependency sensitivity
Compare an external-source/companion/form-dependent build with dependency available vs unavailable. Preserve acquisition history while changing current capability.

### High-risk substitution
At equal paid XP, compare a normal high-cost technique bundle against one high-risk multiplier/bundle. If the high-risk option dominates several dimensions or removes meaningful action/resource constraints, route back to CAB-13/CAB-22 repricing/repair.

## Adopted decisions

Under standing owner delegation:

- equal-XP benchmarks use paid XP plus separate RAV;
- synthetic role fixtures are noncanonical and deterministic;
- no universal score/winner is produced;
- comparison uses explicit multidimensional deltas and scenario assumptions;
- unresolved mechanics yield indeterminate affected dimensions;
- high-risk mechanics are benchmarked against ordinary alternatives at the same paid XP.

No delegation guardrail triggered.
