# CAB-12 — Structural Decisions

Under the standing CAB delegation, owner-approved structural policy is:

- progression scopes explicitly declare start tier and cap tier;
- contiguity is expected from declared start through cap, not universally from Tier 1;
- Prestige Tier-3 starts remain valid with entry requirements; explicit ultimate/special high-tier starts are possible when source-backed;
- unexplained internal gaps become review states and never authorize invented filler;
- no universal prior-tier quantity requirement is restored;
- branches must declare shared/independent access and compatibility;
- prerequisite graphs should use stable IDs/predicates where possible; prose-only dependencies remain unresolved until normalized;
- apparent published-count mismatches are not auto-repaired before record-role classification.

No delegation guardrail triggered.
