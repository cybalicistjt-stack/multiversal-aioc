# CAB-14 — Completion Report

CAB-14 establishes four equal-paid-XP checkpoints (1,300 / 5,000 / 15,000 / 40,000) and eight synthetic noncanonical benchmark roles. Paid XP and RAV are always tracked separately; grants, dependencies, action economy, Resources and unresolved mechanics remain visible.

Equal XP is explicitly a comparator condition rather than a claim of equal damage or universal power. Tests compare multidimensional deltas, same-role progression across budgets, grant/RAV sensitivity, dependency sensitivity and high-risk substitutions.

No delegation guardrail triggered.
