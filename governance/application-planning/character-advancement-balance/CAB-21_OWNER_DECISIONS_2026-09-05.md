# CAB-21 — Owner-Delegated Decisions

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Authority:** standing owner recommendation delegation recorded in `CAB-08_OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-09-05.md`

CAB-21 produced a clear evidence-grounded recommendation with no conflict against prior owner authority. The following recommendations are adopted.

## CAB-Q21-01 — Starting advancement XP

**Decision: retain 1,300 XP as the normal-start default.**

The free creation chassis already supplies foundational breadth. The 1,300-XP discretionary grant supports one substantial starting direction without making advanced tier access or long-form mastery cheap. GM/Campaign override remains legal.

## CAB-Q21-02 — Standard progression pace

**Decision: retain 500 XP per substantive session as the Standard long-run target.**

Slow/Fast/Accelerated Campaign profiles remain 250/750/1,000 XP. These are average pacing profiles, not automatic per-session stipends.

## CAB-Q21-03 — Veteran breadth

**Decision: no new global breadth control.**

The simulated opportunity costs remain substantial through 200-session horizons. Do not introduce a universal Ability-count cap, readied slots, breadth tax or global diminishing-return price. CAB-07/08 simultaneous-power controls and CAB-13 high-risk review remain the nonlinear safeguards.

## CAB-Q21-04 — Direct HP

**Decision: retain the governed direct-HP progression only through +20.**

The current +5/+10/+15/+20 schedule remains usable, but CAB does not authorize further direct-HP blocks without survivability evidence. CAB-22 must preserve this as a testing/repair boundary rather than extrapolating the curve indefinitely.

## CAB-Q21-05 — Simulation authority

**Decision: reference Characters remain synthetic QA fixtures.**

They validate the economy/pacing model but do not become canonical pregenerated Characters or automatically prove every individual Ability balanced.

## Successor

CAB-21 is resolved under standing owner delegation. **CAB-22 — Final Rules & Content Repair Map** is selected next.
