# CAB-05 — Owner Decisions

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Source:** Owner responses to `CAB-05_OWNER_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`  
**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Authority:** explicit owner decision

## CAB-Q05-01 — Shared tier descriptors

**Decision:** Option A.

- Numeric Tier 1–5 remains authoritative.
- Shared semantic descriptors are:
  - Tier 1 — **Foundation**
  - Tier 2 — **Developed**
  - Tier 3 — **Advanced**
  - Tier 4 — **Expert**
  - Tier 5 — **Apex**
- Source-local names such as Legendary, Mastery, Elite, Basic, Foundational, and other domain-specific labels remain valid display/provenance data and do not create a separate tier system.

## CAB-Q05-02 — Higher-tier access cost

**Decision:** Option A.

- An ordinary purchasable tiered Ability progression normally uses a one-time XP access purchase for Tiers 2–5 after all non-cost access conditions are satisfied.
- Tier 1 access is established when the progression is legitimately opened.
- Individual Abilities retain their own XP prices unless granted/free.
- Tier-access prices are not inherited from legacy schedules; CAB-06 owns calibration.
- Explicit special progression modes remain legal, including prerequisite-only, grant, milestone, sequential, innate, acquisition, or other governed access modes.

## CAB-Q05-03 — Short, sparse, and Prestige progressions

**Decision:** Option A, with explicit Prestige exception.

### Ordinary short/capped progressions

- A progression may intentionally cap before Tier 5.
- A well-formed ordinary progression should normally be contiguous from its declared starting tier through its declared cap.
- An unexplained internal missing tier remains a content-quality/provenance warning unless explicitly intentional.
- CAB must never invent filler Abilities merely to populate all five tiers.

### Prestige exception

- **Prestige classes/progressions may legitimately begin at Tier 3.**
- Tiers 1–2 are not considered missing for a Prestige progression whose declared start is Tier 3.
- Prestige entry must have explicit requirements. These may include prior Abilities, Skills, Knowledges, proficiencies, Attributes, milestones, achievements, faction/order status, narrative accomplishments, or other governed prerequisites/eligibility conditions.
- Prestige access does not waive those requirements merely because the progression starts at Tier 3.
- A Prestige progression should normally remain contiguous from Tier 3 through its declared cap unless an explicit owning rule says otherwise.
- CAB-09 and CAB-12 may refine Prestige eligibility/structure, but may not reinterpret a proper Tier-3 Prestige start as damaged source data.

## CAB-Q05-04 — Tier-5 meaning

**Decision:** Option A.

- Tier 5 means **Apex/capstone developmental depth within the owning progression**.
- Tier 5 does not impose a universal legendary/world-shaping power floor.
- Tier 5 does not guarantee equal XP price, damage, encounter power, rarity, scope, or utility across different progressions.
- Actual effect burden and balance remain separately calibrated and tested.

## CAB-06 handoff consequences

CAB-06 — XP Cost Calibration Framework may execute and must inherit:

1. numeric Tier 1–5 with Foundation / Developed / Advanced / Expert / Apex descriptors;
2. ordinary purchasable progressions normally use one-time T2–T5 XP access purchases plus individual Ability prices;
3. special progression modes may explicitly replace the ordinary access method;
4. ordinary short trees may cap early without filler, with unexplained internal gaps treated as warnings;
5. Prestige classes/progressions may declare Tier 3 as their legitimate starting tier and must carry explicit entry requirements;
6. Tier 5 means apex depth within the owning progression, not a universal raw-power floor;
7. no universal prior-tier quantity ladder is restored.
