# CAB-03 — Owner Decisions

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Source:** Owner responses to `CAB-03_OWNER_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`  
**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Authority:** explicit owner decision  

## CAB-Q03-01 — Starting XP rigidity

**Decision:** Option B, with an explicit recalibration permission.

- **1,300 XP is the working default normal-start Character advancement budget.**
- A GM/Campaign creation policy may explicitly choose another starting XP grant for veteran, high-power, low-power, unusual, or otherwise special campaigns.
- A Campaign starting-XP override changes the starting grant only; it does not silently change the XP prices of Abilities or other advancement options.
- The 1,300-XP normal-start number is **not permanently frozen**. CAB may recommend adjusting it after the advancement economy is better calibrated and tested.
- Any later change to the normal-start default must be explicit, source/provenance recorded, and justified by CAB evidence rather than silently drifting during repricing.

**Required later validation:** CAB-06 cost calibration, CAB-14 equal-XP benchmark Characters, CAB-16 progression pacing, and CAB-21 reference Character/campaign simulation must test whether 1,300 XP remains an appropriate normal-start budget.

## CAB-Q03-02 — Attribute creation model

**Decision:** Combined A + B.

### Normal/default creation baseline

- All six attributes begin at 10 before explicit Species modifiers.
- Apply Species modifiers.
- A normal-start Character then receives **one free 12-point attribute allocation pool**.
- Physical / Mental / Balanced may be retained as example distributions or guidance for that normal pool.
- Additional permanent attribute increases beyond the free pool use XP under the attribute economy later finalized by CAB-10/CAB-06.

### Optional alternate creation packages

- The design concept behind **Regular / High / Hyper** may be retained as optional Campaign creation packages or starting-power profiles.
- Their existing point totals, templates, and XP prices are **not valid as written** and do not govern current creation.
- Before any such package becomes usable, CAB must redesign it so point totals, allocation rules, XP cost, starting-XP policy, and resulting Character power are internally consistent.
- These alternate packages must not create hidden bulk-purchase arbitrage against the ordinary per-attribute XP economy.
- Until that later rebalance is completed and accepted, the 12-point free normal-start allocation is the operative baseline.

**Required later routing:** CAB-06 and CAB-10 own price/value calibration; CAB-14/CAB-21 must benchmark any rebuilt alternate starting packages against the normal-start profile.

## CAB-Q03-03 — Background customization cap

**Decision:** Option A.

- There is **no universal 1,800-XP background customization cap**.
- Homeworld Type, Cultural Influence, and Early Life Profession core benefits remain free under the supported creation baseline.
- Optional background upgrades use available advancement XP and must satisfy their own source eligibility, prerequisites, exclusions, and Campaign policy.
- A GM/Campaign creation policy may impose a background-category cap where a specific campaign needs one.

The earlier 1,800-XP universal cap remains historical provenance only and does not govern the default system.

## CAB-Q03-04 — Permanent HP purchase at creation

**Decision:** Option A.

- Characters may invest starting advancement XP in additional **permanent HP** at creation.
- Base starting HP remains `max(10, Constitution)` plus explicit Species modifiers and other governed grants.
- The concept of creation-only permanent HP investment is retained.
- The recovered +5 / +10 / +15 / +20 HP price ladder and 300 / 750 / 1,250 / 2,000-XP prices are **provisional**, not final.
- CAB-06 must calibrate the cost of bought HP against attributes, defenses, resistances, healing, action economy, and other durability options before final prices are accepted.
