# CAB-15 — Completion Report

CAB-15 establishes a 16-dimension Character Balance Profile derived from CAB-07/08/13 and PPIA-11. It explicitly rejects a required weighted sum, universal power rating, breadth tax, or equal-damage target. Paid XP, RAV, dependencies, scenario context, concrete fixture measurements and uncertainty remain separate.

Specialization is valid. The primary warning pattern is simultaneous dominance across several relevant dimensions with weak costs, limits or counterplay.

No delegation guardrail triggered.
