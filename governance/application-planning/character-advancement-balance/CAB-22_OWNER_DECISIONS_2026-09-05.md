# CAB-22 — Owner-Delegated Decisions

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Authority:** standing owner recommendation delegation recorded in `CAB-08_OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-09-05.md`

CAB-22 introduces no new owner-specific creative direction and does not grant implementation/release authority. Its recommendations are therefore adopted under the standing delegation.

## CAB-Q22-01 — Final CAB rules surface

**Decision: adopted.**

`CAB-22_FINAL_ADVANCEMENT_RULES.md` is the final CAB rules synthesis. It consolidates the approved advancement economy, tiers, pricing, action economy, stacking, acquisition, Attributes/Skills/Knowledges/Proficiencies, learning, pacing, awards, veteran play and migration rules without replacing source provenance.

## CAB-Q22-02 — Rules-complete versus content-repaired

**Decision: adopted.**

CAB rule completion does not imply that every legacy Ability row is repaired or balance-certified. Content with unresolved identity, source boundaries, acquisition, timing, interaction, prerequisite, dependency or pricing semantics remains explicitly queued.

## CAB-Q22-03 — Repair priority order

**Decision: adopted.**

Use the repair order in `CAB-22_CONTENT_REPAIR_MAP_v1.0.0.json`:

1. P0 source integrity and stable identity;
2. P1 mechanical determinism and high-risk semantics;
3. P2 economic/structural normalization;
4. P3 validation/presentation follow-up.

Source-boundary and identity problems outrank repricing where they affect the same content.

## CAB-Q22-04 — Repair invariants

**Decision: adopted.**

Repairs must preserve source provenance, never invent missing Abilities/prerequisites, distinguish intentional source absence from defects, avoid retroactive XP debt for valid historical Characters, and rerun multidimensional benchmarks after material repair batches.

## CAB-Q22-05 — Content-repair authority boundary

**Decision: adopted.**

CAB-22 creates a governed repair/repricing handoff, not automatic corpus mutation. Individual content changes still require source-backed or explicitly governed repair execution with before/after provenance and migration handling where relevant.
