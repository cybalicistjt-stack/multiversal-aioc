# CAB-17 — XP Award Framework

## Purpose

Define how Campaigns award advancement XP so the selected CAB-16 pace follows meaningful accomplishment rather than kills, loot, repetitive actions, or global XP multipliers.

## Core rule

XP rewards **meaningful advancement-worthy accomplishment**. The GM/Campaign evaluates what changed, was achieved, learned, protected, discovered, resolved, survived, created, or advanced—not how many enemies were killed.

Combat, negotiation, stealth, bypass, rescue, investigation, travel, crafting, diplomacy, research, survival, retreat, sacrifice, and nonviolent solutions are equally eligible when they materially advance the Campaign/Character goal.

## Award bands

Use CU bands per Character relative to the Campaign's selected pace:

- **0 CU / 0 XP:** no advancement-worthy progress; administrative/setup-only play; or no award by Campaign design.
- **1 CU / 250 XP:** minor but meaningful progress.
- **2 CU / 500 XP:** standard substantive accomplishment.
- **3 CU / 750 XP:** major accomplishment, major risk/obstacle resolution, or meaningful arc advancement.
- **4 CU / 1,000 XP:** landmark achievement, arc/quest resolution, exceptional discovery/creation, or Campaign milestone.

The bands remain anchored to the current 250-XP CU. CAB-16 pacing profiles determine expected long-run frequency, not a different price table.

## Group versus individual awards

Shared objectives normally award the **same advancement XP to participating Characters**. This reduces competition for finishing blows, spotlight capture, or individually countable actions.

Campaigns may grant an additional bounded personal award for an explicitly Character-specific milestone, development objective, sacrifice, discovery, training culmination, or story achievement. Personal awards should not be routine per-action bonuses that recreate checklist farming.

## No kill XP

Creature defeat does not automatically produce Character XP. A hostile encounter may support an award because the party achieved a meaningful objective, protected something, escaped, captured, negotiated, learned, or overcame pressure. Killing extra creatures after the meaningful objective does not automatically increase the advancement payout.

Likewise, hazard/trap reference XP from older sources remains benchmark evidence, not automatic payout.

## Award timing

The GM may award XP:

- at session end;
- when an objective/milestone resolves;
- at an arc checkpoint;
- on another explicit Campaign cadence.

The award receipt records amount, CU equivalent, reason/accomplishment, participants, Campaign/rules version and timestamp. It does not require enumerating every micro-action.

## Pacing reconciliation

A Standard campaign targets ~2 CU / 500 XP per substantive session **on average**. Some sessions may be 0/250; a major resolution may be 750/1,000. Long-run cadence, not session-by-session sameness, is the calibration target.

Slow/Fast/Accelerated Campaigns can shift how often award bands occur rather than multiplying all awards by hidden percentages. If a Campaign deliberately scales the band amounts themselves, that is an explicit rules-profile choice and must be visible.

## Training and learning

Learning projects do not generate spendable XP merely for time spent training. Training can itself become accomplishment-worthy only when the Campaign explicitly treats the achieved objective/milestone as an XP-worthy event. CAB-10 Int/Wis affects learning time, not the award amount.

## Anti-farming

Do not sum XP mechanically for every kill, lock picked, spell cast, Skill check, room explored, item crafted, teaching hour, or hazard touched. Repeating a safe action does not create infinite advancement.

When multiple actions are all components of one accomplishment, award the accomplishment once at the appropriate band.

## Adopted decisions

Under standing owner delegation:

- 0/250/500/750/1,000 XP are normal award bands;
- group-equal awards are default for shared accomplishments;
- bounded personal milestone awards are legal but not routine micro-action bonuses;
- no automatic kill, hazard, training-time or checklist XP;
- peaceful/bypass/creative solutions are equally rewardable;
- CAB-16 long-run pace governs average cadence;
- no ordinary percentage XP multiplier returns.

No delegation guardrail triggered.
