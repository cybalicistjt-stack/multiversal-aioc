# CAB-09 — Owner Decisions

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Authority:** standing owner recommendation delegation from `CAB-08_OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-09-05.md`

The owner authorized clearly stated, evidence-grounded CAB recommendations through CAB-23 to be recorded as owner-approved without a repetitive questionnaire stop, subject to the guardrails in the delegation artifact.

CAB-09 presented no material conflict with prior owner authority, no unrelated implementation/release authority, and no inherently owner-specific creative choice. The following recommendations are therefore adopted.

## CAB-Q09-01 — Eligibility versus acquisition

**Decision: Recommended option adopted.**

Eligibility means the Character is a valid potential recipient. Acquisition is the governed event/process that makes the option owned, granted, active, learned, bonded, installed, inducted, or otherwise available. Eligibility does not itself create ownership, and XP affordability does not establish either eligibility or acquisition.

## CAB-Q09-02 — Species / innate acquisition

**Decision: Recommended option adopted.**

- explicit automatic biological traits are source grants;
- Species-qualified selectable options are eligible because of qualifying Species/lineage identity but are not automatically granted unless stated;
- `Species Perk`, `Innate Ability`, dataset membership, adjacency, or biological-sounding names do not by themselves prove automatic biology or grant status;
- creation-only innate options do not become ordinary later purchases merely because XP exists;
- later or cross-Species acquisition requires an explicit bridge such as transformation, mutation, awakening, bioengineering, implant/symbiosis, Campaign grant, or another owning rule.

## CAB-Q09-03 — Environment / exposure / adaptation

**Decision: Recommended option adopted.**

Current presence in an environment may satisfy use context and may open exposure/acclimation eligibility, but does not automatically create permanent advancement. Permanent environmental Adaptation/Ability acquisition follows the owning exposure, acclimation, training, source-grant, or Campaign rule. Temporary grants remain temporary.

## CAB-Q09-04 — Faction, order, profession and Prestige lifecycle

**Decision: Recommended option adopted.**

Membership/standing/induction can establish eligibility or training opportunity without granting an entire tree. A learned permanent Ability normally remains learned after membership loss unless its owning rule makes the capability dependent on continuing affiliation, patronage, office, facility, relic, supernatural link, or similar source. Membership loss may close future acquisition and suspend source-dependent grants.

Profession/crafting XP never silently waives tools, facilities, ingredients, Skills, practice, or other authored requirements.

Prestige retains explicit entry requirements, legitimate Tier-3 start, no T1/T2 back-pay, and a separate first-Ability acquisition.

## CAB-Q09-05 — Structured spell acquisition

**Decision: Recommended option adopted.**

The current 385 structured spells preserve archetype/school/focus access and minimum caster-tier rules. The spell itself carries 0 direct learning XP; XP purchases slots/ready-known capacity under the owning archetype rules. CAB does not convert the structured spell catalog into universal per-spell XP purchases.

## CAB-Q09-06 — External dependencies and creature relationships

**Decision: Recommended option adopted.**

Character-owned permanent advancement remains distinct from capabilities granted by implants, symbiotes, relics, artifacts, bonded/possessed objects, forms, vehicles, or other external/current-state dependencies. Losing a dependency may suspend its grant without deleting acquisition history.

Mount/pet/familiar/companion eligibility routes through CEW/CCP relationship authority. Character advancement may grant pathway eligibility/capacity/techniques/bond opportunity, but does not automatically create a creature instance, ownership, obedience, training, placement, or consent. Person-level/sapient relationships require voluntary consent.

## CAB-Q09-07 — Source silence

**Decision: Recommended option adopted.**

Source silence is `acquisition_unresolved`, not inferred eligibility or ineligibility. XP cost, dataset membership, tier, current environment, name similarity, creature morphology, or a source example does not by itself authorize acquisition.
