# CAB — Character Advancement & Balance

**Program ID:** CAB  
**Status:** IN PROGRESS — CAB-04 COMPLETED_VERIFIED; CAB-05 SELECTED_NOT_STARTED_PENDING_OWNER_ANSWERS  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner  
**Approved:** 2026-09-04

## Purpose

CAB reconciles Multiversal character advancement into one coherent XP-buy economy and establishes a durable multidimensional balance method for creation, progression, tiers, prerequisites, acquisition eligibility, ability costs, simultaneous-power controls, rewards and long-campaign play.

CAB is a game-rules/content-governance program. It does not grant Multiversal-app implementation authority and does not supersede the currently selected software work item.

## Owner decisions established

1. **Ability Points (AP) are deprecated.** AP no longer governs current character creation, advancement purchases, tier access or balance calculations.
2. **XP is the ordinary spendable Character-advancement currency.**
3. Legacy AP-bearing material remains recoverable provenance but is historical/deprecated mechanically unless a later owner decision explicitly restores a non-AP concept.
4. Five tiers remain the working model unless later CAB evidence justifies an explicit owner-approved change.
5. Tier is not automatically identical to XP price.
6. Character breadth is not inherently imbalance; effective simultaneous capability, stacking, action economy, resources, eligibility and challenge-bypass effects must be evaluated separately.
7. CAB must not introduce a universal Character Level, CR or one-dimensional power score.
8. **Ordinary transferable assets do not use Character XP.** Equipment, vehicles, drones, refits and upkeep use their owning credits/resources/economy. XP may be used only for permanent Character-bound progression or an explicitly exceptional advancement sacrifice.
9. **Starting money is governed by GM/Campaign creation policy.** Starting advancement XP and starting wealth/equipment are separate budgets.
10. **Ordinary percentage XP-gain multipliers are not part of the default progression economy.** CAB must nevertheless support faster learning for high Intelligence and/or Wisdom through a governed mechanic.
11. **Hazard/trap XP values are benchmark/reference evidence, not automatic universal payouts.** CAB-17 owns final award semantics.
12. **There is no ordinary XP-to-credits conversion at Character creation.**
13. **1,300 XP is the current normal-start default, not an immutable constant.** A GM/Campaign may choose another starting grant, and CAB may later recommend adjusting the normal-start default after calibration, benchmarks, pacing and simulation.
14. **Normal attribute creation uses one free 12-point allocation pool after base 10 and Species modifiers.** Further permanent increases use later calibrated XP rules.
15. **Regular/High/Hyper may survive only as optional alternate Campaign creation packages after full rebalance.** Their recovered point totals/templates/prices are invalid as written.
16. **There is no universal 1,800-XP background customization cap.** Campaign policy may impose one where appropriate.
17. **Creation-only permanent HP investment remains a valid XP-buy concept.** Base HP is `max(10, Constitution)` plus governed modifiers/grants; the recovered HP-buy price ladder is provisional pending CAB-06.

## Operating rules

- Source and repository evidence control. Do not silently promote legacy, inferred, synthetic or authored-proposal material to current rules.
- Keep current rule, legacy rule, conflict, unknown, recommendation and owner-approved decision distinct.
- Each tranche is bounded below the 25-minute tool cutoff. Overflow becomes an explicit follow-on rather than expanding the active tranche.
- Every tranche closeout records completed work, findings, recommendations, established decisions, unresolved items and the exact next tranche.
- When informed owner judgment would improve the next tranche, closeout includes a concise questionnaire with recommendations. Unanswered questions remain unresolved rather than being silently assumed.
- CAB may produce repair/repricing maps but does not silently rewrite thousands of canonical content records.
- Existing Character advancement transaction/history architecture remains authoritative unless CAB identifies and governs a conflict: stable IDs, authoritative validation, cost ledger, append-only history, before/after evidence, correction/respec receipts, permission boundaries and source/provenance remain preserved.
- PPIA-11 multidimensional balance principles remain governing input: no universal CR/power scalar, no guaranteed-balance claim, preserve action economy, resource depletion, environment, capability profiles, uncertainty and post-playtest calibration as separate dimensions.

## Dependency order

CAB is intentionally economy-first. CAB-04/05 establish advancement and tier semantics before CAB-06 calibrates cost. CAB-06..10 establish measurement and eligibility rules before CAB-11..13 audit the corpus. CAB-14..15 benchmark equal-XP builds only after those foundations. CAB-16 establishes intended advancement cadence before CAB-17 derives XP awards. CAB-18 stress-tests veteran play; CAB-19 governs correction/migration; CAB-20..23 integrate, simulate, repair-map and close the program.

## CAB-01 closeout

CAB-01 established the source/authority hierarchy, created the advancement source census and formally retired AP mechanics from current progression. Current Character advancement architecture remains usable without AP; legacy AP material remains provenance only.

## CAB-02 closeout

CAB-02 reconstructed XP into distinct governed/recovered families rather than treating every `XP` label as the same rule. Its owner decisions separate XP from ordinary assets/wealth, reject ordinary percentage XP multipliers, preserve hazard XP as reference evidence and require a later Intelligence/Wisdom faster-learning mechanic.

## CAB-03 closeout

CAB-03 established the working normal-start Character baseline: 1,300 XP subject to later evidence-based recalibration; separate GM/Campaign wealth; base 10 attributes plus Species modifiers plus one free 12-point allocation pool; five free starting Ability Trees; free Tier-1 access in those trees; five free eligible Tier-1 Abilities; no universal background cap; creation-only permanent HP investment retained with provisional prices; unspent XP carries forward.

## CAB-04 closeout

CAB-04 separates the advancement decision into distinct gates rather than letting XP or tier count perform every job.

The architecture now distinguishes:

1. authority;
2. source/rules availability;
3. Character eligibility;
4. explicit prerequisites;
5. tier/depth access;
6. learning/acquisition readiness;
7. XP affordability;
8. conflicts/exclusions;
9. required approval;
10. exactly-once commit and dependent-state integrity.

CAB-04 also defines:

- XP as affordability/permanent advancement commitment rather than eligibility;
- tier access as a distinct state whose final five-tier semantics belong to CAB-05;
- governed learning/development projects for advancements that require study, practice, instruction, exposure, attunement or similar development;
- the architectural home for Intelligence/Wisdom faster learning as learning-progress/readiness rather than a global XP-income multiplier;
- typed grants whose source/reason and bypass behavior are explicit;
- special acquisition as an eligibility/opportunity concept rather than merely a price;
- source-linked proposal, revalidation, approval and exactly-once commit with append-only history.

CAB-04's four unresolved policy choices are recorded in `CAB-04_OWNER_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`:

- whether any universal prior-tier quantity requirement exists;
- how broadly training/practice projects apply;
- confirmation of the Intelligence/Wisdom learning-speed attachment;
- default grant-bypass semantics.

## Current tranche

**CAB-05 — Five-Tier Model** is selected_not_started, but execution is held until the CAB-04 owner questionnaire is answered or a question is explicitly deferred.

CAB-05 must determine what each of the five tiers means, what tier access represents, whether tier access normally carries a separate cost/gate, how explicit prerequisites interact with tier depth, and how tier labels remain useful without becoming a universal power score.

## Boundaries

CAB does not:

- revive Ability Points;
- infer current rules from legacy AP tables;
- collapse balance into DPR or a universal scalar;
- assume equal XP must produce equal damage output;
- treat species/environment/innate abilities as ordinary shopping-list purchases merely because an XP cost exists;
- equate acquisition eligibility with purchase price;
- make wealth and permanent advancement freely interchangeable;
- use ordinary percentage XP multipliers as the default way to model learning speed;
- treat the 1,300-XP default as immune to later evidence-based calibration;
- use recovered Regular/High/Hyper numeric packages until rebuilt and accepted;
- silently resolve CAB-04 owner policy questions;
- grant application runtime, schema, migration, release, tester, provider or deployment authority.
