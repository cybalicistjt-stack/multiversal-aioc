# CAB — Character Advancement & Balance

**Program ID:** CAB  
**Status:** IN PROGRESS — CAB-06 COMPLETED_VERIFIED; CAB-07 SELECTED_NOT_STARTED_PENDING_OWNER_ANSWERS  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner  
**Approved:** 2026-09-04

## Purpose

CAB reconciles Multiversal Character advancement into one coherent XP-buy economy and establishes a durable multidimensional balance method for creation, progression, tiers, prerequisites, acquisition eligibility, Ability costs, simultaneous-power controls, rewards, and long-campaign play.

CAB is a game-rules/content-governance program. It does not grant Multiversal-app implementation authority and does not supersede the current software-development work item.

## Owner decisions established through CAB-05

1. **Ability Points (AP) are deprecated.**
2. **XP is the ordinary spendable Character-advancement currency.**
3. Five numeric tiers remain the shared developmental-depth structure.
4. Tier is not automatically XP price, Character Level, CR, damage, rarity, or universal power.
5. Character breadth is not inherently imbalance; effective simultaneous capability is evaluated separately.
6. Ordinary transferable assets/wealth use their owning economy, not Character XP.
7. There is no ordinary XP-to-credits conversion at creation.
8. Ordinary percentage XP-gain multipliers are rejected; Intelligence/Wisdom faster learning attaches to applicable learning progress/readiness.
9. Hazard/trap XP remains benchmark/reference evidence rather than automatic universal payout.
10. **1,300 XP is the current normal-start default and remains recalibratable.**
11. Normal creation Attributes begin at 10, apply Species modifiers, then one free 12-point pool.
12. Regular/High/Hyper may survive only as later rebalanced optional Campaign packages.
13. No universal 1,800-XP background cap.
14. Creation-only permanent HP investment remains valid in concept.
15. No universal prior-tier quantity ladder; explicit tree/path prerequisites and mastery requirements remain legal.
16. Learning projects are conditional; GM/Campaign may explicitly add, waive, shorten, extend, or modify learning requirements without silently changing unrelated gates/prices.
17. Free grants have controlled bypass semantics.
18. Shared tier descriptors are **Foundation / Developed / Advanced / Expert / Apex** while numeric Tier 1–5 remains authoritative.
19. Ordinary purchasable progressions normally use one-time T2–T5 XP access purchases plus individual Ability prices.
20. Special progression access modes remain legal when explicitly authored.
21. Short progressions may cap early; contiguous tiers from declared start through cap are expected; unexplained gaps are warnings; CAB never invents filler.
22. **Prestige classes/progressions may legitimately begin at Tier 3 with explicit entry requirements.** T1/T2 are not missing for that declared scope.
23. Tier 5 means Apex/capstone depth within the owning progression, not a universal world-shaping power floor.

## Operating rules

- Repository/source evidence controls; legacy, inferred, synthetic, recommendation, conflict, and owner-approved decisions remain distinct.
- Each tranche is bounded below the 25-minute tool cutoff; overflow becomes an explicit follow-on.
- A `Continue` order executes the whole active tranche through closeout, then selects the next tranche.
- Every closeout records completed work, findings, recommendations, decisions, unresolved items, questionnaire where useful, and exact successor.
- CAB may create repair/repricing maps but does not silently rewrite the Ability corpus during analysis.
- Existing Character advancement transaction/history architecture remains authoritative: stable IDs, authoritative validation, cost ledger, append-only history, before/after evidence, correction/respec receipts, permission boundaries, and source/provenance remain preserved.
- PPIA-11 multidimensional balance remains governing input: no universal CR/power scalar and no guaranteed-balance claim.

## CAB-06 closeout

CAB-06 established a cost-calibration framework rather than inheriting incompatible source tables.

Bounded evidence:

- 4,816 Ability records total;
- 1,712 numeric direct Ability costs;
- 1,338 source-expressed/numeric tier-unlock costs;
- 402 numeric upgrade costs;
- direct Ability median 700 XP, 90th percentile 5,000 XP, 99th percentile 20,000 XP;
- recurring full-tier direct ladders range from 50/75/150/200/300 to 3,000/7,500/15,000/25,000/40,000;
- dominant recovered tier-unlock schedule is 500/2,000/5,000/10,000/20,000 but is not universally used.

Structural CAB-06 results:

- progression-opening cost, tier-access cost, direct Ability effect cost, upgrade cost, grant/reference value, and actual XP debit are distinct concepts;
- direct Ability price follows actual effect burden rather than tier number alone;
- complexity, prerequisites, training, narrative rarity, and acquisition are not automatic price factors;
- source prices remain provenance through any future normalization;
- upgrades are priced by incremental burden;
- high-risk action economy, stacking, summons, immunity, transformations, and resource loops require CAB-07/08/13 review;
- Attribute/Skill/Knowledge/proficiency prices remain CAB-10 work.

CAB-06 recommends, but does not silently owner-approve:

1. a non-spendable design **Calibration Unit (CU) of 250 XP**, rescalable later with pacing;
2. tier-independent direct Ability burden anchors around **250 / 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 4,000 / 8,000+ XP**;
3. working access anchors: extra ordinary progression 1,000 XP; T2 500; T3 1,250; T4 2,500; T5 5,000;
4. Prestige T3 entry normally pays the T3 access anchor only after requirements, with no T1/T2 back-pay and no automatically stacked ordinary tree-opening charge;
5. increasing-marginal permanent HP working schedule: cumulative +5/+10/+15/+20 at 500 / 1,250 / 2,250 / 3,750 XP;
6. a non-spendable **Reference Advancement Value** so free/granted progression can retain mechanical audit value while final Character debit remains zero.

The five CAB-06 policy gates are recorded in `CAB-06_OWNER_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`. Unanswered items do not silently default.

## Boundaries

CAB does not revive AP; turn CU or Reference Advancement Value into spendable currencies; collapse balance into a single scalar; equate tier with raw power; treat Species/environment/innate abilities as ordinary shopping-list purchases merely because an XP cost exists; make wealth interchangeable with advancement; restore universal prior-tier counts; invent filler Abilities; charge Prestige for nonexistent T1/T2 tiers; or silently adopt CAB-06 recommendations without owner authority.
