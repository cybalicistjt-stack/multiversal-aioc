# CAB — Character Advancement & Balance

**Program ID:** CAB  
**Status:** IN PROGRESS — CAB-07 COMPLETED_VERIFIED; CAB-08 SELECTED_NOT_STARTED_PENDING_OWNER_ANSWERS  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner  
**Approved:** 2026-09-04

## Purpose

CAB reconciles Multiversal Character advancement into one coherent XP-buy economy and establishes a durable multidimensional balance method for creation, progression, tiers, prerequisites, acquisition eligibility, Ability costs, simultaneous-power controls, rewards, and long-campaign play.

CAB is a game-rules/content-governance program. It does not grant Multiversal-app implementation authority and does not supersede the current software-development work item.

## Owner decisions established through CAB-06

1. **Ability Points (AP) are deprecated.**
2. **XP is the ordinary spendable Character-advancement currency.**
3. Five numeric tiers remain the shared developmental-depth structure.
4. Tier is not automatically XP price, Character Level, CR, damage, rarity, or universal power.
5. Character breadth is not inherently imbalance; effective simultaneous capability is evaluated separately.
6. Ordinary transferable assets/wealth use their owning economy, not Character XP.
7. There is no ordinary XP-to-credits conversion at creation.
8. Ordinary percentage XP-gain multipliers are rejected; Intelligence/Wisdom faster learning attaches to applicable learning progress/readiness.
9. Hazard/trap XP remains benchmark/reference evidence rather than automatic universal payout.
10. **1,300 XP is the current normal-start default and remains recalibratable.**
11. Normal creation Attributes begin at 10, apply Species modifiers, then one free 12-point pool.
12. Regular/High/Hyper may survive only as later rebalanced optional Campaign packages.
13. No universal 1,800-XP background cap.
14. Creation-only permanent HP investment remains valid.
15. No universal prior-tier quantity ladder; explicit tree/path prerequisites and mastery requirements remain legal.
16. Learning projects are conditional; GM/Campaign may explicitly modify learning requirements without silently changing unrelated gates/prices.
17. Free grants have controlled bypass semantics.
18. Shared tier descriptors are **Foundation / Developed / Advanced / Expert / Apex** while numeric Tier 1–5 remains authoritative.
19. Ordinary purchasable progressions normally use one-time T2–T5 XP access purchases plus individual Ability prices.
20. Special progression access modes remain legal when explicitly authored.
21. Short progressions may cap early; contiguous tiers from declared start through cap are expected; unexplained gaps are warnings; CAB never invents filler.
22. **Prestige classes/progressions may legitimately begin at Tier 3 with explicit entry requirements.** T1/T2 are not missing for that declared scope.
23. Tier 5 means Apex/capstone depth within the owning progression, not a universal world-shaping power floor.
24. **1 CU = 250 XP** is the current non-spendable design/QA calibration unit; CAB-16 may later rescale its absolute XP value with pacing.
25. Direct Ability prices follow **actual effect burden**, not fixed tier or source-family prices. Working direct bands are approximately 250 / 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 / 4,000 / 8,000+ XP.
26. Working access anchors are: extra ordinary progression 1,000 XP; T2 500; T3 1,250; T4 2,500; T5 5,000.
27. Prestige T3 entry normally pays the T3 access anchor after requirements, with no T1/T2 back-pay and no automatically stacked ordinary tree-opening charge.
28. Working direct-HP cumulative prices are +5 = 500 XP; +10 = 1,250; +15 = 2,250; +20 = 3,750. Further blocks require later evidence.
29. Free/granted permanent progression may retain non-spendable **Reference Advancement Value** while final Character XP debit remains zero.

- PPIA-11 multidimensional balance remains governing input: no universal CR/power scalar and no guaranteed-balance claim.

## CAB-07 closeout

CAB-07 defined how acquired capability becomes **effective simultaneous power** through action delivery rather than by counting total known Abilities.

Bounded corpus findings:

- 4,816 Ability records total;
- only 418 have meaningful populated `Action_Economy` data;
- 476 meaningfully classify Passive/Active;
- 548 meaningfully state usage frequency;
- 337 meaningfully state resource cost;
- 829 meaningfully state duration;
- action-economy values among populated records include 107 Bonus Action, 103 Reaction, 80 source-defined Move, 69 Action, 56 Passive, 2 Free Action, and 1 No Action Required;
- source/text signals identify real extra-Action, extra-Reaction, action-compression, ally-action, and summon/minion mechanics requiring later high-risk review.

CAB-07 establishes structurally that:

- ownership breadth is distinct from simultaneous throughput;
- action economy is a first-class CAB-06 effect-burden dimension;
- Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, Free/No Action, Passive, and movement delivery remain distinguishable;
- action compression, additional attacks, full extra Actions, additional/refreshed Reactions, ally Action grants, and independent companion/minion actions are separate multiplier classes;
- serious balance review uses a non-scalar Simultaneous Power Profile rather than a single power number;
- missing timing information is `balance_unresolved_action_economy`, not presumed free;
- full extra Actions/Reactions and persistent autonomous action economies are manual-review classes;
- CAB-08 owns stacking/combination rules and CAB-13 owns high-risk record review.

CAB-07 recommends, but does not silently owner-approve:

1. a default combat envelope of 1 Action + 1 Bonus Action + movement on the Character's turn and 1 Reaction per round;
2. a default anti-recursion rule for Action/Reaction multiplication;
3. explicit meaningful limiters for consequential Free/No-Action effects;
4. companion/minion balance based on meaningful independent action economy rather than existence alone;
5. action compression/additional attacks as legitimate tools while unrestricted extra Actions, additional Reactions, and ally Action grants remain uncommon, explicit, limited, and appropriately priced.

These five policy gates are recorded in `CAB-07_OWNER_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`. Unanswered items do not silently default.

## Boundaries

CAB does not revive AP; turn CU or Reference Advancement Value into spendable currencies; collapse balance into a single scalar; equate tier with raw power; limit total known Abilities merely to control simultaneous power; invent readied-Ability slots; treat missing action timing as free; or silently adopt CAB-07 recommendations without owner authority.
