# CAB — Character Advancement & Balance

**Program ID:** CAB  
**Status:** IN PROGRESS — CAB-03 COMPLETED_VERIFIED; CAB-04 SELECTED_NOT_STARTED  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner  
**Approved:** 2026-09-04  

## Purpose

CAB reconciles Multiversal character advancement into one coherent XP-buy economy and establishes a durable multidimensional balance method for creation, progression, tiers, prerequisites, acquisition eligibility, ability costs, simultaneous-power controls, rewards and long-campaign play.

CAB is a game-rules/content-governance program. It does not grant Multiversal-app implementation authority and does not supersede the currently selected software work item.

## Owner decisions established

1. **Ability Points (AP) are deprecated.** AP no longer governs current character creation, advancement purchases, tier access, or balance calculations.
2. **XP is the ordinary spendable character-advancement currency.**
3. Legacy AP-bearing material remains recoverable source evidence but is historical/deprecated mechanically unless a later owner decision explicitly restores a specific non-AP concept.
4. Five tiers remain the working model unless later CAB evidence justifies an explicit owner-approved change.
5. Tier is not automatically identical to XP price.
6. Character breadth is not inherently imbalance; CAB must evaluate effective simultaneous capability, stacking, action economy, resources, eligibility and challenge-bypass effects separately.
7. CAB must not introduce a universal Character Level, CR, or one-dimensional power score.
8. **Ordinary transferable assets do not use Character XP.** Equipment, vehicles, drones, refits and upkeep use their owning credits/resources/economy. XP may be used only for permanent Character-bound progression or an explicitly exceptional advancement sacrifice.
9. **Starting money is governed by the GM/Campaign creation policy.** Starting advancement XP and starting wealth/equipment are separate budgets.
10. **Ordinary percentage XP-gain multipliers are not part of the default progression economy.** CAB must nevertheless support faster learning for high Intelligence and/or Wisdom through a later governed mechanic that does not silently become an unrestricted global XP multiplier.
11. **Existing hazard/trap XP values are benchmark/reference evidence, not automatic universal payouts.** CAB-17 owns final award semantics and must support meaningful non-defeat resolution.
12. **There is no ordinary XP-to-credits conversion at Character creation.** Advancement XP and starting wealth/equipment remain separate.
13. **1,300 XP is the current normal-start default, not an immutable constant.** A GM/Campaign may explicitly choose another starting grant, and CAB may later recommend adjusting the normal-start default after price calibration, benchmark Characters, pacing analysis and reference simulations.
14. **Normal attribute creation uses one free 12-point allocation pool after base 10 and Species modifiers.** Physical/Mental/Balanced may serve as examples. Additional attribute increases use later calibrated XP rules.
15. **Regular/High/Hyper may survive only as optional alternate Campaign creation packages after full rebalance.** Their recovered point totals/templates/prices are invalid as written and may not create bulk-buy arbitrage against ordinary attribute pricing.
16. **There is no universal 1,800-XP background customization cap.** Campaign policy may impose a category cap where appropriate.
17. **Creation-only permanent HP investment remains a valid XP-buy concept.** Base HP is `max(10, Constitution)` plus governed modifiers/grants; the recovered HP-buy price ladder is provisional pending CAB-06 calibration.

## Operating rules

- Source and repository evidence control. Do not silently promote legacy, inferred, synthetic or authored-proposal material to current rules.
- Keep current rule, legacy rule, conflict, unknown, recommendation and owner-approved decision distinct.
- Each tranche is bounded for execution below the 25-minute tool cutoff. If evidence exceeds the bounded unit, preserve the remainder as an explicit follow-on rather than expanding the tranche.
- Every tranche closeout records completed work, findings, recommendations, established decisions, unresolved items, and the exact next tranche.
- When informed owner judgment would improve the next tranche, closeout includes a concise questionnaire with recommendations. Unanswered questions remain unresolved rather than being silently assumed.
- CAB analysis may produce repair/repricing maps, but does not silently rewrite thousands of canonical content records.
- Existing Character advancement architecture remains authoritative for transaction/history semantics unless CAB explicitly identifies and governs a conflict: stable IDs, authoritative validation, cost ledger, append-only history, before/after evidence, correction/respec receipts, permission boundaries and source/provenance remain preserved.
- PPIA-11 multidimensional balance principles remain governing historical input: no universal CR/power scalar, no guaranteed-balance claim, preserve action economy, resource depletion, environment, capability profiles, uncertainty and post-playtest calibration as separate dimensions.

## Dependency order

CAB is intentionally economy-first. CAB-02 reconstructs the current XP economy before CAB-03 establishes a creation baseline. CAB-04/05 establish advancement and tier semantics before CAB-06 calibrates cost. CAB-06..10 establish the measurement rules before CAB-11..13 audit the corpus. CAB-14..15 benchmark equal-XP builds only after those foundations. CAB-16 establishes intended advancement cadence before CAB-17 derives XP awards. CAB-18 stress-tests veteran play; CAB-19 governs correction/migration; CAB-20..23 integrate, simulate, repair-map and close the program.

## CAB-02 closeout

CAB-02 reconstructed XP into distinct governed/recovered families rather than treating every `XP` label as the same rule. The CAB-02 owner questionnaire is fully resolved in `CAB-02_OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-09-04.md`: ordinary assets use their owning economies; starting money is GM/Campaign-defined; creation XP and wealth are separate with no ordinary conversion; hazard/trap XP values remain benchmarks; ordinary percentage XP multipliers are rejected; and faster learning for high Intelligence/Wisdom is a required later mechanic routed to CAB-04, CAB-10 and CAB-16/17.

## CAB-03 closeout

CAB-03 reconstructed the Character-creation baseline from current architecture, recovered owner-authored creation rules and specialized source PDFs.

Supported creation rules now include:

- 1,300 XP is the current normal-start default but is explicitly subject to later CAB recalibration;
- a GM/Campaign may explicitly choose another starting XP grant without changing price schedules;
- starting wealth/equipment is a separate GM/Campaign-defined budget;
- no XP-to-money conversion;
- base Species is free;
- Homeworld Type, Cultural Influence and Early Life Profession core benefits are free;
- normal attributes begin at 10, apply Species modifiers, then receive one free 12-point allocation pool;
- Regular/High/Hyper are only optional future package concepts pending full rebalance;
- five starting Ability Trees are free;
- Tier 1 access in those trees is free;
- five eligible Tier 1 Abilities are free;
- no universal 1,800-XP background category cap;
- base starting HP is `max(10, Constitution)` plus governed modifiers/grants;
- creation-only permanent HP investment remains valid in concept, with exact prices provisional;
- unspent creation XP may carry into play;
- Skills, Knowledges, proficiencies, additional Abilities and other permanent Character development remain valid XP-spend categories, while their conflicting legacy prices are deferred to owning CAB tranches.

The CAB-03 owner questionnaire is fully resolved in `CAB-03_OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-09-04.md`.

## Current tranche

**CAB-04 — Advancement Architecture** is selected_not_started and may execute.

CAB-04 must define the distinct jobs of XP, prerequisites, eligibility, training/practice, grants, advancement proposals, tier access, progression history and special acquisition without reviving AP or assuming the broken Attribute Mode economy. It must also determine where the required Intelligence/Wisdom faster-learning effect attaches without using an unrestricted global percentage XP multiplier.

## Boundaries

CAB does not:

- revive Ability Points;
- infer current rules from legacy AP tables;
- collapse balance into DPR or a universal scalar;
- assume equal XP must produce equal damage output;
- treat species/environment/innate abilities as ordinary shopping-list purchases merely because an XP cost exists;
- equate acquisition eligibility with purchase price;
- make wealth and permanent advancement freely interchangeable;
- use ordinary percentage XP multipliers as the default way to model learning speed;
- treat the 1,300-XP default as immune to later evidence-based calibration;
- use the recovered Regular/High/Hyper numeric packages until they are explicitly rebuilt and accepted;
- grant application runtime, schema, migration, release, tester, provider or deployment authority.
