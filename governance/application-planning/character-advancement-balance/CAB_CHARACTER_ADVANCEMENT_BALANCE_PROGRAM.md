# CAB — Character Advancement & Balance

**Program ID:** CAB  
**Status:** IN PROGRESS — CAB-05 COMPLETED_VERIFIED; CAB-06 SELECTED_NOT_STARTED  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner  
**Approved:** 2026-09-04

## Purpose

CAB reconciles Multiversal Character advancement into one coherent XP-buy economy and establishes a durable multidimensional balance method for creation, progression, tiers, prerequisites, acquisition eligibility, Ability costs, simultaneous-power controls, rewards, and long-campaign play.

CAB is a game-rules/content-governance program. It does not grant Multiversal-app implementation authority and does not supersede the current software-development work item.

## Owner decisions established

1. **Ability Points (AP) are deprecated.**
2. **XP is the ordinary spendable Character-advancement currency.**
3. Legacy AP-bearing material is provenance only unless a later owner decision restores a specific non-AP concept.
4. Multiversal uses **five numeric tiers** as the shared progression structure.
5. Tier is not automatically XP price, Character Level, CR, damage, rarity, or a universal power score.
6. Character breadth is not inherently imbalance; effective simultaneous capability, stacking, action economy, resources, eligibility, and challenge-bypass effects are evaluated separately.
7. Ordinary transferable assets do not use Character XP; starting wealth/equipment is a separate GM/Campaign-defined budget.
8. There is no ordinary XP-to-credits conversion at Character creation.
9. Ordinary percentage XP-gain multipliers are rejected.
10. High Intelligence/Wisdom must still provide meaningful faster learning through applicable learning progress/readiness; CAB-10 owns the exact formula.
11. Hazard/trap XP values remain benchmark/reference evidence, not automatic universal payouts.
12. **1,300 XP is the current normal-start default, not an immutable constant.** GM/Campaign may choose another starting grant, and CAB may later recalibrate the normal default.
13. Normal attributes begin at 10, apply Species modifiers, then receive one free 12-point creation pool. Further permanent increases use calibrated XP rules.
14. Regular/High/Hyper may survive only as optional alternate Campaign creation packages after full rebalance; recovered numeric versions are invalid as written.
15. There is no universal 1,800-XP background customization cap.
16. Creation-only permanent HP investment remains valid in concept; exact prices remain provisional pending CAB-06.
17. There is **no universal prior-tier quantity ladder**. Tree/path-specific prerequisites, counts, Skills, Knowledges, perks, milestones, Attributes, or other mastery requirements remain legal where explicitly authored.
18. Learning/development projects are conditional, not universal.
19. GM/Campaign policy may explicitly add, waive, shorten, extend, or modify a learning requirement; that does not silently change XP prices or unrelated gates.
20. Free grants waive XP cost where free and may establish source-backed eligibility; unrelated prerequisite/tier/learning/exclusion bypasses require explicit rule authority.
21. Shared tier descriptors are **Tier 1 Foundation, Tier 2 Developed, Tier 3 Advanced, Tier 4 Expert, Tier 5 Apex**; numeric tier remains authoritative and source-local labels remain provenance/display data.
22. For an ordinary purchasable tiered Ability progression, Tier 1 is established when the progression is legitimately opened and **Tiers 2–5 normally require a one-time XP access purchase** after non-cost conditions are satisfied. Individual Abilities retain separate XP prices. Special progressions may explicitly use another access mode.
23. Ordinary short/capped progressions may end before Tier 5; they should normally be contiguous from their declared start through cap, unexplained internal gaps are warnings, and CAB never invents filler Abilities.
24. **Prestige classes/progressions may legitimately begin at Tier 3 with explicit entry requirements.** Tiers 1–2 are not missing for that declared Prestige scope. Prestige should normally remain contiguous from Tier 3 through its declared cap unless explicitly authored otherwise.
25. Tier 5 means **Apex/capstone developmental depth within the owning progression**, not a universal legendary/world-shaping raw-power floor.

## Operating rules

- Repository/source evidence controls; legacy, inferred, synthetic, recommendation, conflict, and owner-approved decisions remain distinct.
- Each tranche is bounded below the 25-minute tool cutoff; overflow becomes an explicit follow-on.
- A `Continue` order executes the whole active tranche through closeout, then selects the next tranche.
- Every closeout records completed work, findings, recommendations, decisions, unresolved items, questionnaire where useful, and exact successor.
- CAB may create repair/repricing maps but does not silently rewrite the Ability corpus during analysis.
- Existing Character advancement transaction/history architecture remains authoritative: stable IDs, authoritative validation, cost ledger, append-only history, before/after evidence, correction/respec receipts, permission boundaries, and source/provenance remain preserved.
- PPIA-11 multidimensional balance remains governing input: no universal CR/power scalar and no guaranteed-balance claim.

## CAB-01 through CAB-04 closeout summary

CAB-01 retired AP and established authority/source hierarchy. CAB-02 reconstructed the fragmented XP economy and separated Character advancement XP from ordinary assets/wealth. CAB-03 established the working creation baseline: 1,300 XP subject to later recalibration, separate wealth, base-10 attributes plus Species modifiers plus one free 12-point pool, five free starting Ability Trees, free Tier-1 access in those trees, five free eligible Tier-1 Abilities, no universal background cap, creation-only HP investment, and unspent XP carry-forward. CAB-04 separated authority, availability, eligibility, prerequisites, tier/depth, learning/acquisition, XP affordability, exclusions, approval, and exactly-once commit, and established conditional learning with GM override plus controlled grant semantics.

## CAB-05 closeout

CAB-05 retained five tiers as a shared developmental-depth structure rather than a universal power or price scale.

Bounded evidence:

- 4,816 Ability records;
- 3,917 with numeric Tier 1–5;
- 179 tiered progression groups;
- 158 groups with all five tiers;
- 21 partial/sparse/alternate-start groups;
- direct Ability XP medians rise across tiers but observed ranges overlap substantially.

Owner-approved CAB-05 policy:

- numeric Tier 1–5 authoritative;
- Foundation / Developed / Advanced / Expert / Apex descriptors;
- ordinary purchasable progressions normally pay one-time T2–T5 access XP plus individual Ability prices;
- special progression access modes remain legal when explicitly authored;
- short progressions may cap early without filler and unexplained internal gaps are warnings;
- **Prestige progressions may declare Tier 3 as their legitimate start and require explicit entry prerequisites; Tiers 1–2 are not missing**;
- Tier 5 means Apex depth inside the owning progression, not a universal world-shaping floor.

Authority is recorded in `CAB-05_OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-09-04.md`.

## Current tranche

**CAB-06 — XP Cost Calibration Framework** is `selected_not_started` and may execute immediately.

CAB-06 must establish the governed method for calibrating:

- Ability direct XP prices;
- ordinary T2–T5 tier-access prices;
- extra progression/tree opening prices;
- creation-only HP purchases;
- other permanent Character-bound progression cost families where sufficient evidence exists;
- grants/waivers and special progression access interactions;
- cross-domain normalization without treating tier as an exact price table.

CAB-06 must distinguish developmental depth, actual effect burden, breadth, duration, action economy, resource cost, stacking/synergy exposure, reliability, frequency, scope, and special acquisition from price. It may set provisional anchor schedules/frameworks, but later CAB-07/08/10/11–15 evidence may require recalibration.

## Boundaries

CAB does not revive AP, collapse balance into a scalar, equate tier with raw power, treat Species/environment/innate abilities as ordinary shopping-list purchases merely because an XP cost exists, make wealth interchangeable with advancement, restore universal prior-tier counts, invent filler Abilities, or treat a legitimate Tier-3 Prestige start as missing lower tiers.
