# CAB — Character Advancement & Balance

**Program ID:** CAB  
**Status:** IN PROGRESS — CAB-05 COMPLETED_VERIFIED; CAB-06 SELECTED_NOT_STARTED_PENDING_OWNER_ANSWERS  
**Owner and final authority:** John Brandon Turner  
**Approved:** 2026-09-04

## Purpose

CAB reconciles Multiversal Character advancement into one coherent XP-buy economy and establishes a durable multidimensional balance method for creation, progression, tiers, prerequisites, acquisition eligibility, ability costs, simultaneous-power controls, rewards, and long-campaign play.

CAB is a game-rules/content-governance program. It does not grant Multiversal-app implementation authority and does not supersede the current software-development work item.

## Owner decisions established

1. **Ability Points (AP) are deprecated.** AP no longer governs Character creation, advancement purchases, tier access, or balance calculations.
2. **XP is the ordinary spendable Character-advancement currency.**
3. Legacy AP-bearing material remains provenance only unless a later owner decision explicitly restores a specific non-AP concept.
4. Five tiers remain the working model unless later CAB evidence justifies an explicit owner-approved change.
5. Tier is not automatically identical to XP price.
6. Character breadth is not inherently imbalance; simultaneous capability, stacking, action economy, resources, eligibility, and challenge-bypass effects must be evaluated separately.
7. CAB must not introduce a universal Character Level, CR, or one-dimensional power score.
8. **Ordinary transferable assets do not use Character XP.** Equipment, vehicles, drones, refits, and upkeep use their owning credits/resources/economy. XP is reserved for permanent Character-bound progression or an explicitly exceptional advancement sacrifice.
9. **Starting money is governed by GM/Campaign creation policy.** Starting advancement XP and starting wealth/equipment are separate budgets.
10. **Ordinary percentage XP-gain multipliers are rejected.** High Intelligence/Wisdom must nevertheless provide meaningful faster learning through a governed mechanic.
11. **Hazard/trap XP values are benchmark/reference evidence, not automatic universal payouts.** CAB-17 owns final award semantics.
12. **There is no ordinary XP-to-credits conversion at Character creation.**
13. **1,300 XP is the current normal-start default, not an immutable constant.** A GM/Campaign may choose another starting grant, and CAB may later recommend recalibration after pricing, benchmarks, pacing, and simulation.
14. **Normal attribute creation uses one free 12-point pool after base 10 and Species modifiers.** Further permanent increases use later calibrated XP rules.
15. **Regular/High/Hyper may survive only as optional alternate Campaign creation packages after full rebalance.** Their recovered numeric rules are invalid as written.
16. **There is no universal 1,800-XP background customization cap.** Campaign policy may impose one where appropriate.
17. **Creation-only permanent HP investment remains valid in concept.** Exact HP-buy prices remain provisional pending CAB-06.
18. **There is no universal prior-tier quantity gate.** Individual trees/paths may explicitly require prior nodes, counts, Skills, Knowledges, perks, milestones, attributes, or other mastery evidence. The old universal 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 ladder remains deprecated.
19. **Learning/development projects are conditional, not universal.** They apply where the owning advancement rule requires study, practice, exposure, attunement, instruction, research, or similar development.
20. **GM/Campaign policy may explicitly modify learning requirements.** It may add, waive, shorten, extend, or otherwise modify a learning requirement, but that override must be attributable and does not silently change XP prices or unrelated eligibility/prerequisite/exclusion gates.
21. **Intelligence/Wisdom faster learning attaches to applicable learning progress/readiness.** It does not automatically increase XP awards or reduce every XP purchase cost. CAB-10 owns the exact formula.
22. **Free grants have controlled bypass semantics.** A free grant waives XP cost; a source grant may establish eligibility where the source inherently does so; other prerequisite/tier/learning/exclusion bypasses require explicit rule authority.

## Operating rules

- Repository/source evidence controls. Do not silently promote legacy, inferred, synthetic, or proposal material to current rules.
- Keep current rule, legacy rule, conflict, unknown, recommendation, and owner-approved decision distinct.
- Every tranche is bounded below the 25-minute tool cutoff. Overflow becomes an explicit follow-on rather than expanding the active tranche.
- Each tranche closeout records completed work, findings, recommendations, established decisions, unresolved items, and the exact next tranche.
- When informed owner judgment would improve the next tranche, closeout includes a concise questionnaire with recommendations. Unanswered items remain unresolved rather than silently defaulting.
- CAB may produce repair/repricing maps but does not silently rewrite thousands of content records during analysis.
- Existing Character advancement transaction/history architecture remains authoritative unless CAB explicitly governs a conflict: stable IDs, authoritative validation, cost ledger, append-only history, before/after evidence, correction/respec receipts, permission boundaries, and source/provenance remain preserved.
- PPIA-11 multidimensional balance principles remain governing input: no universal CR/power scalar, no guaranteed-balance claim, and preserve action economy, resource depletion, environment, capability profiles, uncertainty, and post-playtest calibration as separate dimensions.

## CAB-01 closeout

CAB-01 established source/authority hierarchy, created the advancement source census, and formally retired AP mechanics. Current Character architecture does not require AP.

## CAB-02 closeout

CAB-02 reconstructed XP into distinct governed/recovered families. Owner decisions separated advancement XP from assets/wealth, rejected ordinary XP multipliers, retained hazard XP as benchmark evidence, and required a later Intelligence/Wisdom learning benefit.

## CAB-03 closeout

CAB-03 established the working normal-start baseline: 1,300 XP subject to later recalibration; separate GM/Campaign wealth; base 10 attributes + Species modifiers + one free 12-point pool; five free starting Ability Trees; free Tier-1 access in those trees; five free eligible Tier-1 Abilities; no universal background cap; creation-only permanent HP investment retained with provisional prices; unspent XP carries forward.

## CAB-04 closeout

CAB-04 finalized the advancement architecture as separate authority, availability, eligibility, prerequisite, tier/depth, learning/acquisition, XP affordability, conflict/exclusion, approval, and exactly-once commit gates.

CAB-04 owner decisions establish:

- no universal prior-tier quantity gate;
- conditional learning/development projects rather than universal training bookkeeping;
- explicit GM/Campaign authority to add, waive, shorten, extend, or modify learning requirements without silently altering XP price or unrelated gates;
- Intelligence/Wisdom learning acceleration on applicable learning progress/readiness rather than blanket XP-income or price effects;
- controlled grant bypass semantics: free cost and source-established eligibility by default, with unrelated bypasses explicit;
- special acquisition as eligibility/opportunity rather than merely price;
- preservation of source-linked proposals, authoritative revalidation, exactly-once commit, and append-only history.

The resolved authority is recorded in `CAB-04_OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-09-04.md`.

## CAB-05 closeout

CAB-05 analyzed the current five-tier structure against the portable Ability corpus and current game framework.

Bounded findings:

- 4,816 Ability records were in scope;
- 3,917 carry a numeric Tier 1–5 value;
- 179 tiered tree groups were observed;
- 158 groups contain all five tiers and 21 are partial/sparse;
- source-local tier names vary substantially and cannot supply one universal canonical wording;
- direct Ability XP medians rise across tiers, but observed cost ranges overlap enough that tier cannot be an exact price or power scalar.

CAB-05 establishes structurally that:

- numeric Tier 1–5 is the stable cross-tree tier identity;
- tier means relative developmental depth within an explicit progression scope;
- source-local labels remain provenance/display data;
- tier access remains distinct from node-specific prerequisites and costs;
- progression scope may be a tree or explicitly independent branch/path;
- unlocking one independent scope does not silently unlock another;
- free-product first-two-tier access remains entitlement, not game-balance semantics;
- missing Ability records are never synthetically invented to fill tiers;
- actual play power remains multidimensional rather than collapsed into tier.

CAB-05 recommends, but does not silently owner-approve:

1. shared semantic descriptors **Foundation / Developed / Advanced / Expert / Apex** while numeric tiers remain authoritative;
2. a normal one-time T2–T5 tier-access XP purchase for ordinary purchasable Ability progressions, while explicit special progression modes remain allowed;
3. intentional early tree caps with contiguous tiers through the cap, unexplained internal gaps treated as provenance/content warnings, and no synthetic filler;
4. Tier 5 as apex/capstone depth within the owning progression rather than a universal legendary/world-shaping power floor.

These four policy gates are recorded in `CAB-05_OWNER_QUESTIONNAIRE.md` and remain unresolved until the owner answers or explicitly defers them.

## Boundaries

CAB does not:

- revive Ability Points;
- infer current rules from legacy AP tables;
- collapse balance into DPR or a universal scalar;
- assume equal XP must produce equal damage output;
- treat Species/environment/innate abilities as ordinary shopping-list purchases merely because an XP cost exists;
- equate acquisition eligibility with purchase price;
- make wealth and permanent advancement freely interchangeable;
- use ordinary percentage XP multipliers as the default way to model learning speed;
- treat 1,300 XP as immune to later evidence-based calibration;
- use recovered Regular/High/Hyper numeric packages until explicitly rebuilt and accepted;
- reintroduce a universal prior-tier count;
- treat GM learning override as an automatic waiver of unrelated gates;
- treat tier as a universal power score or price table;
- silently resolve CAB-05 owner policy questions;
- grant application runtime, schema, migration, release, tester, provider, or deployment authority.
