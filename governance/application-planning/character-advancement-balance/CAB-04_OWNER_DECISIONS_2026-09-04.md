# CAB-04 — Owner Decisions

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Source:** Owner responses to `CAB-04_OWNER_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`  
**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Authority:** explicit owner decision

## CAB-Q04-01 — Universal prior-tier quantity requirement

**Decision:** Option A.

- There is **no universal prior-tier quantity gate**.
- A specific Ability Tree or advancement path may require explicit prior nodes, a defined number of prior abilities, Skills, Knowledges, perks, milestones, attributes, proficiencies, or other mastery evidence where its owning rule genuinely needs them.
- Explicit node/path prerequisites are preferred over filler-buying requirements.
- The deprecated universal 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 ladder does not govern current advancement.

**Forward effect:** CAB-05 must define tier semantics without assuming a global prior-tier count.

## CAB-Q04-02 — Training/practice requirement

**Decision:** Option A, with explicit GM/Campaign override authority.

### Default rule

- A learning/development project is required only when the owning Ability, Skill, Knowledge, archetype, acquisition rule, or other governed advancement rule requires study, practice, instruction, exposure, acclimation, attunement, research, or another developmental process.
- If no learning requirement applies, an advancement may proceed immediately once authority, availability, eligibility, prerequisites, tier/depth access, XP affordability, exclusions, and any required approval are satisfied.

### GM/Campaign override

- A GM/Campaign advancement policy may explicitly **add, waive, shorten, extend, or otherwise modify** a learning/training requirement for a specific Campaign, Character, advancement domain, or situation.
- An override must be attributable and visible in the advancement/learning record; it is not a silent hidden rule.
- A learning override changes the learning/readiness gate only. It does **not** silently alter XP prices, grant eligibility, waive unrelated prerequisites/exclusions, or rewrite advancement history unless the governing rule separately says so.

This preserves meaningful training where desired without imposing universal downtime bookkeeping on every permanent advancement.

## CAB-Q04-03 — Intelligence/Wisdom faster learning

**Decision:** Option A.

- High Intelligence and/or Wisdom improve the **rate or efficiency of applicable learning/development projects**.
- The effect may reduce required training time, increase governed learning progress, or otherwise accelerate readiness under the owning learning mode.
- Intelligence/Wisdom do **not** automatically increase XP awards.
- Intelligence/Wisdom do **not** automatically reduce every XP purchase price.
- The owning learning mode determines whether Intelligence, Wisdom, or a governed combination applies.
- Both attributes do not automatically stack merely because both are high.
- CAB-10 owns the exact attribute-to-learning formula; CAB-16/17/18 must test pacing and long-campaign divergence.

## CAB-Q04-04 — Free-grant bypass semantics

**Decision:** Option A.

- A grant waives XP cost when the grant states that the option is free.
- A grant satisfies eligibility when the grant source itself establishes that eligibility—for example, a Species granting its own innate feature.
- Prerequisites, tier/depth requirements, learning requirements, or exclusions are bypassed only when the granting rule explicitly establishes, substitutes for, or waives them.
- A generic free choice does not automatically bypass biology, compatibility, source availability, exclusions, or unrelated prerequisite gates.
- Temporary grants remain temporary and do not silently become permanently purchased advancement.

## CAB-05 handoff consequences

All four CAB-04 owner gates are resolved. CAB-05 — Five-Tier Model may execute.

CAB-05 must inherit:

1. no universal prior-tier quantity gate;
2. tree/path-specific prerequisites and mastery counts remain legal where explicitly authored;
3. learning projects are conditional by default, not universal;
4. GM/Campaign policy may explicitly modify learning requirements while leaving unrelated advancement gates and prices intact;
5. Intelligence/Wisdom faster learning attaches to applicable learning-project progress/readiness rather than blanket XP-income or XP-price effects;
6. grants waive only the gates their source explicitly governs, with free cost and source-established eligibility as the default controlled behavior;
7. all prior CAB owner decisions remain in force.
