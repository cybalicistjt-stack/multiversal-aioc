# CAB-08 — Owner Decisions

**Program:** CAB — Character Advancement & Balance  
**Source:** Owner response approving all CAB-08 recommendations  
**Date:** 2026-09-05  
**Authority:** explicit owner decision

## Decisions

### CAB-Q08-01 — Reapplying the same named mechanical effect

**Decision: A.** Reapplication does not increase magnitude unless explicitly stackable. Strongest applicable magnitude controls; valid duration-based reapplication may refresh duration. Explicit counters, charges, marks, and authored cumulative systems remain exceptions.

### CAB-Q08-02 — Different numerical modifiers affecting the same statistic

**Decision: A.** Mechanical interaction groups govern. Compatible different groups may combine. In an ordinary noncumulative group, the strongest beneficial modifier and strongest detrimental modifier apply while overlapping. Explicit cumulative systems may use additive or capped-additive rules.

### CAB-Q08-03 — Advantage and Disadvantage

**Decision: A.** Advantage and Disadvantage are binary noncompounding states. Multiple sources do not escalate. At least one applicable source of each cancels to a normal roll unless an explicit rule establishes priority or another mechanic.

### CAB-Q08-04 — Resistance, Immunity, and overlapping defenses

**Decision: A.** Same-type Resistance does not compound and multiple Resistances do not become Immunity. Immunity supersedes ordinary Resistance. Different damage/effect-type Resistances coexist. Flat reduction and other defense mechanics remain separate interaction groups. Duplicate Vulnerability does not compound unless explicitly authored.

### CAB-Q08-05 — Full transformations/forms

**Decision: A.** One full replacement form is active at a time by default. A new full form replaces/ends the prior one unless explicit nesting is authorized. Compatible narrow augmentations/overlays may coexist; native/passive traits remain unless explicitly replaced or suppressed.

### CAB-Q08-06 — Multiple multipliers affecting the same quantity

**Decision: A.** Multipliers in the same mechanical multiplier group do not sequentially multiply by default; the strongest applicable multiplier controls. Deliberate compound multiplication must explicitly define order and limits and receive manual high-risk review.

## Standing owner delegation for the remaining CAB series

1. the recommendation is evidence-grounded and falls within the already-approved CAB program scope;
2. one option is genuinely recommended rather than merely listed among unresolved alternatives;
3. it does not supersede or contradict an earlier explicit owner decision without calling that conflict out;
4. it does not create new application/software implementation authority, paid-service authority, release authority, or unrelated project scope;
5. it does not require an owner-specific creative/worldbuilding choice for which the evidence cannot determine a preferred answer;
6. it does not hide material uncertainty or fabricate missing source rules.

If a later CAB question fails those conditions, it remains an explicit owner gate. Otherwise, the recommended option is recorded as owner-approved under this standing delegation and CAB may continue without a questionnaire stop.

This delegation changes the CAB decision procedure only; it does not change game mechanics by itself.
