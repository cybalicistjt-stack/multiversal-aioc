# CAB-17 — Completion Report

CAB-17 turns CAB-16 pace into accomplishment-based award bands of 0/250/500/750/1,000 XP. Shared objectives default to equal awards for participating Characters; bounded personal milestones are allowed. Kills, hazards, training time and repetitive micro-actions do not automatically generate XP, and peaceful or creative resolution is equally rewardable.

The framework targets long-run campaign cadence rather than identical session payouts and preserves the rejection of ordinary percentage XP multipliers.

**Exact successor:** CAB-18 — Long-Campaign / High-XP Stress Test.
