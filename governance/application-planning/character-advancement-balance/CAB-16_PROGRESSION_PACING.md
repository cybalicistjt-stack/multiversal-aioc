# CAB-16 — Progression Pacing

## Purpose

Translate CAB prices into campaign advancement cadence without converting pacing targets into automatic payouts or kill XP.

## Campaign pacing profiles

CAB adopts four explicit Campaign profiles expressed in CU per substantive session **as long-run targets**:

- **Slow:** 1 CU ≈ 250 XP/session average;
- **Standard:** 2 CU ≈ 500 XP/session average;
- **Fast:** 3 CU ≈ 750 XP/session average;
- **Accelerated:** 4 CU ≈ 1,000 XP/session average, Campaign-explicit rather than default.

Standard is the normal reference profile for CAB simulation, not a mandate for every campaign.

## What standard pacing means

At 500 XP/session average, ignoring prerequisites and already-bankable XP:

- T2 access 500 XP ≈ 1 session;
- a 1,000-XP direct Ability ≈ 2 sessions;
- T3 access 1,250 ≈ 2.5 sessions;
- T4 access 2,500 ≈ 5 sessions;
- T5 access 5,000 ≈ 10 sessions;
- opening one extra ordinary progression and buying access through T5 (10,250 XP before Abilities) ≈ 20.5 sessions.

This supports meaningful advancement without making Apex access routine in a few sessions. Actual time is longer when prerequisites, learning, acquisition, and individual Ability costs apply.

## Starting XP

The current **1,300-XP normal start remains retained** for final simulation. It is intentionally enough for limited customization after the free creation package but not enough to bypass deeper progression. CAB-21 will provide the final deterministic sanity check; no evidence in CAB-06–15 requires changing it now.

## Learning time

Learning projects are a separate clock from XP earning. CAB-10's Intelligence/Wisdom multiplier changes project time/progress only. A Character can have enough XP and still be learning, or finish training and still be unable to afford the advancement.

Campaign downtime, instructors, facilities and exposure may alter project progress under owning rules; they do not change the Campaign's XP pace unless the award framework separately grants XP for accomplishment.

## Banking and burst spending

Unspent XP continues to carry forward. Players may bank XP for expensive advancement. A large purchase after several quiet sessions is not itself a pacing defect.

Tier unlocks and expensive purchases may therefore create intentional step changes. CAB-14/15 comparisons should inspect the before/after profile rather than smoothing those jumps into a fake continuous power curve.

## Catch-up

CAB does not impose a universal newcomer/catch-up multiplier. Campaigns may establish starting/entry grants for later-joining Characters, but those are explicit Campaign grants and do not permanently multiply XP earning.

## Adopted decisions

Under standing owner delegation:

- Standard reference pace = 500 XP/session long-run average;
- Slow/Fast/Accelerated profiles = 250/750/1,000 respectively;
- awards may vary around the target based on accomplishment;
- 1,300 starting XP remains the working normal default pending CAB-21 final simulation;
- learning-time and XP-earning clocks stay separate;
- banking is allowed and no global catch-up multiplier is introduced.

No delegation guardrail triggered.
