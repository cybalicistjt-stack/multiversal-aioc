# MSLR Benchmark Capability Matrix

**Program:** Multiversal Spatial Law Runtime (MSLR)  
**Purpose:** clean-room capability research only. Benchmark products are evidence for generalized interaction patterns; no protected source, assets, authored maps, level layouts, distinctive UI expression, formulas, music, save formats or private protocols may be copied.

## Existing Multiversal foundation

MSLR does not begin from zero. Completed SSA supplies irregular/non-Euclidean topology semantics including non-adjacent links, nested/stacked space and portal anchors. ENV supplies distinct Reality Instability, Dimensional Bleed, Portal Activity, Temporal Instability and Dream Influence contexts. MCS owns future map/interior/topology presentation. GPR owns generic gameplay execution/loop composition. MBES owns built environments. World/Reality/Scene/Transition/Portal/Visibility/Knowledge/Action/Event owners retain canonical truth.

The gap is player-facing **spatial-law gameplay**: learning, testing, mapping, exploiting, resisting, stabilizing and manipulating impossible spatial rules while preserving the above authority boundaries.

## Multiversal-specific source opportunities

Retained source-recovery material contains concepts such as Translimina liminal entry/exit, endless or looping halls, shifting labyrinths, Reality soft spots, memory drift/dissolution, unstable solid/ethereal surfaces, fragmented realities, anomalous gravity/time and recursive/inverted Chaos/Foam geometry. Where those records remain recovery candidates or pending validation, MSLR treats them as provenance/evidence only and does not silently promote their exact DCs, formulas or prose into canon.

The generalized capabilities they motivate are:

- shifting/looping topology with learnable escape laws;
- fuzzy borders whose influence is not equivalent to traversability;
- memory/knowledge-dependent navigation;
- spatial instability and anchor/counterplay mechanics;
- recursive/fragmented geometry;
- nonvisual or altered-sensory navigation;
- variable gravity/orientation;
- topology/metric distinctions;
- law discovery through evidence rather than repeated single-check resolution.

## Deduplication decisions

- **SSA owns semantic spatial topology and authoring primitives.** MSLR does not reopen SSA.
- **ENV owns environmental contexts and overlay composition.** MSLR does not turn an ENV overlay into hidden causation.
- **MCS owns cartographic/interior authoring and presentation.** MSLR supplies semantic requests/projections only.
- **GPR owns generic runtime, replay and loop composition.** MSLR is a spatial specialization over GPR.
- **MBES owns construction and built environments.** MSLR applies laws to accepted spaces rather than constructing them.
- **World/Reality/Scene/Portal/Transition/Visibility/Knowledge/Action/Event owners retain canonical state.**
- **MSWI owns downstream systemic propagation.** MSLR exposes typed spatial events/deltas and does not invent economy/ecology/faction consequences.
