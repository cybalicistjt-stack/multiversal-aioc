## Net-new clean-room capability cluster

The genuinely additive findings collapse into one semantic cluster:

**Perceptual / representational spatial reification and correspondence**

A Spatial Law Profile may explicitly authorize a perception, projection, representation or homologous instance to become an input to a governed spatial/object-state operation. Presentation alone never mutates truth.

The reusable operation families are:

1. **projection-conditioned transform** — an authored viewpoint/projection measurement may parameterize a size, pose, orientation or other permitted transform;
2. **representation-to-space materialization** — a bounded image/map/representation may instantiate spatial geometry/state under explicit clipping, overlap and provenance rules;
3. **projection-aligned correspondence** — authored features in different spaces/projections may create a temporary or persistent governed relation when an allowed alignment predicate is satisfied;
4. **recursive homologous-instance propagation** — multiple scale-linked manifestations may represent one owner-backed identity/state, with deterministic state propagation and explicit scale reindexing across recursion layers.

## Supplemental tranche plan

### MSLR-S01 — Perceptual Spatial Reification & Correspondence Runtime

Implement only the new MSLR-specific semantic adapters over existing owners.

Required contracts:

- `PerceptualSpatialOperation` with explicit actor, observer/view context, source representation or homologous identity, target owner scope, law/profile version, projection/frame transform, requested operation and provenance;
- no canonical mutation from raw screen coordinates, renderer output or client-only inference;
- projection-conditioned transform with owner-domain validation before commit;
- representation-to-space materialization with explicit source identity, placement transform, clipping/overlap policy, creation/replacement policy and compensation/reversal contract;
- projection-aligned correspondence predicates with permission-safe observer inputs and explicit connection lifetime;
- homologous recursive-instance binding with one authoritative owner identity/state, deterministic propagated pose/state fields and explicit cross-layer scale reindexing;
- stale view/snapshot/profile receipts fail before commit;
- every accepted change executes through existing Action/Event/GPR and owner-domain mutation authority;
- hidden/unauthorized geometry or representation content is filtered before predicates/materialization inputs are built;
- unsupported transforms or ambiguous correspondence remain unresolved rather than guessed.

Owner boundaries:

- MSLR owns the spatial-law interpretation and typed spatial outcome only;
- World/Scene/Location/Object/Asset/Character/Inventory owners retain canonical identities and mutations;
- GPR/Action/Event retain generic execution/replay;
- MCS/presentation owners retain rendering/projection;
- ARI/rights/provenance owners retain resource identity and allowed-use capabilities where external/user assets are involved.

Acceptance examples:

- changing camera projection without an explicit commit changes no canonical state;
- an authorized forced-perspective resize commits one attributable object-scale transformation while preserving object identity;
- one placed representation may instantiate governed traversable geometry without silently converting the source asset into World/Scene truth;
- destructive/overlap placement records exactly what was replaced/occluded and supports declared compensation/rewind;
- an alignment predicate can create a connection without leaking protected off-view/hidden topology;
- two recursive visual instances of one object cannot diverge when the profile declares homologous same-state propagation;
- carrying/rebinding across a recursion layer may change relative scale without creating a new canonical object.

### MSLR-S02 — Perceptual Spatial Supplement Golden Proof & Handoff

Prove the supplement without reopening MSLR-18.

Golden cases:

1. **forced-perspective commitment** — apparent scale is proposal/input only until explicit validated commit; accepted transform is replayable and owner-authorized;
2. **representation materialization** — a posed 2D/abstract representation creates spatial structure with deterministic pose, provenance, overlap policy and reversible history;
3. **view-conditioned correspondence** — alignment of two authorized projections creates one typed relation, and breaking the predicate removes/suspends it according to authored law;
4. **recursive homologous scale** — one object/state propagates across at least three scale-linked representations and retains stable identity while cross-layer reindexing changes relative usable scale;
5. **projection invariance** — changing renderer/projection alone never triggers any of the above operations;
6. **permission safety** — hidden source geometry, hidden correspondence targets and protected representation content do not leak through alignment/materialization diagnostics;
7. **accessibility equivalence** — progression-critical alignment/perception mechanics expose non-precision/non-visual semantic alternatives or equivalent authored interaction paths without disclosing protected answers;
8. **cross-system ownership** — Scene/Object/Asset owner writes occur only through their accepted authority while MSLR emits typed spatial outcomes;
9. **replay/recovery** — committed perceptual operations replay deterministically where declared and failed/stale operations leave no partial hidden mutation;
10. **provider-off/local operation** — blocking authoring/runtime proof has no paid/cloud dependency.

Completion of MSLR-S01/S02 may be recorded as a separate post-closure supplement receipt. It MUST NOT rewrite MSLR-18 to a nonterminal state or invalidate the original 45-vector certification.

## Why two tranches instead of one

The concepts share one semantic core, but one bounded tranche would combine new operation contracts, multiple owner adapters, permission/replay rules, four distinct mechanic families and the final cross-system proof. That is not credibly compatible with the project's bounded-tranche rule.

Two tranches keep the change minimal:

- S01 = implementation semantics and focused validation;
- S02 = integration/golden proof only.

No third tranche is justified by the reviewed games.
