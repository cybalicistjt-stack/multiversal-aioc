## Why this placement

MCCS supplies optional character/creature presentation. MNCS then constructs actual fictional TTRPG NPC/creature identities, populations, relationships, knowledge, life context, mechanics-on-demand, ecology and behavior. MAS can then assemble Adventures using real governed cast/creature objects instead of inventing an Adventure-local NPC generator.

MRCS remains later because it is a broad reusable rules/content-definition authoring surface. MNCS can consume existing owner definitions before MRCS and later consume MRCS-authored NPC/creature roles/templates/behavior packages without changing identity authority.

## Party-association reputation requirement

MNCS-08 must consume MIB-09 relationship/reputation authority to support Campaign-scoped association effects where one party member's attributed actions may affect how a specific faction/group regards another party member.

This is not a universal shared-party reputation score. Direct reputation and association-derived influence remain separately attributable, group-specific, Campaign-scoped and visibility-safe.

## Execution

MNCS contains 24 ordered units, each targeted at no more than 24 active minutes. If that target is not credible at governed start, the unit is split before execution.

## Authority preservation

MNCS creates generation/construction candidates and governed handoffs. It does not replace Character/NPC/Creature, MIB-09 Relationship/Reputation, Species/Form, World, DPL, Inventory, Encounter, Action/Event, MCCS, MAS or MRCS authority.
