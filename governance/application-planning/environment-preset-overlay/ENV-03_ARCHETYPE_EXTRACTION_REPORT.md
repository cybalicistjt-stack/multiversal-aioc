## Result

ENV-03 extracts **15 reusable environment archetypes** from the completed forty promoted environment profiles. All **40/40** profiles have a primary archetype; **12** use one or more secondary archetypes because multiple baseline environmental behaviors materially coexist.

Archetypes capture reusable **medium, topology, structural form, and traversal/survival behavior**. Existing profile names remain source/preset identities. No source profile is renamed, rewritten, deleted, or represented as though the new archetype terminology came from the original PDFs.

## Extracted archetypes

1. **ARCH-WETLAND — Wetland** — saturated terrestrial terrain, shallow water, soft ground, dense lowland vegetation, limited firm footing.
2. **ARCH-OPEN-WATER — Open Water Surface** — surface-water travel without reliable traversable ground; vessel/swim dependence, currents, waves, weather exposure.
3. **ARCH-SUBMERGED — Submerged** — fully underwater three-dimensional movement, breathing access, pressure/depth, visibility, buoyancy/current interaction.
4. **ARCH-COASTAL — Coastal Interface** — land-water transition, shore/landing access, changing waterline/tide sensitivity, nearshore obstacles.
5. **ARCH-FOREST — Forest** — tree-dominated terrain, canopy, concealment, route-finding, reduced sightlines, biological resources.
6. **ARCH-OPEN-COUNTRY — Open Country** — broad exposed terrestrial traversal with long sightlines, weather exposure, sparse shelter, substrate-dependent movement.
7. **ARCH-HIGHLAND — Highland** — steep/elevated terrain, slopes, cliffs, vertical traversal, passes, altitude exposure potential.
8. **ARCH-SUBTERRANEAN — Subterranean** — below-ground enclosed networks, darkness, constrained passages, orientation, ventilation and exit limitations.
9. **ARCH-BUILT-COMPLEX — Built Complex** — discrete constructed rooms/chambers/corridors/thresholds without assuming dense city fabric or active life-support.
10. **ARCH-URBAN — Urban** — dense permanent city fabric, streets/districts/buildings, verticality, crowds, services and many route choices.
11. **ARCH-SETTLEMENT — Settlement** — lower-density or temporary inhabited places with community-scale layout, local shelter/resources and strong edge interaction with surrounding terrain.
12. **ARCH-INFRASTRUCTURE — Industrial & Infrastructure** — engineered production, utility, logistics or transport spaces with machinery, service networks, technical access and structural/system hazards.
13. **ARCH-CONSTRUCTED-HABITAT — Constructed Habitat** — enclosed engineered environments whose habitability materially depends on controlled atmosphere/power/pressure/temperature/access systems.
14. **ARCH-SPACE-VOID — Space / Void** — open extraterrestrial space with vacuum, radiation, microgravity potential, enormous distances and sparse fixed shelter.
15. **ARCH-AERIAL — Atmospheric / Aerial** — traversal primarily within an atmosphere above stable ground, dominated by wind, altitude, pressure and aerial support.

## Important compound mappings

The compound-preset rule from ENV-01 is actively used rather than forcing mixed environments into a single misleading bucket:

- **Mangrove Swamp** = Wetland + Forest + Coastal Interface.
- **Sunken City** = Submerged + Urban + Built Complex.
- **Island Chain** = Coastal Interface + Open Water Surface.
- **Underwater Caves** = Submerged + Subterranean.
- **Arctic Tundra and Taiga** = Open Country + Forest; the existing combined source profile remains intact and ENV-09 owns any later split.
- **Post-Apocalyptic Overgrown City** = Urban + Forest; post-apocalyptic, overgrown and structural-decay states remain deferred.
- **Flooded Suburbs** = Settlement + Wetland; flooding/post-apocalyptic state remains deferred.
- **Underground Bunker Network** = Constructed Habitat + Subterranean + Industrial & Infrastructure.
- **Sewers and Forgotten Catacombs** = Subterranean + Industrial & Infrastructure + Built Complex.
- **Bustling Space Station** = Constructed Habitat + Urban.
- **Floating Megacity** = Urban + Constructed Habitat.
- **Port City** = Urban + Coastal Interface.

## State/condition dimensions deliberately not promoted to archetypes

The following remain preset parameters or candidates for later overlay treatment rather than being turned into duplicate archetypes:

- post-apocalyptic state;
- radiation, toxicity and contamination;
- flooded, overgrown, abandoned or derelict state;
- ash, eruption, storm and other hazard intensity;
- temperate/tropical/arctic climate;
- cyberpunk, slum, marketplace, bustling and similar social/style configuration;
- asteroid/debris density, nebular gas/dust, black-hole gravity and wormhole/temporal instability where the underlying baseline remains open space.

ENV-04 owns overlay taxonomy and stacking. ENV-03 only records these as deferred traits.

## Why old source families are not copied into the archetype layer

The original families remain provenance/grouping metadata but several are too broad or state-based to be reusable physical archetypes:

- **Post-Apocalyptic** spans Open Country, Urban, Settlement, Constructed Habitat, Subterranean and Infrastructure behavior.
- **Extreme / Natural** combines unrelated topology: mountains/volcanoes map to Highland, while Sandy Desert maps to Open Country.
- **Space** mixes open vacuum, engineered habitats, atmospheric flight, cities, debris fields and spatial anomalies.
- **Ruins / Subterranean** mixes below-ground topology, built complexes and infrastructure.
- **Aquatic** decomposes into Wetland, Open Water Surface, Submerged and Coastal Interface.

This is normalization, not deletion or rejection of source categories.
