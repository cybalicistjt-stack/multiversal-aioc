# ENV-08 — Grasslands, Open Country & Dry Landforms Expansion Report

**Status:** content candidate awaiting governed progression closeout  
**Application implementation authority:** none

## Result

ENV-08 adds eight owner-authorized modular presets:

1. Grassland / Prairie
2. Savanna
3. Steppe
4. Scrubland / Chaparral
5. Hills / Uplands
6. Canyon / Badlands
7. Rocky Desert
8. Salt Flats

The governed preset library therefore moves from 52 to **60 presets** once ENV-08 is closed.

## Archetype decision

No new archetype is justified. `ARCH-OPEN-COUNTRY` already owns broad exposed terrestrial traversal, distance, sparse shelter and substrate-sensitive footing. `ARCH-HIGHLAND` already owns steep/elevated/vertical relief and restricted passes. Hills, canyons and broken rocky desert compose those two where needed.

Creating Grassland, Savanna, Steppe, Desert, Badlands or Salt as archetypes would move climate, vegetation or substrate flavor into topology and recreate the duplication ENV was designed to remove. The composed archetype count therefore remains **17**.

## Preset versus overlay boundaries

- Grassland/Prairie does not imply Wildfire.
- Savanna does not imply Drought or Extreme Heat.
- Steppe does not imply Dust Storm or Extreme Cold.
- Scrubland/Chaparral has fire potential but does not begin under an active Wildfire overlay.
- Hills/Uplands do not imply Low Oxygen; altitude effects require actual elevation/local authority and later ENV-12 handling.
- Canyon/Badlands do not imply active Flash Flood, Landslide or Rockfall disasters.
- Rocky Desert is distinct from the existing Sandy Desert; it emphasizes rock/gravel/broken relief rather than dunes and loose sand.
- Salt Flats do not universally imply caustic chemistry, extreme heat, extreme cold or supernatural mirages.

Concrete Extreme Heat, Drought, Wildfire, Sandstorm/Dust Storm, extreme cold and related weather/physical conditions remain ENV-11/12. Magical or supernatural influence remains ENV-13.

## Ecology and creature boundary

The content uses terrain events, ecological roles and signs only. It does not declare canonical animal or monster identities, world distribution, mount suitability, pet/familiar eligibility, NPC capability or Havalaea intelligence/personhood. Those remain CEW and the pre-existing creature/relationship/NPC systems. Habitat Signature vocabulary remains ENV-15.

## Software boundary

ENV-08 does not mutate `Multiversal-app`, SCL terrain/zone/runtime behavior, migrations, encounter runtime, environment UI or any application schema. The parallel software track retains independent authority.
