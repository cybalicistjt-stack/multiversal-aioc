# ENV-13 — Magical, Supernatural & Multiversal Overlay Guide

**Program:** ENV — Environment Preset & Overlay  
**Tranche:** ENV-13 — Magical, Supernatural & Multiversal Overlays  
**Authority:** content/design/provenance only; no application implementation authority.

## Purpose

ENV-13 supplies reusable **environment-side context** for nonordinary conditions without turning those conditions into character rules, spell rules, creature rules, technology rules, portal endpoint identities, or World/Reality/Branch identities. Every overlay compiles through the ENV-04 typed-delta/effect-key resolver and keeps a contribution trace.

The library contains ten identities: Magical Saturation, Magical Dead Zone, Reality Instability, Dimensional Bleed, Portal Activity, Psychic Influence, Corruption, Temporal Instability, Chaos/Foam Influence, and Dream Influence.

## Resolver rule: interaction is not causation

An ENV-13 relation only changes resolution **when both conditions are already active in materially overlapping scopes**. It never generates the other condition.

- Reality Instability does not automatically create Dimensional Bleed.
- Reality Instability does not automatically create Temporal Instability.
- Dimensional Bleed does not automatically create Portal Activity.
- Portal Activity does not automatically create Dimensional Bleed.
- Dream Influence does not automatically create Psychic Influence.
- Psychic Influence does not automatically create Dream Influence.
- Chaos/Foam Influence does not automatically activate Reality Instability, and it does not automatically activate Temporal Instability, does not automatically activate Psychic Influence, and does not automatically activate Corruption.

This is deliberately the same anti-hidden-causation rule used by ENV-11 and ENV-12.

## Magical Saturation and Magical Dead Zone

These are opposite **ambient-magic environmental regimes**, not spell-resolution systems.

**Magical Saturation** means ambient magical influence is materially elevated relative to the local baseline. It may expose magic-aware environmental context, but it **does not grant spell bonuses**, additional slots, automatic enchantment, or free abilities.

**Magical Dead Zone** means ambient magical influence is materially suppressed, absent, inaccessible, or disconnected relative to the local baseline. It **does not universally disable magic**. A spell, power, item, creature, vehicle, or technology only changes behavior if its owning rules say that it depends on ambient magic.

The two regimes exclude one another only in the same materially uniform active scope. A larger environment may contain separate saturated and dead subzones.

## Reality Instability

Reality Instability describes unreliable local environmental consistency: geometry, causal cues, material behavior, sensory references, or comparable reality properties may fluctuate when local/source authority establishes those fluctuations.

ENV-13 does not invent a wild-magic table, random geometry table, universal save, damage value, or mutation mechanic. Reality Instability does not automatically create Dimensional Bleed; it can amplify an already-active Dimensional Bleed or Temporal Instability through an explicit interaction row.

## Dimensional Bleed

Dimensional Bleed means properties, signals, matter, influence, or phenomena from another reality/dimension overlap the local environment. The foreign reality and the properties that actually bleed through must come from World/Reality/local authority.

Dimensional Bleed does not automatically create Portal Activity. A bleed may be nontraversable, and an active portal may exist without broad environmental bleed.

## Portal Activity

Portal Activity means portals, gates, foldpoints, breaches, or comparable interfaces are environmentally significant in the scoped area. ENV owns only the environmental activity context. Portal identity, endpoint, authorization, reliability, traversal cost, route semantics, and destination truth remain externally owned.

Portal Activity never fabricates a destination, creature arrival, encounter, or dimensional bleed.

## Psychic Influence

Psychic Influence is ambient environmental psychic, emotional, memory, telepathic, or psionic pressure. Psychic Influence does not automatically impose a mental condition, alter memories, dominate a participant, deal psychic damage, or create a hallucination. Participant-owning systems evaluate any actual effects.

## Corruption

Corruption is a reusable supernatural environmental taint/transformative pressure distinct from ordinary chemical, biological, or radiological contamination. Corruption does not automatically mutate, possess, align, or morally redefine a participant. It also does not define a universal corruption meter, save, disease, addiction, or cleansing formula.

## Temporal Instability

Temporal Instability means local timeflow, ordering, synchronization, recurrence, or duration references are unreliable relative to the governing baseline. Temporal Instability does not adopt a universal time-conversion ratio. Any explicit ratio, loop, aging effect, cooldown adjustment, or travel consequence remains source-/World-/ability-/runtime-owned.

The Chaos source contains a particular time-conversion example and other specific temporal mechanics; ENV-13 preserves them as source evidence rather than promoting them as the general overlay rule.

## Chaos/Foam Influence

Chaos/Foam Influence is the one ENV-13 identity with a direct recovered environment-context seam. The retained source corpus contains a named **Chaos and Foam Environment Perk Tree** whose published perks are conditional on being in the Chaos or Foam, and `The Chaos v2.PDF` describes the Foam/Chaos as dead-reality remnants with inverted/recursive geometry, anomalous light/gravity/time, memory/echo phenomena, remnants and specialized travel/survival concerns.

ENV-13 therefore provides a stable `multiversal.chaos_foam_context` effect key that ENV-14 can later reconcile against exact source ability/adaptation relationships. It does **not** copy the perk tree into the overlay, does not grant any perk, and does not turn every phenomenon described in the Chaos source into an always-on condition.

## Dream Influence

Dream Influence means dream, oneiric, subconscious, symbolic, or sleep-adjacent logic materially affects the environment. Dream Influence does not automatically force sleep or make imagined content real. It also does not automatically create Psychic Influence, hallucinations, memory changes, or reality instability.

## Gehenna boundary

ENV-13 does not create a Gehenna-specific overlay. Retained recovery authority identifies **Gehenna as a Branch identity**. That is not sufficient evidence for a reusable environmental condition. Any Gehenna access/isolation, world distribution, branch topology, faction, technology, or lore rule remains with its owning World/Reality/Branch systems unless a later source/owner decision establishes a concrete composable environmental condition.
