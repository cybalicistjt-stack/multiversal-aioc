## Corrections

- **Morganthyr** is the current canonical species name. Legacy `Nekron` / `Nekrons` references are historical aliases only. `Morganth` may be used as the singular when needed.
- **Akwi** is the 26th core playable species. Its existing governed `AKWI-01` integration remains authoritative; supplemental file placement is a storage detail, not a non-core taxonomy.
- **Ratman** remains one player-facing core species. The recovered Ratman lineages/subspecies set is: Rattori, Nybra, Rattakar, Ratborn, Chitta, Taipanua, Ska, Muridian, and Raughtt.
- **Raughtt** currently has only three established facts: proper, aristocratic, and of Dominix. No additional biology, mechanics, history, or culture is authorized.
- The older Evernote species formerly named **Bougadahl** is now **The Jee**. It is outside the Core 26. The later beetle people retain the Bougadahl name.

## Historical evidence policy

PPIA-05, PPIA-06, and CAPP completed work is not reopened merely because its sealed evidence says 25 species or uses the then-current `Nekron` name. Those artifacts remain proof of what was validated at their exact historical heads.

Future **current-authority** species pickers, appearance/character production regeneration, migration, runtime catalogs, and validators must consume the Core 26 overlay.

## Development impact

The five species recovered in lore review—Ratman, Giantkin, Mythragara, Suula, and ManyToms—were already included in the historical 25-species game-development pipeline. They do **not** require their completed PPIA/CAPP work to be repeated.

The actual reconciliation work is:

1. protect the current 26-species roster;
2. resolve current Nekron/Nekrons display identity to Morganthyr;
3. preserve Akwi as a core playable species while reusing its existing governed integration;
4. protect the nine Ratman lineages under Ratman rather than promoting them to peer core species;
5. require successor/current regenerations to consume this authority instead of a hard-coded 25-species assumption.
