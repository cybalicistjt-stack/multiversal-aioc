## 1. Purpose

MCB builds the commercial equivalent of MIB: reusable market, brand, research, acquisition, analytics, content, partner, lifecycle, pricing and launch machinery before Multiversal needs to spend heavily or launch publicly.

Its purpose is to prevent the failure mode of finishing the product first and only then asking who it is for, how to explain it, how to acquire customers, what evidence supports its claims or how growth should work.

MCB is evidence-governed. It may prepare systems, research frameworks, brand assets, hypotheses and experiments before launch, but final claims, pricing, paid acquisition, app-store materials and launch campaigns must use current product/customer evidence rather than aspirational assumptions.

## 2. Relationship to MIB and SMB

- MIB remains the active implementation-backbone program until completed.
- SMB is the default post-MIB product-completion successor.
- MCB is an owner-approved commercial-preparation program. Evidence-independent preparation tranches may later be selected in bounded parallel with MIB/SMB through explicit owner routing.
- MCB does not become active merely because it exists in the roadmap.
- Product-dependent tranches must wait for the evidence they require.
- Paid acquisition, public launch, final commercial pricing, public community activation and binding feature claims remain separately gated.

## 3. Tranche plan

### MCB-01 — Market Definition, Category & Strategic Positioning
**Purpose:** define the market Multiversal intends to enter/shape, its beachhead, substitutes, differentiation, expansion path and defensibility thesis.

### MCB-02 — Customer Segment, Persona & Jobs-to-be-Done Registry
**Purpose:** create a structured registry of segments, jobs, pains, triggers, desired outcomes, objections, alternatives, willingness-to-pay hypotheses, retention drivers and acquisition paths.

### MCB-03 — Customer Research System
**Purpose:** build repeatable interview/survey/concept/pricing/churn research methods plus coding taxonomy, evidence scoring and a durable research repository.

### MCB-04 — Competitive Intelligence & Market Observatory
**Purpose:** maintain structured competitor/substitute tracking for audience, positioning, claims, pricing, channels, strengths, weaknesses, complaints, praise, releases and strategic changes.

### MCB-05 — Multiversal Brand Strategy
**Purpose:** define brand promise, personality, emotional territory, vocabulary, trust posture, humor/AI/family/creator language and continuity with the warm knowledgeable in-product voice.

### MCB-06 — Brand Identity System
**Purpose:** define the reusable visual brand system: logo family, typography, colors, imagery/illustration, iconography, screenshot/video treatment, social/press/presentation templates and co-branding rules.

### MCB-07 — Messaging Architecture
**Purpose:** create a canonical audience × problem × capability × benefit × emotional payoff × proof × CTA messaging matrix plus short/long explanations for major audiences/channels.

### MCB-08 — Claims, Proof & Evidence Registry
**Purpose:** ensure public claims have approved wording, current feature/test/customer evidence, limitations, proof strength, last verification and channel rules.

### MCB-09 — Product Demonstration & Media Capture System
**Purpose:** create deterministic demo states, seeded scenarios and repeatable screenshot/video capture workflows so marketing media can be regenerated from known product states.

### MCB-10 — Website & Landing-Page Architecture
**Purpose:** build reusable marketing-site structures for hero/problem/use-case/demo/comparison/trust/pricing/FAQ/testimonial/audience/signup pages before final launch copy is required.

### MCB-11 — Search, SEO & Evergreen Content Knowledge Graph
**Purpose:** map audience search intent to governed topics, content, product capabilities, related topics and calls to action for durable organic acquisition.

### MCB-12 — Content Production Engine
**Purpose:** create reusable workflows/templates and an asset registry for articles, tutorials, videos, shorts, newsletters, guides, social posts and content repurposing.

### MCB-13 — Acquisition Channel Strategy & Experiment Library
**Purpose:** define channel hypotheses, audiences, messages, creatives, landing pages, conversion events, success thresholds and kill thresholds before significant spend.

### MCB-14 — Creator, GM, Influencer & Partner Network
**Purpose:** build a relationship/partner registry for actual-play creators, reviewers, GM educators, RPG creators/publishers, convention organizers, accessibility advocates and family-gaming voices.

### MCB-15 — Community Architecture
**Purpose:** design official community spaces, moderation boundaries, creator/GM spaces, support/social separation, spoilers, ownership, family-safety implications and recognition/challenge systems before opening them broadly.

### MCB-16 — Waitlist, Alpha Recruitment & Audience Seeding
**Purpose:** prepare segmented signup/waitlist/recruitment systems that collect useful consented market/testing signals and can later support remote alpha/beta cohorts.

### MCB-17 — CRM, Lifecycle & Customer Communication Model
**Purpose:** define visitor→interest→waitlist→invite→activation→Campaign→retention→advocate states and the onboarding, activation, support, reactivation and milestone communications for each.

### MCB-18 — Analytics, Attribution & Experiment Registry
**Purpose:** define canonical growth metrics, event naming, attribution, UTM conventions, funnel measurement and an evidence-backed experiment registry before acquisition scale.

### MCB-19 — Pricing, Packaging & Value Architecture
**Purpose:** research/structure Personal/Player/GM/creator/family/AI/storage/campaign/collaboration value boundaries without prematurely locking final prices or arbitrary feature withholding.

### MCB-20 — Acquisition Economics & Market-Capture Model
**Purpose:** model CAC, organic/paid mix, activation, conversion, churn, retention, ARPU, referral, creator acquisition and especially GM→Player invitation economics.

### MCB-21 — Referral, Virality & Growth-Loop Design
**Purpose:** design value-first growth loops around Campaign invitations, GM conversion, creator sharing/remixing and other natural product behaviors without spam mechanics.

### MCB-22 — Launch Narrative, PR & Press Kit
**Purpose:** prepare launch/founder narrative, press FAQ, product fact sheet, media kit, review guide, demo materials and announcement templates, with final claims gated by current evidence.

### MCB-23 — Trust, Privacy, AI & Family Communication
**Purpose:** make privacy, ownership, AI boundaries, Campaign privacy, creator rights, parental controls, data handling and user control understandable and part of Multiversal's trust advantage.

### MCB-24 — Launch Simulation & Commercial Readiness
**Purpose:** run synthetic/fake-launch cohorts through advertisement/landing/signup/onboarding/Campaign/support/retention paths to stress-test messaging, analytics, support, pricing hypotheses, FAQ and failure handling before real scale.

### MCB-25 — Market-Capture & Expansion Playbook
**Purpose:** turn validated research/metrics into an explicit beachhead→adjacency expansion strategy across GMs, Players, remote/asynchronous groups, creators/worldbuilders, writers, solo/AutoGM users, families, publishers and any later validated segments.

## 7. Evidence and ethics rules

- Do not fabricate customer evidence, testimonials, market size, performance or product capabilities.
- Hypotheses must be labeled as hypotheses until validated.
- Visibility/private product data must not be used for marketing evidence without authorization.
- Do not use deceptive scarcity, dark patterns, spam loops or misleading AI claims.
- Final pricing/claims/paid acquisition require current product and customer evidence plus owner routing.
- Brand and marketing materials must preserve Multiversal's warm, creative, trustworthy, non-obsequious identity.

MCB completes when its market/brand/acquisition/growth/launch systems are evidence-backed, reusable and ready to support a real commercial rollout rather than a one-time promotional burst.
