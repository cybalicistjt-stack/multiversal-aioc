# Melee Weapons Source-Enrichment Pilot

This pilot converts owner-supplied `Melee Weapons.PDF` evidence into reviewable game-object drafts without overwriting the certified 487-object collection.

It contains 11 shared weapon-property definitions, one generic Weapon Attack action, and 10 representative enriched weapon items including Spear, Retractable Sleeve Blade, Aeon Sword, and Power Fist. The next batch extends the same governed mapping to every remaining clearly parseable weapon entry.

The source remains authoritative. Ambiguous or malformed source text is preserved in the exceptions queue rather than silently corrected.
